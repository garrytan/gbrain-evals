## The Lumopact Deal — Notable Outcomes
- **Board seat traded for pro-rata rights** — Tomas gave up his board seat entirely (no observer seat either) in exchange for stronger pro-rata rights. Rationale: at seed stage, a single board seat has limited governance power; pro-rata rights protect ownership through future rounds when valuations step up. Tomas is relying on his existing relationship with Anouk for informal access.
- **Option pool negotiated down** — Anouk successfully argued the pool from 15% to 10% pre-money. Tomas conceded cleanly, likely because he'd already secured the pro-rata. This was a meaningful win; pool size directly affects founder dilution.
