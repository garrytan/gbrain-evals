# Prep Notes: Anouk / Ostrafell Pilot Negotiation

## Context
- Anouk is leading the pilot negotiation with Ostrafell
- Her manager is coaching from the sidelines and finds it more energizing than running his own deals
- A role-play rehearsal session was partially completed the day before the call (cut short by a video freeze)

---

## Pilot Scope
- **Duration:** 6 months
- **Scale:** 40 stores
- **Structure:** Fixed fee (not per-store pricing at this stage)
- **Price anchor:** $90,000 (~$375/store/month)

> Rationale: Free pilots die in procurement. A paid pilot creates internal ownership and establishes budget precedent for full rollout conversion.

---

## Success Metrics
- **Efficiency gains** (route optimization, load consolidation)
- **Sustainability KPIs:** 10% estimated emissions reduction per store
- Quarterly sustainability report included as a bonus deliverable — low cost to produce, high perceived value for Ostrafell's brand and sustainability teams

---

## Exec Sponsor Strategy
- Ostrafell procurement will not move without a named exec sponsor
- **Suspected sponsor:** Fleet manager (identified via LinkedIn — directly upstream of both efficiency and emissions targets)
- Surface the sponsor requirement early without sounding pushy; suggested framing: *"Who on your leadership team would we loop in for quarterly reviews?"*
- Watch for who asks forward-looking questions on the call (expansion, board reporting) — that person is already invested beyond the pilot
- If procurement goes quiet post-agreement, ping the sponsor directly rather than chasing the procurement contact

---

## Procurement Expectations
- Ostrafell's process is thorough and slow — expect **4–6 week cycle** after verbal agreement
- Anticipate multiple security questionnaires and compliance reviews
- Keep momentum by locking in a concrete next step on every call (technical overview, draft SOW, etc.)

---

## Call Logistics
- Anouk runs the room **solo** to establish her own credibility
- Manager attends but in **backchannel-only** mode via Slack DM
- Backchannel protocol to confirm: dedicated Slack channel vs. DM (open question at end of session)
- One more role-play rehearsal recommended before the call, focused on tough procurement objections

---

## Key Talking Points for Anouk
1. Anchor at $90k and frame the paid structure as a partnership signal
2. Surface the exec sponsor question early using the logistics framing
3. Lead with the 10% emissions target — it writes their annual report story for them
4. Mention the quarterly sustainability report as an included deliverable (sweetens the deal without discounting)
5. Close every interaction with a named next step to prevent procurement stall
