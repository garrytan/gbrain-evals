# Mira Voss Call Debrief — Driftless Pricing & Early Strategy

## Pricing Decision
- **Model: per connected calendar, not per seat**
  - Seats create incentives to minimize access; calendars map directly to value delivered
  - Seats may be revisited for larger orgs later, but calendars are right for early stage
- **Pilot price: $40/calendar/month**
  - Low enough for a team lead to expense without procurement
  - High enough to signal the tool is serious
  - Example: 10-person team = $400/month
