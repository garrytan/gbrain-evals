# Meal Planning Session – Weekly Dinners from Pantry Staples

## Fridge/Freezer Inventory at Time of Session
- Half a head of cabbage, carrots, block of cheddar, eggs, assorted condiments
- Leftover rice (2 days old)
- Frozen ground turkey
- Whole frozen chicken (had been in freezer a couple of weeks)

## Weekly Meal Plan Framework
Goal: stretch one roast chicken across **5 dinners** as a personal challenge.

| Night | Meal |
|-------|------|
| 1 | Roast chicken (main event) |
| 2 | Chicken fried rice (leftover rice + cabbage + carrots) |
| 3 | Chicken tacos (tortillas + salsa + sour cream) |
| 4 | Chicken salad or sandwiches |
| 5 | Stuffed cabbage or stir fry with ground turkey |
| Backup | Simple pasta (penne) for a lazy night |

**Bonus:** Use carcass to make homemade broth if motivated.

## Shopping List

### Farmers Market (Saturday, opens 8am)
- Fresh rosemary
- Fresh thyme
- Cilantro
- Green onions
- Salad greens
- Tomatoes

### Grocery Store
- **Produce:** Onions, garlic, lemons, limes
- **Dairy:** Butter, sour cream
- **Pantry:** Olive oil, chicken broth, penne pasta
- **Mexican aisle:** Tortillas, salsa

## Notes & Preferences
- User cooks economically and likes to minimize waste
- Prefers farmers market for fresh herbs and produce (better quality)
- Onions and garlic are staples used in nearly everything — keep stocked
- Olive oil was critically low at time of session
