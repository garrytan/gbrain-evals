## Reusable Red Flag Checklist (B2B SaaS Diligence)

| Category | Flag Threshold |
|---|---|
| Customer concentration | Any top-2 logo > 25% of ARR |
| Contract terms | Largest contract has < 60-day termination window |
| Churn pattern | Logo churn > 15% annually; any single reason > 50% of events |
| Revenue quality | Recurring < 85% of total; gross margin < 60% |
| Expansion behavior | NRR < 95%; top logos contracting |

*Lumopact triggers the concentration and contract terms flags. Both are priced into the conditions above.*
