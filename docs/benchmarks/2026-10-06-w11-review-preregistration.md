## Question

Two published results rest on labels only an agent has checked: the Cat35 coverage judge (its 88.1% headline carries a "pending human calibration" banner) and the 38 reference events behind the 2026-10-04 `auto_chronicle` default-on verdict. A person checks them. Evidence class: label validation.

## Arms and materials

- **Chronicle** (about 15 minutes, the requested sitting): the 38 labeled events on their 28 pages of amara-life-v1, one card per page with the whole page, the label rules, and calendar times as raw `DTSTART`, UTC day and Pacific time. Per event: supported as written, has an error (date, description or person, not an event, keywords; a corrected day is required for a date error), or can't tell. Per page: optional missed anchor events with day, description and keywords all entered by the reviewer.
- **Cat35** (optional, 30-minute timebox): the 24 coverage pairs of `judge-calibration-2026-08-25.json`, rated on the judge's scale (fully, partly, not conveyed, can't tell) from the statement and the note the judge saw, blind to the judge's verdict, lane and grouping. The 21 rows with a non-empty note come first in a seeded random order (seed 20261006); the 3 empty-note rows come last. The transcript paragraph appears only after a rating, for a separate "context changes my answer" flag.

## Metrics, denominators and decision rules

**Cat35.** Headline population: answered rows with a non-empty note, excluding "can't tell". Agreement and linearly weighted kappa (`weightedKappa`, `eval/runner/cat35-checks.ts`) between the committed judge verdicts and the reviewer's ratings, with a 95% bootstrap interval resampling transcripts (5,000 draws, seed 20261006). Also reported: the same over all answered rows, the 3x3 judge-by-human table, the "can't tell" count and the context-flag count. Never computed with `--judge-calibration`, which reruns the judge.

| Outcome | Rule | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| pending | fewer than 12 eligible ratings | nothing published; banner stays "pending" |
| undefined | kappa undefined | reported as undefined; banner stays |
| imprecise | interval wider than 0.5 | headline stays, with agreement and interval in its first paragraph |
| calibrated | lower bound >= 0.4 | banner becomes the calibrated statement (coverage only) |
| not_calibrated | upper bound < 0.4 | the 88.1% leaves README's current results until the judge is fixed |
| moderate | otherwise | headline stays, with agreement and interval in its first paragraph |

**Chronicle.** Revised reference set: a corrected label replaces the original, "not an event" removes it, an added missed event joins it, and "can't tell" keeps the agent's label (counted and reported as a bound). Both committed ON runs of the 2026-10-04 rerun (`events-on-a.json`, `events-on-b.json`) are rescored with `matchPage`; the recall rule becomes matched >= ceil(34/38 x revised size). If either run then fails the recall rule, or a corrected label turns a counted match into a wrong event that pushes the rate above 0.20 per judged page, the report says default-on is no longer supported on human-checked labels and a gbrain issue is filed. Corrections that leave every rule passing are published by id with the verdict unchanged. A partial review publishes partial counts, by id, with the rest marked "not reviewed by a person".
