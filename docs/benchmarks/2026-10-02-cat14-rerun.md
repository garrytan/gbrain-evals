# Cat 14 rerun: track-record advice with a blind judge

**Finding.** On October 2, 2026, at gbrain `d44296c` (v0.60.30.0), Cat 14's current blind runner scored all 8 of 8 probes and the run's gate failed. The judge preferred the advice written with a track-record profile in 5 of the 6 probes where a winner is expected (the sixth was a tie), and the wiring checks passed: gbrain put the calibration block in the prompt exactly when a profile existed. Two behavior axes missed their targets. The calibrated answer offered a counter-argument to the user's usual pattern in 2 of 4 positive probes (target 80%), and its conversational-voice score averaged 31% over the 8 probes (target 95%). This replaces nothing: the May 75% stays retracted, and this is the first valid measurement.

## What Cat 14 tests

[gbrain](https://github.com/garrytan/gbrain) can keep a "calibration profile": a summary of how a person's past predictions turned out, for example a habit of dismissing companies outside coastal cities that later succeeded. `think` can add that profile to its prompt (`withCalibration: true`). Cat 14 asks whether the advice gets better when it does.

Each of 8 hand-written probes seeds a fresh in-memory brain with evidence pages and, when the probe has one, a profile. It runs gbrain's production `runThink` twice, without and with calibration. `claude-sonnet-4-6` at temperature 0 writes both answers. A `claude-haiku-4-5` judge at temperature 0 compares them in both orders without seeing the probe's notes or expected behavior (judge prompt `cat14-ab-2026-09-28-untrusted-v1`). Retrieval quality is not under test: embeddings are deterministic hashes.

## What this means

The profile reaches the prompt and the judge prefers the advice that uses it, which is the part of the May claim this fixture can test. The style the fixture asks for, a conversational answer that pushes back on the user's habit, did not come through reliably with this model at temperature 0. The probes are 8 hand-written cases with one judge model, so treat the rates as a description of this fixture, not a general rate. A judge from another model family and a human-labelled check would be the next steps.
