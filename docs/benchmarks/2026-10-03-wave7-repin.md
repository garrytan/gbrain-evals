# The categories after gbrain fix wave 7 (2026-10-03)

## The finding

gbrain fix wave 7 does what it says on every ledger entry it targets. A rerun here at gbrain master `48ed5e8` (v0.60.32.0) shows each of the eight changes it made for this repository's [bug ledger](2026-10-01-wave-bugs.md), and all 20 bugs fixed in waves 5 and 6 stay fixed. Every gating rule passes at the new pin. One rule needed an amendment first, because gbrain changed the documented behavior it encoded, and one paid decision rule now fails by one pair.

[gbrain](https://github.com/garrytan/gbrain) is a memory system for agents. It stores notes as Markdown and builds a database index for search and relationships. Fix wave 7 is [garrytan/gbrain#5908](https://github.com/garrytan/gbrain/pull/5908), merged as `48ed5e8233f617479df989998560840747af0425`. The comparison point is the [October 2 re-pin](2026-10-02-wave-repin.md) at `d44296c` (v0.60.30.0), rerun here with the same runners, seeds and settings. The plan, including one budget change, was [preregistered](2026-10-03-wave7-repin-preregistration.md) before any run.

What changed for a user:

- **Contradictions (N2).** With judge prompt version 4, gbrain's judge called 149 of 150 planted same-time conflicts contradictions, up from 132. All 50 conflicts between undated notes are now caught (was 36). The cost is more false alarms: 6 of 51 compatible pairs (was 3 of 50) and 38 of 1,977 unrelated pairs (was 26 of 1,968). The compatible-pair rate, 11.8%, is above the preregistered 10% limit, so this report may not say the judge "separates same-time conflicts from dated changes" on this world. It did on October 2.
- **Open loops (N7).** A reply that only says "Thanks!" to someone's question no longer closes their loop: 0 of 4 such store rounds closed (was 4 of 4). An FYI whose only question mark sits inside a link opens no loop (0 of 4, was 4 of 4). A loop's age now starts at the request, so a 30-day-old request found on first sync ranks 690 hours older than a 30-hour one (it ranked the same). `open_loops` takes `as_of`, so a published ranking can be reproduced.
- **Relationships (N9).** "Who works at Acme?" now finds the company page titled "Acme" when "Acme Labs" also exists. Template one-hop questions with an unresolved seed fell from 45 to 6 of 435 runs, the relationship arm fired on 213 instead of 174, and first-place hits with the arm on rose from 223 to 238 of 435 (keyword path).
- **Meeting attendance (N12).** People listed on a `Participants:` line are now typed as attendees: 5 of 5 on each seed (was 0 of 5), with no false attendance.
- **Code intelligence (N13).** A call such as `segments.join("/")` no longer resolves to a same-file function named `join`. Resolved caller edges went from 76 to 72 of 91, and all 42 compiler-checked same-file calls stay resolved.
- **Answer grading (A4).** gbrain's retrieval grade no longer calls every question `moderate`. All 120 unanswerable questions now grade `weak` (were `moderate`), but so do 80 of 120 answerable ones.

Measured on 2026-10-02 and 2026-10-03 (UTC) at gbrain `48ed5e8`, through a copied overlay, on Bun 1.4.2, with provider keys stripped except where a paid arm names one, and System One off. Paid spend was $15.68 against a $30 cap.

## What one of these changes looks like

N7 checks `gbrain waiting`, which lists who is waiting on you in Gmail. Take a thread where a colleague asks "Can you send the signed form?" and you reply "Thanks!". The colleague is still waiting for the form. Until v0.60.32.0, gbrain's documented rule was that any reply closes the loop, so the form request disappeared from the list. We recorded that as feature gap N7-2.

gbrain's [open-loops guide](https://github.com/garrytan/gbrain/blob/48ed5e8233f617479df989998560840747af0425/docs/guides/open-loops.md) now says: "A reply of yours that only acknowledges their question ("Thanks!", "Got it, thanks.") is not an answer: the loop stays open and its clock keeps running." In the rerun all eight such threads stay open as `unanswered_inbound`, and a reply with content ("Here it is") still closes its loop.

That improvement broke a gate here, which is the subject of the next section.

## The experiment and results

**What was rerun.** Each category that owns a wave 7 entry: N2 (hermetic and paid), N7, N9 (hermetic, paid, and the one-hop `relational-ab` paid rerun), N12 (seeds 12 and 7), N13 and A4 (hermetic and paid). Also the N1 and N5 CI slices, and every ledger repro. A4 was last run on October 1 at `3a284ae`, so it was run at both `d44296c` and `48ed5e8`. N1 and N5 full runs and N8 were not rerun: wave 7 changes nothing they own.

**Three differences from the October 2 setup.**

1. **The N2 probe budget.** On October 2 one of N2's four probe runs hit its $1.50 cap and left 13 of 2,680 pairs unjudged. Prompt version 4 adds about 200 tokens to every judge call, so a $1.50 cap would have stopped every run short. Before any run, the [preregistration](2026-10-03-wave7-repin-preregistration.md) raised the budget from $6 to $10 ($2.50 a run). It is a stop condition only. This time every run finished: 2,680 of 2,680 pairs judged.
2. **The N7 oracle.** See the next section.
3. **The installed AI SDK.** gbrain now asks for `@ai-sdk/anthropic` ^3.0.125, so the hoisted copy moved from 3.0.115 to 3.0.127. gbrain says this changes request shapes for Claude 5 models only. The N2 judge is Haiku 4.5 and the A4 reader is Sonnet 4.6.

**The N7 amendment.** N7 compares gbrain's loop detector with an independent implementation of the open-loops guide, frozen on October 1 as the guide read at `3a284ae`. The first run at `48ed5e8` failed the preregistered closure-accuracy threshold: 34 of 39 closure cases (87.2%, threshold 95%). All five misses, and all eight extra opens that cut precision to 45 of 53, were the eight "Thanks!" threads, where gbrain now follows its new documented rule. Every other gating number held. Holding the old rule would make N7 fail every gbrain release with the fix, and would require gbrain to keep the behavior the ledger called a gap. So a [dated amendment](2026-10-03-n7-oracle-amendment.md), committed before the counted run, makes the oracle apply the rules documented by the gbrain version under test. No threshold, contract or input changed. At `d44296c` the amended oracle reproduces the October 2 receipt byte for byte. The failing receipt is kept, and the ledger records the defect as N7-8.

The compatible-pair rule fails by one pair: 5 of 51 would be 9.8%. gbrain's own release notes report the same direction on a fresh seed (compatible pairs called contradictions 2 of 50 to 5 of 50). The negation rows are the clearest case: version 4 added a line saying that "Fund A is not leading" and "Fund B is leading" agree, yet negation false alarms went from 2 to 4.

**Run-to-run variation.** The judge samples once per pair, so some of these differences could be noise. As preregistered, prompt version 3 was rerun at `d44296c` with the new budget ($6.26). It called 129 of 150 conflicts contradictions (October 2: 132), 34 of 50 undated ones (36), 5 of 51 compatible pairs (3 of 50) and 26 of 1,977 unplanted pairs (26 of 1,968), and passed all three decision rules with the compatible-pair rate at 9.8%. So between two runs of the same prompt, conflict recall moved by three and compatible-pair false alarms by two. Version 4's gain on conflicts (149, against 129 and 132) is far larger than that. Its compatible-pair count (6 of 51, against 3 and 5) is within it. The failed rule says version 4 sits at the limit, not that it is clearly worse than version 3 on compatible pairs. The rule is applied as written.

**A4: a stricter grade, at a cost.** On `d44296c`, every one of the 240 questions graded `moderate`, so the grade said nothing. On `48ed5e8`, a top result that only matched some of the query's words grades `weak`, with the new reason `keyword_relaxed_top`. That covers all 120 unanswerable questions, which is what A4-2 asked for. It also covers 80 of the 120 answerable questions, whose answer was in the top five but whose top result was such a partial match. So a reader that answers only at `moderate` or better would give up 80 correct answers on this world. The reader itself did not need the grade: on retrieved evidence it answered all 120 answerable questions and declined all 120 unanswerable ones at both commits. Name-only lookups still grade `strong` for 90 of 90 existing companies whose asked attribute is missing, the part of A4-2 that stays open.

**Why six one-hop seeds stay unresolved.** All six are "Who works at Iris?" and "Who invested in Iris?" on three seeds. "Iris" matches `companies/iris-36` and `people/iris-lee-82`. gbrain's new tiebreak picks the exactly titled page within one directory, and documents that a collision across directories stays unresolved.

## Gates

The preregistered rules decide every gate change. We moved no threshold, and no gate flipped. No row's frozen condition names a wave 7 entry.

- **N7 still gates, after the amendment.** Without it, the re-pin would have failed N7's closure-accuracy gate in CI on a change gbrain made on purpose. The amendment changes which documented rule the oracle implements, not the threshold.
- **N2, N12 and A4 still gate and pass.** N2's gate covers the hermetic arm only. The paid decision rules decide what this report may say; they never gate.
- **N1 and N5 CI slices pass every rule,** in 59 s and 80 s.
- **N9 and N13 stay report-only.** Neither has a gating rule.
- **N8 stays report-only.** Its privacy contracts still need their own preregistered rule.

## What to use and what to watch

- If you surface contradictions, prompt version 4 catches nearly every same-fact conflict, dated or not. Expect more false alarms on compatible notes, especially a negative claim about one party next to a positive claim about another. Review flagged pairs before acting on them.
- If you rely on `gbrain waiting`, upgrade. Acknowledgements no longer hide open requests, old requests rank as old, and `--as-of` makes an order reproducible.
- If you write meeting notes, a `Participants:` line now counts as attendance. People who only speak in a transcript still do not.
- If you use gbrain's retrieval grade to decide when to answer, note that `moderate` now excludes keyword matches that hit only part of the question. On this world that removes every unanswerable question and two thirds of the answerable ones.
- All of this is synthetic data with generator gold, and these worlds were inspected during the fix waves. None of it is a held-out result. The sealed confirmation sets were not touched, and no corpus was regenerated.
