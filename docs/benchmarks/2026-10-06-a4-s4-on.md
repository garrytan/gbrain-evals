# gbrain's answerability check (S4) separates answerable from unanswerable questions perfectly on A4, but ships no threshold

**Finding.** On A4's 240 synthetic questions, gbrain's System One answerability check (S4) gave every answerable question a probability of 0.93 or more and every unanswerable one 0.29 or less. With S4 gating the reader at a threshold of 0.5, the answerer abstained on **all 120 unanswerable questions and refused none of the 120 answerable ones**. Measured October 6, 2026, at gbrain `c5fb0201` (v0.60.95.0), with Claude Sonnet 5.5 as the reader and TypeSafe `jev-1.13.0` as the S4 model. The reader alone (S4 off) already abstained on 117 of 120, so S4's gain here is 3 questions (exact McNemar p = 0.25, Holm 0.50): this world is near the ceiling. One practical catch matters more than the score: **gbrain ships no calibrated threshold for the answerable slot, so turning S4 on in a fresh brain does nothing** (`no_calibration`) until a user sets a threshold and `force_on`.

Status: **Complete** (240 of 240 questions, both reader arms and S4; 0 errors). Evidence class: **development evidence**. Preregistration: [2026-10-06-a4-s4-on-preregistration.md](2026-10-06-a4-s4-on-preregistration.md), pushed before the first call (`18bae26`).

## What S4 is

[gbrain](https://github.com/garrytan/gbrain) is a memory system for agents. Its optional System One layer asks a small, fast classification model (TypeSafe's Jev) yes/no questions as probabilities. S4 asks one: "taken together, do these retrieved candidates contain the information needed to answer the query?" In `gbrain think`, an S4 verdict of `abstain` skips the answer-writing call and lists the nearest pages instead. gbrain's reducer abstains only when the probability is below the threshold minus a margin, the check covered every candidate, and no deterministic signal says the brain holds the answer (an exact identity hit or a strong retrieval grade).

A4 (an abstention benchmark in this repository) asks about fictional companies. An unanswerable question can name a company whose page lacks the asked attribute ("What is the runway of Brightline?" when the page lists only headcount), ask for a sibling company's attribute, or name a company that doesn't exist.

## The experiment

- **World and retrieval.** A4's generator at its default seed: 240 questions, 120 answerable (60 from profiles, 60 from notes) and 120 unanswerable (50 missing attribute, 40 sibling attribute, 30 absent entity). Each question's top five results come from gbrain's `query` operation, keyword-only, with no embedding provider.
- **Reader.** gbrain's house reader (the LongMemEval notes-mode prompt, 1,024 output tokens) with `anthropic:claude-sonnet-5-5` at the provider's default temperature (the model rejects `temperature`), reading the top five (retrieved arm) or the ledger's oracle pages (oracle control).
- **S4 on.** For each question, the same top five go to S4 through gbrain's own code: `candidateItem` evidence, `answerableK`, `answerableQuestion`, one `runDecide` call to `typesafe:jev-1.13.0`, and the production reducer `reduceAnswerable` with threshold **0.50** and margin 0.05, `force_on`. Where S4 says `abstain`, the retrieved arm's answer is replaced by an abstention, as `think` would do. Because the A4 world is fictional, the arm allows TypeSafe to receive query and candidate text (`decide.egress.typesafe.query/candidates: allow`, `decide.egress.private: allow`); the receipt records the config.
- **Scoring.** The preregistered A4-4 revision, applied to this arm and its matched S4-off comparison only: "I don't have the information" phrasings count as abstentions, and an answer that abstains while naming another company's value only as context counts as an abstention rather than `wrong_source`. Every number is also given under the frozen 2026-10-01 rule.

## Results

Reader `claude-sonnet-5-5`, gbrain `c5fb0201`, 120 answerable and 120 unanswerable questions per arm.

| Arm | Abstain recall (of 120 unanswerable) | False refusals (of 120 answerable) | Correct answers (of 120 answerable) | Abstains usefully (recall ≥ 0.80, false refusal ≤ 0.10) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| **S4 on** (revised rule) | **120 (100%)** | **0** | 120 | yes |
| S4 off, retrieved (revised rule) | 117 (97.5%) | 0 | 120 | yes |
| S4 on (frozen rule) | 120 (100%) | 0 | 120 | yes |
| S4 off, retrieved (frozen rule) | 116 (96.7%) | 0 | 120 | yes |
| S4 off, oracle evidence (frozen rule) | 105 (87.5%) | 0 | 120 | yes |

Paired, S4 on against S4 off (revised rule): S4 newly abstained on 3 unanswerable questions and newly refused 0 answerable ones (exact McNemar p = 0.25 and 1.0; Holm 0.50 and 1.0). That is not a demonstrated difference: the reader alone is already near the ceiling on this world.

S4's probabilities, by question class:

| Class | Questions | S4 probability, range (mean) | S4 verdict at 0.5 |
|---|---:|---|---|
| answerable, profile | 60 | 0.95 to 0.98 (0.974) | pass 60 |
| answerable, note | 60 | 0.93 to 0.97 (0.961) | pass 60 |
| missing attribute | 50 | 0.02 to 0.10 (0.030) | abstain 50 |
| sibling attribute | 40 | 0.02 to 0.06 (0.033) | abstain 40 |
| absent entity | 30 | 0.02 to 0.29 (0.062) | abstain 30 |

The report-only threshold sweep gives the same result at every threshold from 0.1 to 0.9 (recall 100%, 0 false refusals): the classes don't overlap. No question was blocked by the identity-hit or strong-grade signals; on the A4 question wording, retrieval at this pin grades no unanswerable question strong. (Looked up by company name alone, the same companies would be blocked, 90 of 90 existing-company lookups, which the hermetic arm reports as before.)

## What to use and what to avoid

- **S4 is a strong abstention signal on this kind of question.** If you run System One with a TypeSafe key and need an agent to say "I don't know" reliably, S4 over the retrieved evidence separated answerable from unanswerable questions without a single error here.
- **But you must set the threshold yourself today.** gbrain ships reference calibrations for other slots (triage, conflict), not for `answerable`, so `decide.slots.answerable.mode: on` stays inactive with reason `no_calibration`. Setting `decide.slots.answerable.threshold` (0.5 worked across the whole 0.1 to 0.9 range here) and `force_on` activates it. A gbrain follow-up should ship a calibrated reference threshold for the slot.
- **The world is easy for a good reader.** Sonnet 5.5 alone abstained on 117 of 120. A harder world (partial evidence, near-miss attributes, longer pages) would test whether S4 adds more than a few questions.

Limits: one synthetic world, one seed, one reader, one S4 model; keyword-only retrieval; S4 measured on the query top five, not on `think`'s own gathered evidence. Results describe Sonnet 5.5 as reader, not gbrain's default `claude-sonnet-4-6`.
