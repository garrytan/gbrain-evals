## What Cat 20 measures

[gbrain](https://github.com/garrytan/gbrain) is a memory system for agents. Its `brainstorm` command generates ideas by crossing pages close to a question (found by search) with distant pages from the brain (a "far set"), then has an internal judge score each idea on five axes (originality, resistance, thesis density, concrete grounding, cognitive load) and show only those whose weighted score reaches 4.0. Cat 20 asks two things: are the ideas grounded (do they cite real pages in their own text), and are they any good?

An example idea from this run (question: "What is a non-obvious next-step product for an inference-platform company that already serves autonomous-picking?"), with its internal judge note: *"Standard 'sell memory not inference' packaging; thesis is plausible but generic and lightly grounded"* (weighted 2.4, not shown to the user). Every idea's text, citations, internal scores and each outside judge's reason are in the receipt.

## The experiment

- **Generation.** `runBrainstorm` on synthetic-v1 (240 pages; embeddings `text-embedding-3-large`, reranker off) with gbrain's `brainstorm` profile: 4 close pages × 6 far pages × 3 ideas = 72 ideas per question, 3 questions. Generator and internal judge: `anthropic:claude-sonnet-5-5`. Results describe Sonnet 5.5, not gbrain's default chat model (`claude-sonnet-4-6`).
- **Outside judges.** `claude-sonnet-5-5`, `gpt-6.1-sol`, `claude-opus-5-5`, `claude-fable-5-1`, each scoring every idea (passing or not) blind to the internal verdict and to each other: one call per close-far pair with the question, both cited pages (first 1,500 characters) and the 3 ideas, novelty and usefulness on 0 to 5 integer scales with a one- or two-sentence reason. An idea's score is the mean of its two scales.
- **Decision rule** (preregistered): Cat 20 passes when the median of the four judges' mean scores on **passing** ideas is at least 2.5; inconclusive when fewer than 10 ideas pass.

## What to use and what to avoid

- **Don't read Cat 20's earlier failure as "gbrain's ideas are bad".** Four current judges put the same kind of output well above the floor.
- **With Sonnet 5.5, `gbrain brainstorm` currently shows nothing.** Its internal judge rated every idea under the 4.0 threshold, so a user would see an empty result. That is a product issue to raise with gbrain: either the threshold is too strict for this judge model or the scorer is miscalibrated against outside judges, who also disagree with its ranking.
- **Cat 20 stays report-only** until the passing set is large enough to decide on, as the preregistration requires. A rerun with gbrain's default chat model, or after gbrain adjusts the threshold, would give the decision.

Limits: one run, three questions, provider-default sampling, one corpus; the outside judges' rubric (novelty and usefulness) differs from the internal judge's five axes.
