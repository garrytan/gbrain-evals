**Finding, 2026-10-11.** This repository pins gbrain v0.60.169.0, master
`77cf8e1116e9af67c1983adcbb3223b96f0991a5` (tag `v0.60.169.0`), the release after wave 14. The previous pin was
`8a3eedeac` (v0.60.126.0). The range holds 43 first-parent merges, among them memory trust (#6396), the
verbatim-probe fixes (#6391), scoped vector search (#6380), the content-repair lane (#6384), the memory proof wave
(#6066), efficiency waves 5 to 11, wave 14 (#6480, #6481) and question-ranked `recall` (#6447). On paired keyless
VMs:

- **No category, metric, repro or check got worse because of gbrain.** Every category in the offline tier passes at
  `77cf8e111`, and the deterministic metrics hold or gain coverage. N1 (388 of 388 current values, 0 stale) and N5
  (0 prohibited outputs) pass on PGLite and on Postgres through every interface. N6 passes in process and on
  Postgres over real HTTP (155 of 155 mandatory cells, 0 leaks, 0 oracles), and so does N8. 28 of the 29 ledger
  repros pass in the counted pair, and the 29th, Cat7-1, passes at both pins on both round 4 hosts. Every
  preregistered check passes, and so do the three memory trust repros.
- **Memory trust gates at the pin and passes.** Cats 37 to 39 fail at `8a3eedeac`, which predates the feature. At
  `77cf8e111` they pass every keyless contract, so this re-pin turns them from held to gating (`eval/registry.ts`).
- **The counted pair broke two rules, and both causes sit outside gbrain.** At `77cf8e111`, N6 on Postgres lost
  coverage of `recall` because the harness misused v0.60.169.0's new `question` parameter (defect N6-5). Every
  Cat 7 operation ran 1.3 to 2.7 times slower, which made the Cat7-1 repro fail, and that came from the after VM's

  ([amendment 3](2026-10-11-wave14-repin-preregistration.md#2026-10-11-a-third-harness-defect-and-a-host-check-after-the-counted-pair-and-before-any-further-run))
  shows N6 complete at both pins and Cat 7 within 25% on every operation on at least one host. The Cat7-1 repro
  also passes at both pins.
- **The harness had four defects, and none of them was in gbrain.** Two showed in development run 1 (N6-4 and 39-8,
  [amendment 1](2026-10-11-wave14-repin-preregistration.md#2026-10-11-two-harness-defects-and-the-postgres-steps-before-the-second-paired-run)).
  Two showed in the counted pair (N6-5 and an operator-wave check that parsed gbrain's notices as part of a
  receipt). Each fix leaves the old pin's results unchanged.

### Harness defects (none in gbrain)

1. **N6-4 (run 1).** `delta`'s `next_cursor.cursor` is base64 JSON that echoes the caller's own `since_slug`, so
   N6 at `3001fd9a5` counted 60 existence-oracle probes on `delta`. The runner now decodes such cursors before
   masking them.
2. **39-8 (run 1).** Cat 39 at `3001fd9a5` counted 4 purged claims as recovered through `query`. Each one was a
   fact row from #6066's query facts arm, for the page where the preregistration says the claim remains. The runner
   read a row's page from `slug` or `entity_slug`, and fact rows name it in `page_slug`. During development the
   change was bisected to #6066: Cat 39 passes at `a2b7496c3` and fails at `36fa693ad`, and passes at `36fa693ad`
   with the facts arm off.
3. **N6-5 (counted pair).** The runner filled v0.60.169.0's optional `recall` `question` with the target's query on
   every call, alongside `include_expired` and `supersessions`. gbrain refuses that combination by design
   (`invalid_params`, with the corrected call as its fix), so `recall` read nothing for the trusted principals. An
   optional `question` now gets its own variant without the widening flags. No operation at `8a3eedeac` has an
   optional `question`.
4. **E2 (counted pair).** `edit-page-diff-order` parsed every text block of the `edit_page` reply as one JSON
   value. gbrain sometimes appends a `first_run_decisions` notice as a second block, and when it did, the check saw
   an empty diff. The diff in the reply was right every time. The check now reads the first block. It failed at both
   pins before the fix and passes 10 of 10 runs per pin on the Capy machine after it.
