## Question

How does gbrain at current master compare with the Q1 scoreboard rows at an 8,000-token reader budget, and which of
gbrain's shipped delivery features (the facts block, recall, auto budget units with date and title headers, fact source
pages) move the score?

## System under test

gbrain pinned at `0ccf2bdd4f42ed50be4cfb515eaa804ddb4b7049` (v0.60.493.0, the newest published release when the
harness was built). F6 cells set `GBRAIN_SHA` in their launch environment; the Q1 bundle's own pin is unchanged.

### Brain build, one brain per conversation

1. The Q1 install recipe at the pin (`eval/systems/gbrain-defaults/capability.json` `install`), with
   `gbrain config set facts.default_visibility world` written and read back at install. That is the value gbrain's
   bootstrap writes when the key is unset, and it has to be in place before facts are written, or a remote reader
   cannot see them.
2. Every session written with `put_page` as a `type: conversation` page carrying its session date, as in Q1.
3. With serve stopped, `gbrain extract-conversation-facts --source-id default --max-cost-usd <cap> --json --yes` over
   the whole brain. The cap is a recorded parameter of each cell (`GBRAIN_F6_EXTRACT_CAP_USD`): twice the largest
   conversation's extraction estimate in the set, at gbrain's own ledger rate ($1.08 per million tokens, what
   `--max-cost-usd` counts against), at least $1, rounded up to the dollar. The largest conversation is the set's mean
   times its `max_conversation_ratio` (below). A pass that ends `partial` is run again with the cap left, up to
   three passes. The realization fails, and is never scored, when a pass stops on its cap, when pages are still
   failed, pending, blocked, ambiguous, too large or unparsed after the last pass, or when the extraction model has no
   price. Facts written, segments, spend and each pass's counters are recorded in the finish receipt.
4. `gbrain repair safe-chunks --source default --json`, then `--apply` when the preview finds pages; recorded.
5. Q1's quiesce barrier (doctor with the brain to itself, every page, full embedding coverage, job drain).
6. A fact index: one `recall {session_id, limit 100, include_expired}` per session page, mapping every saved fact to
   its source page, so fact rows carry exact provenance.
7. Serve on the full surface (`gbrain serve --surface full`), the same stack for every arm.

### Arms

Every arm is a packed component arm at 8,000 tokens, read from the same brain. Every arm writes all six search keys
explicitly before it reads (the overlay):

| Arm | Call | Search keys beyond arm A's |
|---|---|---|
| A0 | `query {limit: 50}`, no token_budget (gbrain's auto delivery, the harness packs 8,000) | none |
| A | `query {limit: 50, token_budget: 5500}` | arm A's keys: query_facts_block false, query_budget_unit chunk, evidence_date_header false, evidence_titles false, fact_source_pages false, saved_facts_supersede true |
| P | as A | query_facts_block true |
| B | `recall {question, query: <question>, budget_tokens: 5500}` | none |
| C | as A | query_budget_unit auto, evidence_date_header true, evidence_titles true |
| D | as A | C's, plus query_facts_block true |
| E | as A | D's, plus fact_source_pages true |

A0 takes arm A's keys. Without a token_budget gbrain delivers in auto units at its conversation default of 24,000
tokens whatever query_budget_unit says, so for A0 the keys only matter for the facts block (off). No arm sends
`autocut`; gbrain's shipped default applies (tokenmax resolves autocut off).

The harness applies Q1's packing rule (`packContext`, cap and cut) to gbrain's delivered rows in delivered order, fact
rows and recall facts first, and records packing loss per row.

Each arm's retrieval is identified by `{op, args, overlay}` and staged under `<mode>@<op>-<12 hex of its sha256>`, so
no two arms share a retrieval unless they name the same one. F6 cells retrieve policy-major: every question of a
conversation under one arm before the next arm. The shim applies an overlay only when it changes: stop serve,
`gbrain config set` all six keys, `gbrain config get` each one back (a mismatch invalidates the cell), start serve, and
write the overlay hash and the switch time into the retrieval record.

## Statistics

The estimand on every set is Q1's: the per-question mean over the three readers' canonical scores, clustered by
conversation on S1, S2a, S2b, S3 and D8 and by question on S4-slice.

**Headline (S1, descriptive, as in Q1).** gbrain's headline F6 row is arm **P**, the shipped product at the pin
(`query` with an 8k token budget and master's defaults), fixed now and never chosen after results. It is reported
beside the reused A24 rows and the F6 reruns (temporal graph 0.31.2, file agent, hybrid), and beside Q1's gbrain row
(0.60.138.0) labeled with its version. S1 has 10 clusters and Q1's minimum detectable difference is about 13.6 points,
so these rows are **descriptive and unranked**: Q1's "What the report may say" sentences apply, and no "leads", "beats"
or "best" is written for any S1 comparison against another system.

**Confirmatory family (D8, the F3 repeat on fresh long histories).** Sonnet 5.5 decides, as in #194; Opus 5.5 and
gpt-6.1-sol are reported beside it.

1. **F3, the facts block (primary):** P − A on D8. Exact two-sided McNemar test on Sonnet's paired correct/incorrect
   (canonical score ≥ 0.5), with the difference in points and a question-level paired bootstrap 95% interval (10,000
   resamples, seed `f6-d8`). A conversation-cluster bootstrap interval is reported as a sensitivity view.
2. **Full stack:** D − A on D8, the same test.
3. **recall:** B − A on D8, the same test.

Holm across the three, α = 0.05. "The facts block helps on D8" is written only if (1) passes with gbrain ahead; a
failing or reversed (1) is written as plainly. Sizing (simulated with `~/.capy/work/f6/d8-size.py`, binomial
win/loss model): at 300 paired questions, a test at #194's B3 − A0 effect (33 wins, 1 loss per 200) passes in 100% of
draws, at half that effect 99.95%, at a third 96.5%; at 200 questions the half effect passes 98.3%. The D8 limit stays
300 unless the custodian's count of eligible items is lower, in which case every eligible item runs.

**Secondary, descriptive.** Every arm on every set with clustered intervals; the arm contrasts P − A0 (master's budgeted
product against Q1's call shape), C − A, D − C and E − D on S1, S2b, S2a and S3, each a measurement with no claim. On S1
the Sonnet P and D answers also get the frontier-judge column, with Q1's 90% agreement flag.

**Ceilings and models.** People-facing models are reported first (Sonnet 5.5 and gpt-6.1-sol, then Opus 5.5). A reader at
or above 95% on both arms of a contrast is called out as a ceiling and not counted as a tie or a win.

**Harness checks.** Each cell reports packing loss, overlay switches with their verified config, the count of retrieval
records whose overlay hash differs from the arm's (expected 0), extraction completeness per realization, and every
`harness_invalid` answer with its reason. A cell with an unverified overlay is invalid, never scored.

**Cohorts.** Q1's rules: a claim needs at least 9 of 10 clusters and at least 95% of scheduled questions scored; excluded
ids and reasons are published as counts with best-case and worst-case bounds.
