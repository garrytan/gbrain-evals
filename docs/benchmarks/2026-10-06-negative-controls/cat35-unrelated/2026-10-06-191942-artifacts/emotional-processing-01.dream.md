The user debriefed a pitch meeting with Anouk Verlinden, founder of Lumopact, a startup building software for regional freight coordination. The debrief shows what is persuading them and what they still want to verify. See [[people/anouk-verlinden]], [[people/tomas-brekke]] and [[companies/vantabrook]].

## Deal facts as stated
- Lumopact is raising a two million seed, led by Vantabrook. The user thinks the round is coming together faster than expected.
- The product is a software layer for regional freight coordination that cuts empty miles by matching loads across smaller carriers.
- Terms: a twelve million post-money cap on the note, which the user called reasonable for a pre-revenue company with early traction.
- The SAFE has a twenty percent discount, and pro rata is offered to anyone above fifty k.
- In their words: "I am in for twenty five k, final answer". That is below the pro rata threshold, so they get no pro rata rights unless they negotiate something on the side. They said this is "Not the end of the world."
- Everyone is on the same SAFE. Vantabrook only gets a board observer seat, which the user appreciates.
- The team is seven people, five of them engineers, so go-to-market is lean.

## What is building conviction
- A grocer pilot cut estimated freight emissions eighteen percent. The user called it a real number from a real customer.
- Anouk has about eight years in supply chain.
- On founder temperament, the user said: "Anouk stayed dead calm under the nastiest diligence questions, I like that." She did not get defensive when pushed on unit economics and walked through her assumptions clearly. The assistant added that this composure is a good signal for investor updates and board meetings.

## What the user is hesitant about
- The market is competitive, with bigger players that have more capital.
- The user wants to know who else is in the round and whether they add strategic value or just cash.
- The user has never worked with Tomas Brekke, who is running point for Vantabrook. They want to "figure out if he's going to be helpful or just a name on the cap table."
- The deck font was a minor annoyance, which the user acknowledged does not matter.

## Planned next steps (user intentions)
- Back-channel on Tomas: the user will ping a couple of founders he has backed before.
- Diligence: "I need two customer reference calls before I wire anything", to confirm the pilot results are real and repeatable. The assistant offered to draft questions on implementation friction and measurable outcomes, and the user said yes.
- Value beyond the check: the user is thinking of introducing Lumopact to Ostrafell for a second pilot, since Ostrafell runs regional distribution. The assistant agreed it could help build a case study library.
- The assistant offered to set reminders for the reference calls and the Tomas back-channel. The transcript does not show that the user accepted.

## Pattern worth noting
The user separates the size of their commitment, which is firm, from the conditions for releasing the money, which are reference calls and a read on the lead investor. They weigh founder character, shown by composure under hostile questioning, alongside the numbers. They also think about how to be useful to the company after investing.
