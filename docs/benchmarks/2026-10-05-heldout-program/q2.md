# Q2: typed list-line guards and relationship phrasings

This page records the held-out verdicts of Q2, the follow-up to [P5](p5.md) in the
[nine-plan held-out program](../2026-10-05-heldout-program.md). Q2 is decided on gbrain branch `capy/q2-parser-gaps`
(frozen build `4ec7fbbe4`, baseline master `5b5891069`), from one held-out campaign run by the second custodian.

Typed relation lines (`line_grammar.enabled`) stay an opt-in. The grammar reads relation lines precisely: it recalls
every valid relation line in the conformance set (511 of 511), keeps 99.4% of the correct lines master reads, and 299
of 300 relation lines agents wrote are correct. It still reads too many bracket-led list items as fact lines on
natural text. Every wrong line comes from the stress stratum of template-, glossary- and changelog-shaped notes: 459
of 583 minted lines are wrong, so the junk gate (G1) and the conformance decoys (G3) fail. The write-then-answer test
(G6) does not run, because it runs only after G1 to G5 pass. The guards against template slots and usage labels, the
grammar diagnostics and settings-bound link extraction ship regardless, because they only remove junk, explain refusals
or make a setting change converge.

Two link-typing units pass their fresh confirmation and ship: U34 (advisory, board and investor roles are not
employment starts; it cuts false employment starts by 0.41 per probe person) and U1 (adviser wording; advisor-trap
accuracy +0.43). U25 (ordinary job roles with leave idioms) changes nothing on the confirmation set and is reverted. U6
(onboarding and first-day starts) improves its own metric but costs current-employer recall and adds one wrong
transition, so it is reverted.

## Deviations

The [freeze record](../2026-10-06-q2-parser-gaps-preregistration.md#freeze-record) lists every deviation with its
hashes. In short:

- The overlap check against sealed-confirmation-v1 was not run (the custodian does not hold it).
- C-gate baseline arms ran on the units-off comparator `2d95d01b0`, as the freeze record states; W1 and W2 were read
  from their page directories because the runner expected a manifest key the frozen manifests did not use.
- The career and N manifests were reformatted to the runner's keys before use, with every path and hash unchanged.
- Two N template files with invalid frontmatter were dropped and N was re-minted before any label.
- The G2 sample and label steps wrote receipts without `run_status`, so the custodian recorded wrapper receipts;
  nothing was re-run.
- [Run deviations after the freeze](../2026-10-06-q2-parser-gaps-preregistration.md#run-deviations-after-the-freeze-recorded-2026-10-08-after-the-verdicts):
  the export ran outside the campaign guard because G6 did not run (deviation 5); the stress floor was built against
  the custodian's looser count; nine access-log lines were reconstructed after another lane destroyed the custodian's
  VM, and the material still verifies.

The harness defects behind the wrapper receipts, the manifest keys and the export are fixed on this branch after the
decision.
