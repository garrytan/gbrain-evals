# P2: hub dampening, date-grounded extraction, speaker attribution

This page mirrors the evaluation records of gbrain [#6020](https://github.com/garrytan/gbrain/pull/6020) for the [nine-plan held-out program](../2026-10-05-heldout-program.md). Two of its three ideas pass their held-out tests and turn on by default. Date-grounded extraction cuts saved facts that still carry an unresolved relative date from 8.95% to 2.05%. Speaker attribution lifts answers about what the assistant said from 44.1% to 95.8%. Hub dampening waits on its sealed run, and development predicts a fail.

## Development

Records at the PR head: [date grounding](https://github.com/garrytan/gbrain/blob/7c2406a5c97478bcd376734c228b22800d55e869/docs/eval/decisions/p2-date-grounding-dev/README.md), [attribution](https://github.com/garrytan/gbrain/blob/7c2406a5c97478bcd376734c228b22800d55e869/docs/eval/decisions/p2-attribution-dev/README.md), [attribution gate](https://github.com/garrytan/gbrain/blob/7c2406a5c97478bcd376734c228b22800d55e869/docs/eval/decisions/p2-attribution-gate-dev/README.md), [hub dampening](https://github.com/garrytan/gbrain/blob/7c2406a5c97478bcd376734c228b22800d55e869/docs/eval/decisions/p2-hub-dampening-dev/README.md).

- **Date grounding.** Unresolved relative-date facts fall from 6.9% to 1.3% on LoCoMo dev and from 6.8% to 2.3% on LongMemEval-S, with the extractor's audit rows excluded. QA is inconclusive.
- **Attribution.** On the baseline, saved facts trail pages by 73.3 points on questions about what the assistant said. With attribution on, single-session-assistant accuracy rises from 23.3% to 60.0% (+36.7 [+20.0, +53.3]), and single-session-user stays at 73.7%.
- **Hub dampening.** This was measured on the development hub-heavy world (seed 1, 32,652 pages). Every half degree raises concept recall by 1.4 to 3.0 nDCG@5 points but loses most hub-as-answer recall (−78.5 to −83.0), and both controls beat it on concept recall. Development predicts a held-out fail.
