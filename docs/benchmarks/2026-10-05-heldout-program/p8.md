**Why quote grounding failed.** The second custodian classified the first run's 15 false flags:

- 8 come from three matcher gaps: link display text kept as `[Name]`, the source's inner double quotes written as single quotes, and editorial brackets inside a word.
- 3 are quotes of the user's question.
- 3 alter a word and were judged "close".
- 1 changes a possessive.

Build `7715e647a` accepts the three writer habits and counts the user's question as a source, as the retest amendment records.

**Corpus exposure.** Both the first sealed run and the voided retest drew sessions from the sealed-confirmation-v2 corpus, which is reserved for release decisions:

- The first run asked about 60 v2 sessions and imported 30 of the 40 v2 histories.
- The voided retest sent 260 v2 sessions to its question writer and imported 5 more histories, for 34 of 40 in all.
- Only custodian machines read the sessions. Their text went to model providers inside embedding, think and judge prompts. v2's questions and labels were not used.

The retest on v2 is void, not a verdict, and its results do not shape P8's default. The [v2 protocol](../2026-10-01-sealed-confirmation-v2-protocol.md) records the exposure.

**Scorer fix in this change.** The quote-grounding runner now attributes gbrain's unverified quotes to answer spans by exact text in gbrain's reported form, not by substring containment (`flaggedSpans`). `--make-questions --exclude-questions` keeps a retest set disjoint from an earlier held-out set.

Aggregates only: sealed questions, answers and spans stay with the custodians.
