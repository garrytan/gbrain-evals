## 1. The plain-English version

gbrain is a personal knowledge brain that runs on your laptop. Files on disk in markdown, indexed in Postgres or PGLite, with a hybrid retrieval pipeline that beats commodity vector RAG by 30+ percentage points on relational queries and beats published memory-benchmark baselines on public conversational data. v0.40.6.0 is the 24th release since the first BrainBench scorecard. Across all 24 releases the headline retrieval numbers have **not regressed** by even a tenth of a point.

The headline product change in the last two months is `gbrain think` — the synthesis layer that turns "here are 10 chunks that mention your query" into "here is an actual answer, with citations and an honest note on what the brain doesn't know yet." This snapshot ships **Cat 29** to quantify the difference: on a 5-question fixture of multi-page relational questions over a 164-page synthetic corpus, `gbrain think` averages **5.60/10** vs raw `search` averaging **1.60/10** — a **+4.00 point** lift, with think winning 3 of 5 questions and tying 1 more. That's the X-thread claim ("search gives you raw pages, think gives you the answer") backed by Haiku-judged numbers.

Plus 12 more new Cats this wave covering everything else that shipped v0.36 → v0.40.6: ZeroEntropy default (Cat 17 partial + Cat 18), `doctor --remediate` (Cat 19), source-isolation P0 seal (Cat 22), phantom-redirect (Cat 23), capture/ingest provenance (Cat 24), trajectory routing (Cat 25), contextual retrieval (Cat 26), graph signals (Cat 27), federated sync latency (Cat 28), brainstorm novelty (Cat 20), code retrieval (Cat 21).

## 4. Methodology notes

### Synthetic-v1 corpus (new this wave)

`eval/data/synthetic-v1/` — 164 pages generated deterministically (seed 0x42424242) by `eval/generators/synthetic-v1-gen.ts`. Pure placeholder names (alice-example, bob-demo, acme-ai-0, fund-x, foundry-labs). No PII. Mirrors a real production brain's shape (~1/100 scale): 30 companies, 50 people, 25 concepts, 20 meetings, 15 deals, 10 daily notes, 5 originals, 5 writing, 5 projects. Each company has a typed-claim `## Facts` fence with 3-5 metric readings (ARR, team_size) on different dates for trajectory tests. Wiki-link density mirrors the production graph.

The generator is the artifact — re-run `bun eval/generators/synthetic-v1-gen.ts` to reproduce byte-for-byte. CI privacy guard at `scripts/check-synthetic-no-pii.sh` (TODO).

### GBRAIN_HOME isolation

A critical fix landed this wave: every Cat that calls `configureGateway()` now sets `process.env.GBRAIN_HOME` to a fresh tempdir BEFORE engine setup. Without this, the user's real `~/.gbrain/config.json` embedding pin overrides the per-cell gateway config — the embedding-column registry reads file-plane cfg first and ignores the gateway override. Pre-fix, Voyage and ZE cells returned 0 results; post-fix they return the right numbers. Cat 18 + Cat 25 + Cat 27 + Cat 29 all use this pattern.

### Haiku judge across cats

Cat 20 / 25 / 29 use `claude-haiku-4-5-20251001` as the answer-quality judge with a strict JSON-output prompt. Single judge per pair (not ensemble) — keeps cost ~$0.10/probe. Cross-modal multi-judge (Cat 17 sibling pattern) would tighten reliability; deferred for follow-up.

## 6. Honest limitations + what would tighten this

1. **Synthetic-v1 is small (164 pages).** Real brains are 100K+. Numbers on synthetic-v1 are directionally honest but Cat 26 already shows the corpus is too small to differentiate contextual-retrieval modes. Scaling to amara-life-v1 (already in repo, ~500 emails+slack+calendar) is the cleanest next step.

2. **Cat 25 trajectory has only 2 probes.** Baseline + wave both score 9.5+ because the corpus is small enough that the Facts fence is fully in-context. To show the trajectory routing win, run against LongMemEval `_oracle` temporal-reasoning bucket (the real-world tilt scenario from the v0.28.8 published number where think -1.5pt vs MemPalace). Filed as follow-up.

3. **Cat 27 graph signals don't flip rank-1.** Multipliers are 1.05x / 1.10x / 0.95x by design — they're ranking refinements, not rank-1 flips. The right metric is "do signals improve nDCG@10 on probes where baseline lands the gold at rank 2-3?" Current probes have baseline at rank 5+ where the boost is structurally too small. Next iteration: probes calibrated to the boost magnitude.

4. **Cat 28 PGLite WASM dominates.** Parallel sync's production claim is on Postgres where workers share the pool. The hermetic single-process PGLite benchmark catches per-engine cold-start overhead instead. Needs a `DATABASE_URL`-gated Postgres-mode Cat 28 variant.

5. **Cat 23 PGLite lacks pg_trgm.** The phantom-redirect resolver's fuzzy path expects pg_trgm. PGLite hermetic mode misses 4 of 7 — Postgres mode would resolve them via trigram similarity. Needs Postgres-mode variant.

6. **Cat 21 corpus is small (60/401 files).** Both providers tie because the per-file slug shape ("code/__core__index") makes substring matching fragile. Larger corpus + better symbol-extraction-based gold (parse `export` lines, check chunk-text contains the symbol) would surface the voyage-code-3 win this Cat exists to measure.

7. **No fail-loud CI privacy guard yet.** The synthetic corpus uses placeholder names (`alice-example`, `bob-demo`, etc.) but there's no checked-in script that fails the build if a real name leaks into the corpus. Next-wave TODO: `scripts/check-synthetic-no-pii.sh` matching CLAUDE.md's privacy rule.

## 9. What changes for gbrain users

If you're upgrading from v0.36.x or older to v0.40.6.0:

- Retrieval numbers will not move. If they do, file an issue with the diff.
- `gbrain think` now grounds temporal answers in the typed-claim timeline by default. Cat 29 confirms the synthesis layer is +4pt over raw search on relational questions.
- `gbrain doctor --remediate --yes --target-score 90 --max-usd 5` drives the brain to 90/100 unattended. Cat 19 confirms the loop closes.
- ZeroEntropy is the new default for embedding + reranker. Cat 18 suggests Voyage may be the right swap for synthetic-corpus shape; Cat 17 phase2 (in progress) will give the production-grade comparison on rich-prose. Bring your own key from `zeroentropy.dev`.
- `gbrain sync --all` parallelizes every configured source under a per-source lock. New `gbrain sources status` shows what's syncing right now.
- Federated source-isolation is provably clean per Cat 22.

If you're upgrading from v0.20.0 or older: the retrieval contract is the same, but everything else around it changed. Read [`CHANGELOG.md`](https://github.com/garrytan/gbrain/blob/master/CHANGELOG.md) for the migration story.
