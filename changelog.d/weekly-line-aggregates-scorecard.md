- **Weekly held-out line: aggregates writer and scorecard.** `eval/runner/weekly-line/` turns a week's or a block's cell records into the only files that leave custody under the [weekly line's preregistration](docs/benchmarks/2026-11-23-weekly-outcome-line-preregistration.md): per world and per class, each row's mean score and gbrain's paired difference against the grep agent and the two memory servers, per reader and pooled, with cost per correct answer, the gbrain release and commit, the comparators' versions and the seed and phrasing ids. A block adds its words (ahead, behind, level within 3 points, split by reader, no measurable difference, ceiling, descriptive, changed within the block), Holm's step-down in each family with the O'Brien-Fleming bounds of the one look at the 16th counted world, the restricted wild cluster bootstrap beside the paired t, per-kind rows, exposure and the grep agent's turn-cap and context-overflow rates. Weeks carry no verdict word, a rolling display over the last four weekly points that names their builds, and a regression flag with a seeded false-alarm rate. The writer refuses any field outside an allow-list of keys and value patterns, so question text, values, names and plan content cannot leave custody, and a Markdown scorecard and block section render only from aggregates that pass it. Keyless tests in `test/eval/weekly-line.test.ts` cover every word, the look, the missing-data mask, the ceiling, the refusal and a hand-computed t interval and Holm step-down.
