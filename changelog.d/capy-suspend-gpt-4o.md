- **New campaigns can no longer use gpt-4o or gpt-4o-mini.** From 2026-11-21 a preregistration's registry entry lists every model it runs with its role, and `bun eval/runner/prereg.ts` in CI and the launch check in `attestPreregistration` refuse any gpt-4o model in a role we choose, judges included, with an error that names the rule in [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md#choose-models). A compared system's shipped default, such as a comparator server's own extraction model, is declared as `system-internal` and tested as shipped. Earlier campaigns and published results keep their models.
