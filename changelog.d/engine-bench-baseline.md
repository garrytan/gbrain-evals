engine-bench baseline ([docs/benchmarks/2026-11-17-engine-bench.md](docs/benchmarks/2026-11-17-engine-bench.md)): development runs of gbrain on PGLite and Postgres at v0.60.734.0 and v0.60.735.0. Both engines pass six keyless correctness checks with identical counts, 301 of 302 CLI, MCP and markdown outputs are byte-identical (the exception is `stale` in `gbrain search --json`), and LongMemEval-S retrieval returns the same top 5 on 102 of 102 questions. At 16 concurrent clients PGLite serves 0.05x Postgres's reads, and PGLite's import slows from 27.7 to 1.5 pages per second by 45,000 pages while Postgres holds 23. The Rust engine is not measured yet.
