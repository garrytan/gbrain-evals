- **The changing-facts generator can mint long worlds of about 1,000,000 tokens for the weekly held-out line.** A new generator version, `changing-facts-gen@2`, plans 700 to 800 sessions over the same 18 months instead of 140 to 160, so each world is about five times longer while evidence keeps its place in time and each session stays the size the renderer handles well. Standard worlds still plan as `changing-facts-gen@1`, byte for byte as before. Set W gets a second seed set, `W1-long`, on slots 2001 to 2999 with its first batch template (`eval/decisions/datasets/bgw-2001-manifest.json`), and the mint, validation and manifest fill in [eval/custody/bg1/](eval/custody/bg1/) size each batch at its seed set's length; a real fill refuses any other length. The weekly cells take their set from the batch (`BGW` or `BGW-long`), and on long worlds only the ingest part of an estimate grows with the extra tokens, while the 200,000-token rates are unchanged. A template-rendered long fixture and a report of where each question's evidence sits in its world, by tenth of the history, are in [eval/data/changing-facts-v1/long-fixture/](eval/data/changing-facts-v1/long-fixture/); the fixture is never sealed.
