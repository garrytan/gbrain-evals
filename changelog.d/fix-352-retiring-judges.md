- **Runners no longer fall back to the retiring gpt-4o and gpt-4.1-mini judges.** OpenAI retires `gpt-4o-2024-08-06` (LongMemEval's and LoCoMo's official judge) and `gpt-4.1-mini` (BEAM's) on 2027-01-14. `eval/runner/judge-repeat.ts` and `judgeAnswer` in `eval/runner/memory-qa/qa-v2.ts` now refuse to run those benchmarks without a judge named by `--judge`, with an error that gives the date; re-scoring a frozen or published run still works by naming its judge, such as `--judge openai:gpt-4o-2024-08-06`, until that date, after which the error says Azure OpenAI serves both until 2027-04-14. The instruments are marked `historical` in `eval/runner/memory-qa/instruments.ts`, with their hashes and published scores unchanged, and [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) records the retirement.
