- **Questions that describe their subject instead of naming it, for the weekly held-out line.** `changing-facts-gen@3` (profile `alias`, about 200,000 tokens) gives every person, company, project and deal a nickname, a role, a one-letter misspelling and a pronoun, plus a property and sometimes a relation to another entity; sessions use those instead of the canonical name, which appears only in the line that introduces it, and each question describes its subject by its property or in two steps through a relation, so no name ties a question to its evidence sessions. The generator refuses to plan a world where a question shares a distinctive word with the planned lines of its evidence sessions (a kind word such as "offsite" still finds the evidence of 8 of the 192 dev questions, where only one entity holds that kind, and `isolatingWords` counts them), where a description fits no entity or more than one, or where an alias names two entities, and the alias worlds carry the gold-audit fixes; `validate.ts` adds an `alias_leak` gate that fails a rendered @3 world naming an entity outside its introduction or holding a question's key terms, as whole phrases, in its evidence.
