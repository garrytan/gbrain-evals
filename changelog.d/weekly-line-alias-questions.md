- **Questions that describe their subject instead of naming it, for the weekly held-out line.** `changing-facts-gen@3` (profile `alias`, about 200,000 tokens) gives every person, company, project and deal a nickname, a role, a one-letter misspelling and a pronoun, plus a property and sometimes a relation to another entity; sessions use those instead of the canonical name, which appears only in the line that introduces it, and each question describes its subject by its property or in two steps through a relation, so no name ties a question to its evidence sessions. The generator checks that no question shares a distinctive word with its evidence sessions (a kind word such as "offsite" still finds the evidence of 8 of the 192 dev questions, where only one entity holds that kind, and `isolatingWords` counts them), that every description resolves to exactly one entity, and that no alias names two entities; `validate.ts` adds an `alias_leak` gate that fails a rendered @3 world naming an entity outside its introduction or holding a question's key terms in its evidence. Three public dev worlds, `bg1-dev-alias-01` to `-03` (seeds `bg1-dev-alias-seed-v1:<id>`), are committed with their plans, questions and template fixtures under [eval/data/changing-facts-v1/](eval/data/changing-facts-v1/); @1 and @2 output is byte-identical. The weekly pilot gains an alias length (`pilot.ts plan|campaigns|report --length alias`): the four rows on `bg1-dev-alias-01` and the grep agent's judge check on `-02` and `-03` at set BGW's caps, the grep agent first and the other rows only when its claude-opus-5-5 accuracy pooled over the three worlds, from complete cells with every scheduled answer, is below 0.95 (`campaigns` writes those rows only from `--grep-cell` readings that clear it), under campaign ids of their own; the dry run shows each cell's scheduled questions. `render.ts`, `validate.ts` and `mint.sh --dev` take the alias ids.
