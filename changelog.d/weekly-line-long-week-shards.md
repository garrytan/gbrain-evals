- **A week of 1,000,000-token worlds can now run on the weekly line's VMs and keep its records apart from the standard line.** A cell too long for one VM (a long week's extract-first server, about 121 hours) now runs as launch units of its week's worlds, each within one VM's 72 hours, pushed and opening only its own worlds and leased its share of the cell's estimate; the weekly runner merges the units into the run directory, with a run configuration that covers the whole week, before the cell's judging and its particle-1 re-read, which reads every unit and refuses one staged by another weekly run, and the week settles only once every unit and the merge are recorded, as done or as harness failures. The merge keeps any unit's invalid status, and for sealed worlds it writes only to a custody root outside the repository. Standard weeks still run each cell as one unit. Long worlds are recorded under their own seed id, `W1-long`, and the aggregates refuse a week or block that mixes the two lengths, including through its history or previous block, or labels a long world `W1`. A long batch's mint now pauses after its first five slots until a supply limit measured from those slots is recorded, while BG1-C and W1 keep their limit of 504 Gemini requests per kept world ([eval/custody/bg1/supply.ts](eval/custody/bg1/supply.ts)). A long week's estimate refuses a row with no measured ingest rate instead of counting that ingest as nothing.
