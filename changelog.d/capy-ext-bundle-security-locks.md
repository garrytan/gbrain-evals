- **The comparison bundles and the comparator install patched versions of their vulnerable Python packages.** Six external bundles under [docs/comparison-systems/](docs/comparison-systems.md#systems-in-the-head-to-head-scoreboard) now lock the releases [#433](https://github.com/garrytan/gbrain-evals/pull/433) chose for `aiohttp`, `anyio`, `certifi`, `cryptography`, `httpcore2`, `httpx2`, `pyjwt`, `urllib3` and `yarl`, each relocked with the uv its own Dockerfile pins; five are byte-identical to their `eval/systems` shim's lock. [comparator.lock.json](eval/harness-provider/comparator.lock.json) raises `aiohttp` and `certifi` the same way. The pin table carries the new hashes. Earlier campaigns (the Q1 scoreboard, F6, RS1, BG1 and BG1-C) ran on the old ones, and their published rows keep them.
