# Acme Example
Acme Example builds warehouse picking robots. It was founded in 2021 by [[people/alice-example]] and [[people/bob-example]].
Headquarters: Denver, Colorado. [[companies/fund-a]] led the $4M seed round in 2022 and the $18M Series A in March 2025.
Its first large customer is [[companies/kestrel-logistics-example]]. See [[concepts/warehouse-robotics]].
