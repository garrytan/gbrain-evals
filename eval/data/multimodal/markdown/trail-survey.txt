Walked the north ridge trail with the club on Saturday to map the sections
that failed during the winter storms. The worst damage is a forty meter
washout below the second switchback where the old culvert collapsed and the
runoff cut a channel straight down the tread. Two smaller slumps near the
overlook can be fixed with rock armoring in a single work day.

We measured grades with a clinometer every fifty meters. The design standard
for new tread in this district is a ten percent maximum sustained grade, and
the washed out section was running at nearly eighteen percent, which explains
why it keeps failing. The reroute we flagged follows a bench to the east and
stays under eight percent the whole way.

The grade math the crew uses on site is simple enough to keep in a script:

```ts
export function gradePercent(riseMeters: number, runMeters: number): number {
  if (runMeters <= 0) throw new Error('run must be positive');
  return (riseMeters / runMeters) * 100;
}
```
