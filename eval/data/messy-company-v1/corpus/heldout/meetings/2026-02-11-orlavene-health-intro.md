# Intro: Orlavene Health

February 11, 2026

Intro call. Orlavene Health, hospital group in Denver. Prospect, not a customer. No signed agreement. Mostly receiving-team questions; less interest in the yard map than I expected.

Orlavene described the morning pinch at the central receiving dock: linen truck, food delivery, then medical supplies all arriving around shift change. Door 2 gets treated as the overflow door, except nobody tells the driver when the assignment changes. Driver waits outside, receiving calls security, security calls receiving. Lots of phone tag.

Walked through Pemberline Dock. Appointment board first, then door assignment and arrival status. They wanted to see what a receiving clerk actually clicks with a truck already waiting. Skipped the broader overview. Used a sample food-service delivery; moved it from Door 1 to Door 2 and showed the updated board.

Orlavene asked about deliveries that arrive without an appointment. Also whether the afternoon team could see why a morning booking moved. Need a tighter follow-up demo around those two situations, not another tour of every screen.

Pricing question came late. Explained per seat per year. No numbers discussed. They need to work out who would use it: receiving desk, shift leads, possibly security. Not everyone who gets a delivery notification.

Name looks close to Orlavine Medical Supply. Different company. Keep Orlavene Health on the invite.

## Actions

- Send recap and ask for a sample receiving schedule.
- Build demo: unplanned arrival, door change, afternoon handoff.
- Ask Orlavene which receiving staff should join the next call.
