# Enterprise Master Subscription Agreement: Vantorre Group and Affiliates

**Written on:** 2025-06-30  
**Drafted:** 2025-06-02  
**Signed:** 2025-06-30  
**Effective date:** 2025-07-01

## 1. Parties, Purpose, and Construction

1.1 **Parties.** This Enterprise Master Subscription Agreement is entered into by Pemberline Systems (“Pemberline”) and Vantorre Group, together with its affiliate Vantorre Mobility. Vantorre Group and Vantorre Mobility are referred to collectively as “Customer” and individually by their respective names. References to a particular subscribing entity apply to that entity’s subscription and operations.

1.2 **Purpose.** Pemberline provides Pemberline Dock, a hosted yard and dock scheduling product. Customer subscribes to the Service to coordinate vehicle arrivals, appointment reservations, dock assignments, yard movements, and related operational records. This Agreement governs those subscriptions and the handling of information submitted through the Service.

1.3 **Distinct dates.** The Agreement was drafted on June 2, 2025, and signed on June 30, 2025. Its effective date is July 1, 2025. Execution on 2025-06-30 does not advance the effective date or substitute the signature date for the commencement of the Agreement.

1.4 **Affiliate participation.** Vantorre Mobility participates as the affiliate expressly identified in this Agreement. A reference to affiliates does not itself subscribe any other entity, grant seats to another entity, or create a separate order. Operational correspondence may use VTG or VTRG for Vantorre Group and VTM or VMob for Vantorre Mobility, but the full entity names control this Agreement.

## 2. Definitions

2.1 **Service.** “Service” means Pemberline Dock as made available by Pemberline, including its scheduling interface, associated administrative functions, and documentation describing supported use.

2.2 **Seat and Authorized User.** A “Seat” is a subscribed user allocation for the Service. An “Authorized User” is an individual whom the relevant subscribing entity authorizes to use a Seat. Authorization may include a gate clerk, dock coordinator, transport planner, or administrator, according to Customer’s actual staffing requirements.

2.3 **Customer Data.** “Customer Data” means information supplied by or for Customer to the Service, including appointment details, carrier references, vehicle identifiers, driver contact information, arrival records, dock assignments, and attachments. It includes information created from those records through Customer’s ordinary use of Service functions.

2.4 **Documentation and Support Request.** “Documentation” means Pemberline’s instructions for supported operation of the Service. A “Support Request” is a request concerning access, a suspected defect, configuration, or another Service issue submitted through the support channels made available by Pemberline.

2.5 **Order Schedule.** “Order Schedule” means Article 23. “Agreement” means these articles, the Order Schedule, and the execution block. Examples of operational records explain the subject matter of a clause and do not establish additional subscriptions, performance measurements, or fees.

## 3. Agreement Period

3.1 **First period.** The first agreement period ends on 2026-06-30 for both Vantorre Group and Vantorre Mobility.

3.2 **Successive periods.** The Agreement continues for successive twelve-month periods.

## 4. Subscription and Seats

4.1 **Subscription grant.** Subject to this Agreement, Pemberline grants Customer access to the Service for Customer’s internal yard and dock scheduling activities. The grant is a right to use the hosted Service, not a transfer of ownership in the software or its underlying components.

4.2 **Subscribed allocations.** Vantorre Group subscribes to **260 Seats**. Vantorre Mobility subscribes to **40 Seats**. Each allocation applies to the named entity. Listing both allocations in one Agreement does not convert them into an undifferentiated seat pool.

4.3 **User administration.** Customer shall designate personnel to administer Authorized Users and maintain accurate access assignments. When a receiving supervisor changes shifts or a gate clerk leaves Customer’s employment, Customer shall update the affected access rather than leaving credentials available for general use. User credentials shall not be displayed at a shared dispatch desk.

4.4 **Operational permissions.** Customer is responsible for deciding which users may create appointments, revise dock assignments, view records, or administer other users. A clerk assigned to outbound Door 12 need not receive authority to alter the full site calendar merely because the clerk can view that door’s appointments.

4.5 **Changes.** Requests to change subscribed Seat quantities shall be handled through a written agreement identifying the affected entity. An access request, configuration ticket, or roster spreadsheet does not by itself amend the quantities stated here.

## 5. Fees and Discount

5.1 **Annual Seat price.** The price per Seat per year is **USD 1,950** for Vantorre Group and **USD 1,950** for Vantorre Mobility. The price is stated before application of the discount in Section 5.2.

5.2 **Discount.** A **15% discount** applies to the annual Seat price for each of Vantorre Group and Vantorre Mobility. The same percentage applies to both subscribing entities; the separate Seat quantities remain those specified in Article 4.

5.3 **Basis of charges.** Subscription charges shall be calculated from the applicable subscribed Seat quantity, the annual per-Seat price, and the stated discount. The number of trucks scheduled, appointments completed, doors configured, or rows appearing in an operational report does not replace the Seat-based pricing basis stated in this Agreement.

5.4 **No implied additional order.** A discussion about another facility, a demonstration of a scheduling workflow, or a request to investigate a report does not establish a separately priced purchase. Additional commercial arrangements require a written agreement identifying their scope and applicable charges.

5.5 **Invoice clarity.** Subscription line items shall identify the relevant subscribing entity and make the Seat quantity, annual Seat price, and discount understandable. Descriptions such as “dock software” without the corresponding entity allocation shall not override the Order Schedule.

## 6. Invoicing and Payment

6.1 **Payment terms.** Payment terms are **net 60 days** for Vantorre Group and **net 60 days** for Vantorre Mobility. The period is measured from the invoice date.

6.2 **Invoice particulars.** Pemberline shall identify Pemberline Dock and the subscribing entity on its invoice documentation. Customer shall provide the procurement routing information needed to send invoices to the appropriate recipient. A purchasing-system label using VTG or VMob shall not change the legal entity identified in this Agreement.

6.3 **Administrative discrepancies.** If Customer identifies an incorrect Seat quantity, omitted discount, duplicate line item, or mismatched entity name, Customer shall describe the discrepancy with enough detail for review. Pemberline shall investigate the relevant calculation or record and correct an established invoicing error. An inquiry about one line item shall identify that line item rather than treating unrelated charges as part of the same discrepancy.

6.4 **Payment records.** Customer shall include information sufficient to match a payment to the applicable invoice. Pemberline shall apply payments using that information and address identified allocation errors through its ordinary finance process.

6.5 **Procurement documents.** Purchase-order references and vendor-registration forms may facilitate administration. They do not change the Seat quantities, annual price, discount, payment terms, or other provisions of this Agreement merely by being transmitted with an invoice inquiry.

## 7. Support and Service Levels

7.1 **Support tier.** The support SLA tier is **Tier 1** for Vantorre Group and **Tier 1** for Vantorre Mobility. Support shall be administered under that tier through the support channels and procedures made available for the Service.

7.2 **Request contents.** Customer shall provide information reasonably necessary to investigate a Support Request, including the affected entity, user role, function, approximate occurrence time, and relevant record identifier. For example, a report that appointment AP-1846 remained assigned to Door 7 after a dispatcher selected Door 9 should include the appointment reference and the steps taken.

7.3 **Sensitive material.** Customer shall not include passwords in a Support Request. Screenshots should exclude unrelated driver telephone numbers, personal notes, or records belonging to another operating unit where those details are not needed to reproduce the issue.

7.4 **Investigation.** Pemberline may ask Customer to reproduce an issue, confirm a browser setting, review an access permission, or provide an appropriately limited sample record. Customer shall make a knowledgeable operational contact available for those inquiries.

7.5 **Operational coordination.** If a scheduling issue affects the evening inbound shift, Customer shall identify whether staff are still using the affected screen and whether a workaround is already in use. Such information assists support handling without altering the Tier 1 designation.

## 8. Customer Data and Operational Records

8.1 **Ownership and authority.** Customer retains its rights in Customer Data. Customer is responsible for having authority to collect, submit, and use that data through the Service, including information received from carriers, drivers, contractors, and other participants in Customer’s logistics operations.

8.2 **Permitted processing.** Customer authorizes Pemberline to process Customer Data to provide, secure, maintain, and support the Service and to carry out Customer’s authorized instructions. This authorization includes processing necessary to display appointment calendars, associate records with dock assignments, and investigate reported defects.

8.3 **Record quality.** Customer is responsible for the accuracy of operational entries. A dispatcher who changes an arrival from 14:20 to 15:10 shall ensure that the intended appointment is selected. Pemberline is not responsible for determining whether a carrier reference corresponds to the truck actually standing at Customer’s gate.

8.4 **Exports and reconciliation.** Customer shall review exported records before using them for downstream operational decisions. A daily “Inbound Exceptions” report may require comparison with gate notes where an arrival was recorded manually during a local connectivity interruption.

8.5 **Instructions.** Requests concerning Customer Data shall identify the entity, records, and requested action. Pemberline may seek clarification before acting on an instruction that would affect another entity’s records or remove information needed for an ongoing investigation.

## 9. Security and Access Controls

9.1 **Protective measures.** Pemberline shall maintain administrative, technical, and organizational measures appropriate to protect Customer Data against unauthorized access, use, or disclosure in connection with providing the Service. Customer shall maintain corresponding controls over its devices, users, and local network access.

9.2 **Customer access responsibilities.** Customer shall restrict administrative access to personnel with a business need, remove access when that need ends, and protect authentication information. A shared workstation at the gatehouse shall be managed so that the departing shift does not leave an administrative session available to the arriving shift.

9.3 **Security reporting.** A party that becomes aware of suspected unauthorized access affecting the Service or Customer Data shall report the concern to the other party without undue delay. The report should distinguish observed facts from assumptions and identify relevant accounts, timestamps, and actions already taken.

9.4 **Investigation cooperation.** The parties shall preserve relevant information reasonably available to them and cooperate in investigating the concern. Neither party shall require the other to disclose passwords or introduce a new security weakness as an investigative step.

9.5 **Hosting involvement.** Pemberline may use Carrowmere Cloud in providing hosted infrastructure. Pemberline remains responsible for its obligations under this Agreement and shall manage hosting access consistently with the protection of Customer Data.

## 10. Confidentiality

10.1 **Confidential Information.** Confidential Information means nonpublic information disclosed by a party that is identified as confidential or that reasonably should be understood as confidential from its nature or circumstances. It includes Customer Data, nonpublic Service details, security information, and the commercial provisions of this Agreement.

10.2 **Use and disclosure.** A receiving party shall use Confidential Information only for purposes connected with this Agreement and shall disclose it only to persons who need it for those purposes and are subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations. A screenshot supplied to investigate a dock-calendar issue shall not be circulated as an unrelated sales example.

10.3 **Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that the receiving party can demonstrate was lawfully known without restriction, became public without breach, was lawfully received from another source without a confidentiality duty, or was independently developed without use of the disclosing party’s Confidential Information.

10.4 **Required disclosure.** A receiving party may disclose information when legally required, provided it gives the disclosing party an opportunity to seek protection where legally permitted and limits disclosure to what the requirement calls for.

10.5 **Handling after use.** The parties shall protect retained Confidential Information and dispose of unnecessary working copies appropriately. A printed appointment extract used during troubleshooting shall not remain unattended at a reception counter or shared printer.

## 11. Intellectual Property

11.1 **Pemberline materials.** Pemberline retains its rights in the Service, software, Documentation, interfaces, underlying methods, and improvements. Customer receives only the rights expressly granted by this Agreement.

11.2 **Customer materials.** Customer retains its rights in Customer Data and materials it supplies. A facility diagram showing Door 3, the weighbridge, and the overflow staging lane remains Customer material even when uploaded for configuration assistance.

11.3 **Feedback.** Customer may provide suggestions concerning Service functionality. Pemberline may use feedback to develop or improve the Service, but that permission does not authorize disclosure of Customer Data or Customer’s Confidential Information. A request for clearer appointment-status labels does not transfer Customer’s underlying operational records.

11.4 **Restrictions.** Customer shall not copy or distribute the Service software, remove proprietary notices, or attempt to obtain source code except to the extent applicable law permits notwithstanding this restriction. Ordinary use of supported export functions does not constitute copying the underlying software.

11.5 **Names and marks.** Neither party receives a general license to the other party’s name, logo, or marks. Identifying a party in procurement records, support correspondence, or this Agreement does not authorize a public endorsement or customer announcement.

## 12. Acceptable Use

12.1 **Lawful operation.** Customer shall use the Service for lawful business purposes and shall not submit material that it lacks authority to use. Customer shall ensure that Authorized Users follow this Article.

12.2 **Prohibited conduct.** Customer shall not use the Service to distribute malicious code, interfere with another user’s access, bypass access restrictions, or obtain records outside its authorized scope. Attempts to guess another dispatcher’s credentials or manipulate a request to view an unrelated entity’s appointments are prohibited.

12.3 **Operational entries.** Customer shall distinguish genuine scheduling records from training or diagnostic entries when using supported workflows for those purposes. A practice appointment for “Training Vehicle A” shall not knowingly be presented to gate staff as an actual inbound delivery.

12.4 **Automated activity.** Customer shall use supported methods for integrations and data transfers. It shall not use uncontrolled repeated requests that impair Service operation or attempt to evade safeguards intended to protect the Service.

12.5 **Corrective cooperation.** If Pemberline identifies conduct that appears to violate this Article, the parties shall cooperate to identify the affected user or integration and address the conduct. Customer shall not direct a user to resume a prohibited action through a different login after the issue has been raised.

## 13. Warranties and Operational Responsibilities

13.1 **Authority.** Each party represents that it has authority to enter into and perform this Agreement. Each signatory represents that the signatory is authorized to execute for the party or entities identified in the signature block.

13.2 **Service conformity.** Pemberline warrants that the Service will operate materially in accordance with its Documentation when used as instructed. Customer shall report an alleged material nonconformity with sufficient detail to permit investigation, and Pemberline shall address a substantiated nonconformity through its support and corrective work.

13.3 **Customer inputs.** Customer warrants that it has the necessary rights to provide Customer Data and authorize the processing contemplated here. Customer remains responsible for operational decisions based on the Service.

13.4 **Physical operations.** The Service is a scheduling tool, not a substitute for site safety controls. Customer’s personnel remain responsible for checking trailer position, dock-door readiness, pedestrian clearance, and other physical conditions before directing a vehicle or starting loading work.

13.5 **Limitations.** Pemberline does not warrant that Customer’s carrier will arrive on time, that a trailer will match its booking description, or that a particular dock will be physically available. Except as expressly stated in this Agreement and subject to applicable law, neither party gives an implied warranty concerning a particular operational outcome.

## 14. Indemnities

14.1 **Pemberline indemnity.** Pemberline shall defend Customer against a third-party claim that Customer’s authorized use of the Service infringes that third party’s intellectual property rights and shall indemnify Customer against resulting amounts awarded by a court or agreed in a settlement controlled by Pemberline.

14.2 **Excluded causes.** Pemberline’s obligation does not apply to the extent a claim results from Customer’s unauthorized modification, combination with materials not supplied or authorized by Pemberline, or use outside the rights granted by this Agreement, where the claim would not otherwise have arisen.

14.3 **Customer indemnity.** Customer shall defend Pemberline against a third-party claim arising from Customer Data that Customer lacked authority to submit or from Customer’s unlawful use of the Service, and shall indemnify Pemberline against resulting amounts awarded by a court or agreed in a settlement controlled by Customer.

14.4 **Procedure.** The party seeking indemnification shall promptly inform the other of the claim, provide reasonable cooperation, and permit the indemnifying party to control the defense. Delay relieves the indemnifying party only to the extent the delay materially prejudices the defense.

14.5 **Settlement protections.** An indemnifying party shall not agree to a settlement that admits wrongdoing by the protected party or imposes a nonmonetary obligation on it without its consent. The protected party may participate through its own counsel.

## 15. Limitation of Liability

15.1 **Excluded categories.** Subject to Section 15.3, neither party shall be liable to the other for indirect, incidental, special, punitive, or consequential loss arising from this Agreement, including lost business opportunities or anticipated savings.

15.2 **Operational causation.** A claim concerning a delayed vehicle, missed loading sequence, or disputed appointment shall be assessed according to the actual cause and the obligations in this Agreement. The presence of an appointment in Pemberline Dock does not by itself establish that a Service defect caused the resulting operational loss.

15.3 **Preserved obligations.** The exclusions in this Article do not excuse payment obligations, fraud, willful misconduct, unauthorized use of the other party’s intellectual property, or liabilities that applicable law does not permit the parties to exclude. They do not negate an express indemnification obligation under Article 14.

15.4 **Mitigation and recovery.** Each party shall take reasonable steps to mitigate loss for which it seeks recovery. A party shall not recover the same loss more than once by describing it under separate contractual or legal theories.

15.5 **Evidence.** Relevant evidence may include appointment history, user actions, carrier messages, and local gate records. Neither party shall treat an incomplete screenshot as conclusive where the underlying records provide material context.

## 16. Insurance

16.1 **Coverage responsibility.** Each party shall maintain insurance appropriate to the business activities it performs in connection with this Agreement and to its legal obligations. Insurance does not replace the party’s responsibility to perform those activities properly.

16.2 **Operational distinction.** Customer remains responsible for insurance relating to its facilities, vehicles, personnel, and physical yard operations. Pemberline remains responsible for insurance relating to its provision of the Service. Use of a dock appointment screen does not transfer responsibility for a loading incident to Pemberline.

16.3 **Evidence requests.** On reasonable request, a party shall provide available evidence of relevant coverage, subject to appropriate protection of sensitive policy information. A request routed through a vendor-onboarding portal shall identify the entity requesting the evidence and the purpose of the request.

16.4 **No expansion by certificate.** A certificate or summary of insurance is evidence of coverage, not an amendment of this Agreement. Statements on a procurement checklist do not expand indemnities or alter responsibility for a particular activity.

16.5 **Claims handling.** A party presenting a claim shall preserve relevant records and cooperate with reasonable inquiries concerning the event. Cooperation under this clause does not constitute an admission of liability.

## 17. Suspension and Protective Action

17.1 **Grounds.** Pemberline may suspend affected access where reasonably necessary to address a material security threat, unlawful use, or material breach that cannot safely remain active while being addressed. Protective action shall be directed to the affected access or function where practicable.

17.2 **Communication.** Pemberline shall explain the basis of the action to Customer unless doing so would materially compromise security or violate law. Where feasible, the explanation shall identify the affected users or functionality rather than referring only to a general “access problem.”

17.3 **Customer cooperation.** Customer shall assist in correcting the condition. If an integration repeatedly submits unauthorized requests, Customer shall identify the process owner and stop the affected activity rather than asking gate staff to retry the same process under another user’s credentials.

17.4 **Restoration.** Pemberline shall restore affected access when the basis for suspension has been adequately addressed. The parties shall coordinate any necessary checks, including whether queued actions should be reviewed before users resume scheduling.

17.5 **Preservation.** Suspension does not itself authorize destruction of Customer Data. The parties shall continue to protect relevant records, including records required to investigate the condition that led to protective action.

## 18. Breach and Closure of Access

18.1 **Material breach.** A party alleging a material breach shall provide a written description identifying the relevant obligation and the conduct at issue. The parties shall address a remediable breach through reasonable corrective action before treating the Agreement as terminated for that breach.

18.2 **Serious circumstances.** A party may exercise remedies available under applicable law where the breach cannot reasonably be remedied or continued performance would require unlawful conduct. This Article does not limit the protective measures described in Article 17.

18.3 **Access closure.** When a subscription ends, Customer shall cease use of the affected Service access. Pemberline and Customer shall coordinate authorized handling of Customer Data, including requests concerning available exports and disposition of records.

18.4 **Operational handoff.** Customer shall arrange its own replacement scheduling procedures. For example, the receiving desk should know where to record carrier arrivals if Service access will no longer be used; Pemberline does not assume operation of Customer’s gatehouse.

18.5 **Continuing protections.** Provisions concerning accrued payment obligations, confidentiality, intellectual property, indemnities, liability, and the protection of retained data survive to the extent needed to give them effect. Closure of access does not remove an obligation arising from conduct that occurred while this Agreement applied.

## 19. Election Concerning Continuation

19.1 **Written election.** Either party may elect that this Agreement will not continue into a succeeding period by delivering to the other party a written election at least **90 days** before the close of the then-current period; this rule applies to the subscriptions of both Vantorre Group and Vantorre Mobility.

19.2 **Identification.** The written election shall identify this Agreement and the party making the election. Its routing shall follow Article 21. A request to investigate a invoice discrepancy, disable a user, or remove a dock from a calendar is not such an election.

## 20. Assignment and Service Providers

20.1 **Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party’s written consent, except in connection with a transfer of its business to a successor that assumes the obligations of this Agreement. An assignment shall not relieve a party of obligations arising before the assignment.

20.2 **Affiliate distinction.** An internal reorganization, facility-name change, or revised purchasing code does not by itself change the subscribing entities or Seat allocations. The parties shall document an actual change of contracting entity separately from an administrative label change.

20.3 **Service providers.** Pemberline may engage service providers to assist with hosting, security, and delivery of the Service, subject to obligations consistent with its responsibilities under this Agreement. Pemberline remains responsible for performance of its own contractual obligations.

20.4 **Access boundaries.** Service-provider access shall be limited according to the work being performed. A hosting investigation involving application logs does not authorize unrelated use of Customer’s appointment records.

20.5 **Customer contractors.** Customer may authorize its contractors as users within the applicable subscribed allocations. Customer remains responsible for their access administration and use of the Service. A temporary gate operator shall be subject to the same credential protections as Customer’s employees.

## 21. Notices and Formal Communications

21.1 **Delivery.** Formal communications under this Agreement shall be in writing and directed to the receiving party’s designated contractual recipient through the contact details maintained between the parties. A party changing those details shall inform the other in writing.

21.2 **Customer routing.** Contractual communications for Vantorre Group and Vantorre Mobility shall be directed through Mateo Quiroga, Head of Procurement, using the contractual contact details maintained by the parties. The communication shall identify whether it concerns Vantorre Group, Vantorre Mobility, or both.

21.3 **Pemberline routing.** Contractual communications for Pemberline shall be directed to its contracts function using the contractual contact details maintained by the parties. Sending a message only to a product-support conversation does not substitute for contractual routing.

21.4 **Content and receipt.** A formal communication shall identify this Agreement, its sender, the relevant subject, and the action being communicated. The sender shall retain evidence of delivery. A receiving party shall address a delivery ambiguity in good faith rather than relying on an unrelated message thread.

21.5 **Routine exchanges.** Support tickets, scheduling questions, and invoice-processing correspondence may use their ordinary channels. A subject line such as “Door 5 calendar showing yesterday” should remain a support matter unless the message expressly communicates a contractual action through the appropriate route.

## 22. General Terms

22.1 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement contains the parties’ agreement concerning its subject matter and supersedes prior proposals and discussions concerning that subject matter. The drafting date identifies preparation of the document and does not create a separate service commencement.

22.2 **Amendments.** An amendment must be in writing and agreed by authorized representatives. A dispatcher’s request to add a calendar view, a support agent’s reproduction note, or a procurement-system checkbox does not amend this Agreement.

22.3 **Precedence.** The Order Schedule controls the expressly stated commercial particulars. The remaining articles govern their administration and the parties’ general obligations. Operational examples do not override either.

22.4 **Waiver and severability.** Failure to enforce a provision on one occasion is not a waiver of future enforcement. If a provision is unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue to apply, and the parties shall give effect to the lawful purpose of the affected provision where possible.

22.5 **Independent parties.** The parties are independent contractors. Neither may bind the other in dealings with carriers, drivers, facility operators, or other third parties.

22.6 **Execution.** The Agreement may be executed in counterparts and through electronic signatures. Each executed counterpart forms part of the same Agreement. The signatures below record execution, while Article 1 separately states the effective date.
