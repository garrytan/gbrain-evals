# Master Subscription Agreement: Pellucid Harbor Logistics

**Document Control:** Drafted 10 Feb 2025 | Executed 26 Feb 2025 | Effective 1 Mar 2025

This Master Subscription Agreement (the "Agreement") was initially drafted on 10 Feb 2025 and is entered into and executed on 26 Feb 2025 (the "Execution Date") by and between **Pemberline Systems** ("Provider"), a corporation having its principal place of business at 440 West Foundry Avenue, and **Pellucid Harbor Logistics** ("Customer"), a Gulf Coast port drayage and terminal logistics operator having its principal operational offices at Terminal Berth 9, Galveston Bay. 

Notwithstanding the Execution Date of 26 Feb 2025, the parties expressly agree that the operational and commercial obligations set forth herein shall become effective on 1 Mar 2025 (the "Effective Date").

### 1. Definitions and Scope of Services
**1.1. Hosted Software.** Provider hereby grants to Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable, worldwide right to access and use the cloud-based yard and dock scheduling software platform known as **Pemberline Dock** (the "Service") during the Term, solely for Customer's internal port drayage, chassis staging, and marine terminal gate scheduling operations.

**1.2. Permitted Users and Operational Environment.** A "Seat" is defined as an individual named user credential provisioned within the Customer's Pemberline Dock tenant, whether assigned to a marine gate clerk, harbour dispatch coordinator, yard jockey supervisor, or container berth scheduler. Credentials may not be shared concurrently across separate shifts, such as between the 05:00 early morning gate crew at Pier 14 and the 17:00 twilight container staging shift at the East Turning Basin.

**1.3. Hosting Infrastructure.** Customer acknowledges that the Service is hosted on enterprise cloud infrastructure provided by Provider's third-party subcontractor, Carrowmere Cloud. Provider remains fully responsible for the performance of Carrowmere Cloud in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

### 2. Subscription Schedule and Commercial Terms
**2.1. Subscribed Seats.** Beginning on the Effective Date of 1 Mar 2025, Customer subscribes to a baseline commitment of **130** Seats of Pemberline Dock.

**2.2. Subscription Fees and Discount.** The standard list price for the Service is **1900** USD per Seat per year. Pursuant to commercial approval by Provider's Chief Financial Officer, Anselm Grieve, Customer is granted a contractual discount of **11**% off the standard list price for the duration of the Current Term.

**2.3. Invoicing and Payment Terms.** Provider shall issue the annual subscription invoice upon the Effective Date (1 Mar 2025). All undisputed invoices are payable by Customer on Net **30** days terms from the date of invoice receipt. Remittance shall be made via ACH or wire transfer in United States Dollars to the account designated on the applicable invoice.

### 3. Term and Renewal
**3.1. Current Term.** This Agreement is legally binding upon both parties as of the Execution Date of 26 Feb 2025. The initial subscription term shall commence on the Effective Date of 1 Mar 2025 and shall continue in full force and effect until 28 Feb 2026 (the "End of the Current Term" and "Renewal Date"), unless terminated earlier in accordance with Section 5.

**3.2. Renewal Mechanics.** Prior to the Renewal Date of 28 Feb 2026, the parties shall review Customer's Seat utilization across its Gulf Coast terminal gates and container yards to establish the commercial schedule for any subsequent term.

### 4. Data Ownership and Operational Security
**4.1. Customer Data.** Customer retains all right, title, and interest in and to all electronic data submitted by Customer's personnel to the Service, including drayage carrier SCAC records, TWIC verification logs, tractor license plate reads from Gate Lanes 1 through 8, and bill of lading manifests ("Customer Data").

**4.2. Security and Telemetry.** Provider shall maintain administrative, physical, and technical safeguards designed to protect the security and confidentiality of Customer Data while stored within the Carrowmere Cloud hosting environment.

### 5. Termination for Cause
Either party may terminate this Agreement for cause upon written notice if the other party materially breaches any provision of this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within thirty (30) calendar days of receiving written specification of the breach.
