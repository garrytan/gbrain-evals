# Order Form: Covey & Lark Retail

## 1. Document Identification

This Order Form is entered into by Pemberline Systems and Covey & Lark Retail for the subscription identified below. The initial draft was prepared on 2025-02-27. This executed record was written on 2025-03-12 and signed by both parties on 2025-03-12.

The effective date is 2025-04-01. The date of execution and the effective date are distinct; execution on 2025-03-12 does not change the effective date stated in this Order Form.

## 2. Parties and Product

Pemberline Systems is the supplier of Pemberline Dock, a yard and dock scheduling software product. Covey & Lark Retail is the subscribing customer and is a home goods retailer operating in the Mid-Atlantic.

The product subscribed under this Order Form is Pemberline Dock. References to “Covey” or “CLR” in the associated implementation worksheet identify Covey & Lark Retail. Those abbreviations do not identify a separate contracting party.

## 3. Subscription and Pricing

The subscription established by this Order Form consists of the following:

| Item | Order |
|---|---:|
| Subscribed seats | 42 |
| Price per seat per year, USD | $1,700 |
| Discount | 0% |
| Effective date | 2025-04-01 |
| End of current term / renewal date | 2026-03-31 |

The annual subscription amount calculated from the stated seat quantity and annual seat price is USD $71,400. No discount is applied to that calculation. The subscribed quantity is 42 seats; the operational notes below do not alter that quantity or establish separate subscriptions for individual receiving locations.

## 4. Term Identification

The current subscription term begins on 2025-04-01 and ends on 2026-03-31. For purposes of this Order Form, 2026-03-31 is also the renewal date.

The signature dates record the parties’ execution of this document. They are not substituted for the subscription commencement date, the end of the current term, or the renewal date.

## 5. Operational Record

The implementation worksheet accompanying this order identifies the first receiving schedule to be configured as “Morning Inbound — Home Goods.” The customer’s preparation notes distinguish booked deliveries from unscheduled arrivals and identify dock doors 3 and 4 as the initial examples for configuration review.

The receiving team has requested that appointment descriptions distinguish palletized shelving from boxed lighting fixtures. A sample delivery entry includes a carrier reference, a trailer identifier, an expected arrival window, and a receiving-door assignment. These entries are working examples for configuration and are not additional commercial line items.

The customer’s internal roster worksheet separates receiving supervisors, yard coordinators, and scheduling personnel. Duplicate entries arising from employees appearing on both morning and afternoon shift lists are to be reconciled before the roster is used for setup. The subscribed seat quantity remains as stated in Section 3.
