# Amendment 1 (unsigned): Pellucid Harbor Logistics seat expansion

**Document Status:** Unsigned — For Review Only  
**Preparation Date:** 2025-12-15  
**Reference:** PHL-AMD-001  

This Amendment 1 ("Amendment") to the Master Subscription Agreement is prepared on 2025-12-15 by and between **Pemberline Systems** ("Provider"), a Delaware corporation, and **Pellucid Harbor Logistics** ("Customer"), regarding Customer's licensed use of the Pemberline Dock yard and dock scheduling software platform.

### 1. Purpose and Scope
1.1. The parties desire to modify the existing subscription terms governing Customer's access to Pemberline Dock across its Gulf Coast port drayage and terminal logistics operations, specifically to accommodate expanded gate clerk and yard jockey scheduling across Berth 4, Berth 7, and the East Container Yard at the Port of Mobile facility.

1.2. Except as expressly modified by the numbered sections of this Amendment, all terms, conditions, definitions, and schedules of the underlying Master Subscription Agreement between Provider and Customer shall remain in full force and effect without modification.

### 2. Modification of Subscribed Seats
2.1. Upon full execution of this Amendment by authorized representatives of both parties, Customer's total allotment of subscribed seats for the Pemberline Dock platform shall be amended and set as follows:

* **Subscribed Seats:** 150

2.2. The 150 subscribed seats stated in Section 2.1 represent the total aggregate seat count authorized for Customer's named users across all active terminal profiles, including night-shift dispatchers, inbound drayage coordinators, and chassis yard supervisors. This figure replaces and supersedes the total seat quantity stated in the prior order form.

### 3. Provisioning and Operational Allocation
3.1. Provider shall configure the additional seat capacity within the production tenant (`phl-gulf-prod`) and enable the corresponding user role permissions for the Terminal B gatehouse kiosks and the West Gate Twic-check lanes following receipt of a fully countersigned copy of this Amendment.

3.2. Customer's designated system administrator shall remain responsible for deactivating credentials for seasonal drayage contractors and reassigning named user licenses within the 150 subscribed seats ceiling via the Pemberline Dock administration console.

### 4. Effectiveness and Execution
4.1. This Amendment shall have no binding legal effect unless and until executed by authorized signatories of both Pemberline Systems and Pellucid Harbor Logistics. 

4.2. This instrument may be executed by electronic signature and in one or more counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original when countersigned.
