# Amendment 1: Saltmarsh Provisions retention concession

## 1. Parties and Record Dates

This Amendment 1 is entered into by Pemberline Systems and Saltmarsh Provisions and amends the earlier agreement between those parties concerning Pemberline Dock (the “Agreement”).

This Amendment was drafted on 2025-10-13 and executed by both parties on 2025-10-20. Its effective date is 2025-11-01. The execution date and effective date are distinct; execution does not accelerate the commencement of the concession.

## 2. Retention Concession

Effective 2025-11-01, Pemberline Systems grants Saltmarsh Provisions a retention discount of **10%** on the per-seat annual charges for Pemberline Dock under the Agreement. The discount shall be reflected in the applicable billing calculation as a retention concession.

This Amendment changes the pricing provisions of the earlier paperwork solely to give effect to that discount. It does not establish a new seat quantity or replace the Agreement.

## 3. Continuing Effect

Except as expressly amended herein, the Agreement remains unchanged and in effect. In the event of a conflict concerning the retention discount, this Amendment controls. Operational arrangements for yard arrivals and dock scheduling remain governed by the Agreement.
