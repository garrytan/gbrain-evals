# Renewal Order Form: Driftmoor Marine Freight 2026

## 1. Parties and Record Date

1.1. This Renewal Order Form is entered into by Pemberline Systems and Driftmoor Marine Freight for the renewal of Driftmoor Marine Freight’s subscription to Pemberline Dock, Pemberline Systems’ yard and dock scheduling software.

1.2. This Order Form was drafted on Dec 1st, 2025. The executed record was written and signed on Dec 17th, 2025. Its effective date is Jan 1st, 2026. The execution date and effective date are distinct: execution on Dec 17th does not advance the effective date.

1.3. References below to the “earlier agreement” mean the existing agreement and associated subscription paperwork between Pemberline Systems and Driftmoor Marine Freight. This Order Form changes that earlier paperwork only as expressly stated below.

## 2. Renewal Subscription

2.1. The product covered by this renewal is Pemberline Dock. The subscribed quantity is **58 seats**.

2.2. The price is **USD 1,880 per seat per year**. The annual subscription amount represented by the quantity and price stated in this Order Form is **USD 109,040**.

2.3. The renewal takes effect on **Jan 1st, 2026**. The end of the current term, and the renewal date recorded under this Order Form, is **Dec 31st, 2026**.

2.4. The subscribed quantity is a seat quantity, not a count of dock doors, scheduled appointments, barges, trailers, drivers, or operating shifts. Operational references in this record do not increase or decrease the 58 subscribed seats.

2.5. The quantity, price, effective date, and term-end date shall be entered together in the renewal record so that the commercial summary corresponds to this executed Order Form.

## 3. Amendment of Earlier Paperwork

3.1. This Order Form replaces any inconsistent statement in the earlier agreement concerning the subscribed seat quantity, annual price per seat, effective date of this renewal, or end of the current term.

3.2. The earlier agreement remains the governing agreement for matters not changed by this Order Form. This document does not restate the earlier agreement in full.

3.3. Any operational note, routing instruction, or administrative checklist included below is included to support implementation of the renewal record. Such material does not separately amend the earlier agreement or establish an additional commercial term.

3.4. For purposes of this renewal, references in working files to “DMF” identify Driftmoor Marine Freight. The abbreviated reference is suitable for internal file labels; the full party name shall remain on the executed Order Form and its associated commercial summary.

## 4. Effective-Date Administration

4.1. The signed document shall be retained as the executed renewal instrument dated Dec 17th, 2025. The subscription record shall separately show Jan 1st, 2026 as the effective date.

4.2. The draft dated Dec 1st, 2025 shall remain identifiable as a draft. Any working copy used for review shall not be substituted for the version bearing both signatures.

4.3. The renewal entry shall carry Dec 31st, 2026 in the term-end and renewal-date fields. A calendar entry used to prepare internal work shall not replace that date in the subscription record.

4.4. The commercial summary shall display the seat quantity and annual unit price in separate fields. The annual subscription amount may also be displayed as the arithmetic product of those fields, without replacing either underlying value.

4.5. June Osei-Lark shall complete the executed-file routing and check that the subscription summary matches Section 2 before closing the contract task.

## 5. Operational Context for the Renewal Record

5.1. Driftmoor Marine Freight operates marine freight and barge services in Puget Sound. The operational file accompanying this renewal includes yard arrival planning, dock appointment review, and shift handoff materials used with Pemberline Dock.

5.2. The current handoff packet includes the report titled “Morning Barge Arrival Review.” Its working columns distinguish expected arrival, actual yard arrival, assigned dock door, and the dispatcher’s exception note.

5.3. The dispatch review also includes the Door 4 appointment list. A recurring point of confusion in that list is the difference between a driver checking in at the yard and freight being ready for movement at the dock. The operating note shall keep those events separately labeled.

5.4. These references describe the working context for the renewal. They do not define additional purchased services, extend the subscribed quantity, or change the annual unit price.

5.5. The renewal file shall not treat the number of names appearing in a shift handoff as the subscribed seat count. The applicable subscribed quantity remains the quantity stated in Section 2.

## 6. Seat-Roster Reconciliation Notes

6.1. The administrative review shall compare the active-user export with the dispatch roster used for the morning and evening handoffs. The purpose of the comparison is to identify naming discrepancies before the renewal entry is closed.

6.2. The working roster contains entries labeled “Gate Desk,” “Barge Dispatch,” and “Evening Handoff.” Those labels shall be reviewed against the corresponding user entries rather than counted as separate operational departments for purposes of this Order Form.

6.3. A duplicate display name or an outdated shift label shall be recorded in the cleanup worksheet with the relevant user entry. Corrections to display names shall not be represented as changes to the contracted seat quantity.

6.4. Where an operational contact needs a roster correction, the request shall identify the existing entry and the desired correction. A general request to “fix the night list” is insufficient for the administrative worksheet.

6.5. The completed worksheet shall be stored with the renewal handoff materials, separately from the signed instrument.

## 7. Scheduling Review and Open Work Items

7.1. The operating packet contains a screenshot of two appointments shown against Door 4 during the evening handoff. The dispatcher’s note states that one appointment was moved after a delayed barge arrival, while the printed list still showed the earlier assignment.

7.2. The follow-up review shall compare the appointment history with the printed list and identify which version was used at the gate. The review shall preserve the original screenshot and the associated appointment references.

7.3. The renewal shall not be marked incomplete merely because that operational review remains open. Conversely, execution of this Order Form shall not be recorded as resolution of the scheduling question.

7.4. The handoff note shall distinguish contract administration from product follow-up: the executed renewal belongs in the contract file; the Door 4 review belongs in the scheduling support record.

## 8. Execution and File Completion

8.1. Both parties have signed this Order Form on Dec 17th, 2025. Their signatures record execution on that date, with the renewal becoming effective on Jan 1st, 2026.

8.2. The final file shall contain this signed Order Form, the commercial summary reflecting Section 2, and the administrative handoff worksheet. Operational attachments shall remain identifiable by their own titles.

8.3. Before filing, the contract task shall be checked for the correct party names, 58 subscribed seats, USD 1,880 annual price per seat, and Dec 31st, 2026 term-end date.

8.4. The executed copy shall be returned to Driftmoor Marine Freight and retained by Pemberline Systems.
