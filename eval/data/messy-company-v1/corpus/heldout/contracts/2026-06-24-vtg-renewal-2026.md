# Renewal Order Form: Vantorre Group 2026

## 1. Parties and Subject Matter

This Renewal Order Form is entered into by Pemberline Systems and Vantorre Group and its affiliate, Vantorre Mobility, for the renewal of Pemberline Dock, the yard and dock scheduling software supplied by Pemberline Systems. Vantorre Mobility is a subsidiary of Vantorre Group. References in this Order Form to the customer parties mean Vantorre Group and Vantorre Mobility together, except where a provision expressly applies to only one entity.

This Order Form modifies the earlier agreement for this customer with respect to the renewal particulars expressly set out below.

## 2. Preparation, Execution, and Effectiveness

This Order Form was drafted on 2026-06-08. The completed execution record was written on 2026-06-24, and the parties signed this Order Form on 2026-06-24, as shown in Section 9.

The effective date of this Order Form is 2026-07-01. The signature date and effective date are distinct: execution on 2026-06-24 records the parties’ agreement, while the renewal particulars specified herein take effect on 2026-07-01. The preparation date does not replace either the execution date or the effective date.

## 3. Subscribed Seats

The subscribed seat count for Vantorre Group is **280 seats**.

That count applies to Vantorre Group. It is not stated as a combined allocation for Vantorre Group and Vantorre Mobility. The separate inclusion of Vantorre Mobility as a party to this Order Form does not alter the entity to which the stated seat count applies.

For entry into the renewal record, the quantity field shall identify Vantorre Group by its full legal name and shall show 280 subscribed seats.

## 4. Price Per Seat Per Year

The price for Pemberline Dock is **USD 2,000 per seat per year**.

This price applies to both Vantorre Group and Vantorre Mobility. The annual per-seat price shall therefore be recorded against each entity under its respective name. The common price does not convert the Vantorre Group seat quantity in Section 3 into a shared quantity.

The pricing description shall remain “per seat per year” in the renewal record so that the annual unit price is distinguishable from a seat quantity or an aggregate amount.

## 5. Current Term End and Renewal Date

The end of the current term, identified for this Order Form as the renewal date, is **2027-06-30**.

This date applies to both Vantorre Group and Vantorre Mobility. The renewal record for each entity shall show the same term-end date. Neither the drafting date nor the signature date shall be entered in the term-end field.

The effective date specified in Section 2 remains 2026-07-01 and is separate from the current term end specified in this section.

## 6. Relationship to Earlier Paperwork

This Order Form changes the earlier agreement for this customer as to the subscribed seats, annual per-seat price, effective date, and current term end expressly stated herein, subject to the entity-specific application of each provision.

Where earlier paperwork contains a different entry for any of those particulars, this Order Form supplies the replacement entry for the applicable entity. Its inclusion of both customer parties shall not be read to erase the distinctions expressly made in Sections 3 through 5.

## 7. Administrative Record

The executed copy shall be filed under “Renewal Order Form: Vantorre Group 2026.” The renewal worksheet shall retain separate rows for Vantorre Group and Vantorre Mobility rather than a single abbreviated customer row.

For the filing check, the working packet contains the signature page, the renewal worksheet, and the earlier agreement reference. The worksheet’s review notes identify a morning receiving-shift roster and a dock-door assignment export as operational materials. Those materials concern scheduling administration; they do not replace the subscribed seat count, price, or dates stated in this Order Form.

## 8. Execution Record

The signatures below record execution by Pemberline Systems and by the customer signatory for Vantorre Group and Vantorre Mobility on 2026-06-24.
