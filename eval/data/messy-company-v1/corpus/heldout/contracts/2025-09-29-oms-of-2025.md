# Order Form: Orlavine Medical Supply

## 1. Document Identification

**Drafted:** 9/15/25  
**Written and executed:** 9/29/25  
**Effective date:** 10/1/25

This Order Form records the subscription order between Pemberline Systems and Orlavine Medical Supply for Pemberline Dock, a yard and dock scheduling software product. The execution date and the effective date are separate dates. Both parties signed this Order Form on 9/29/25; the subscription described herein becomes effective on 10/1/25.

The administrative account abbreviation for Orlavine Medical Supply is **OMS**. The full customer name, rather than the abbreviation alone, shall appear on the executed order record.

## 2. Parties

**Provider:** Pemberline Systems.

**Customer:** Orlavine Medical Supply, a medical supply distributor operating in the Mountain West.

References to “Provider” and “Customer” in this Order Form mean the entities identified in this section. References to receiving personnel, dispatch personnel, site coordinators, or other operational users describe functions within Customer’s scheduling activities and do not identify additional contracting parties.

The Customer named in this Order Form is Orlavine Medical Supply. Orlavene Health is a separate hospital group and is not a party to this Order Form.

## 3. Subscription Ordered

Customer subscribes to **50 seats** of Pemberline Dock.

The subscribed product is intended for yard and dock scheduling. Customer’s implementation worksheet identifies receiving appointments, outbound staging appointments, and gate-arrival review as the initial operational uses. The worksheet uses separate labels for “Scheduled Arrival” and “Actual Gate Arrival” so that receiving personnel can distinguish an appointment time from a driver’s recorded arrival.

Those worksheet entries provide operational context for setup. They do not change the subscribed product or the number of seats ordered. A dock-door label, appointment category, carrier record, or report heading is not a separate subscription quantity under this Order Form.

## 4. Price and Discount

The price per subscribed seat per year is **USD 1,900**.

The discount applicable to this order is **5%**.

The stated seat quantity, annual per-seat price, and discount are the commercial entries for this subscription. Internal setup worksheets and correspondence concerning receiving shifts shall reproduce those entries without alteration when referring to this order.

Operational references to appointment volume, dock-door usage, or the number of trucks expected during a receiving window do not replace the subscribed-seat quantity. In particular, the receiving worksheet’s count of dock doors is a configuration reference and is not the basis for the subscription price.

## 5. Current Term and Renewal Date

The subscription’s effective date is **10/1/25**.

The end of the current term, recorded as the renewal date, is **9/30/26**.

The signed order record shall retain both dates in their respective fields. The execution date of 9/29/25 shall not be entered in place of the effective date. Likewise, the drafting date of 9/15/25 shall remain a document-preparation date and shall not be used as the start of the subscription.

For the receiving-team handoff, the order reference sheet shall display “Effective: 10/1/25” and “Current term ends / renewal date: 9/30/26.” Calendar entries used for internal preparation shall remain separate from these subscription dates.

## 6. Operational Setup Record

Customer’s receiving supervisor, Tessa Vale, has supplied a preliminary door list for the Mesa Receiving site. The list identifies Door 2 for parcel consolidation, Door 4 for palletized medical supplies, and Door 6 for outbound staging. These descriptions are setup labels, not additions to the commercial terms.

The appointment template shall distinguish the morning receiving shift from afternoon outbound staging. Customer’s dispatch coordinator, Nolan Beck, is preparing the carrier-name worksheet and checking duplicate entries for carriers that appear under both an abbreviated name and a full dispatch-office name.

The proposed “Daily Door Queue” report shall show the appointment reference, carrier name, scheduled arrival, assigned door, and arrival status. A sample appointment, OMS-TEST-014, is marked as a training entry so that receiving personnel do not mistake it for an actual inbound load.

These details are included to make the initial handoff usable by the personnel preparing the scheduling workspace. They do not establish additional seats, prices, discounts, or service commitments.

## 7. Execution and Administrative Handoff

Before the operational handoff, Customer’s setup worksheet shall be checked for legible door names and consistent shift labels. Tessa Vale is to resolve the worksheet entry currently labeled “rear receiving” by identifying the corresponding numbered door. Nolan Beck is to remove the duplicate carrier entry from the sample appointment list.

The executed copy shall be retained with the order record. The receiving-team packet shall contain the door worksheet and sample report layout separately, allowing operational corrections without replacing this signed Order Form.

The signatures below record execution by both parties on 9/29/25. Execution does not alter the effective date of 10/1/25 or the current-term end and renewal date of 9/30/26.
