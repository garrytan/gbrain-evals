# Pellow Home Living renewal order form v2

**DRAFT — NOT SIGNED**

**Drafted: March 9, 2026**

## 1. Parties and Subject Matter

This draft renewal order form identifies **Pemberline Systems** as the supplier and **Pellow Home Living** as the customer. The subject matter is the renewal of the customer’s subscription to **Pemberline Dock**, Pemberline Systems’ yard and dock scheduling software.

This document is version 2 of the proposed renewal order form. It is a draft only and has never been signed. The signature spaces below remain blank. Its preparation date is not an execution date or the commencement date of a renewal term.

## 2. Customer Identification

Pellow Home Living is a home goods retailer operating in the Mid-Atlantic. The customer was renamed from **Covey & Lark Retail** on **February 16, 2026**. References to Covey & Lark Retail in historical receiving records concern the former customer name.

For purposes of this draft, the customer name is Pellow Home Living. The former name is included solely to assist personnel reviewing receiving schedules, appointment exports, and other materials generated before the name change. It is not the name presented for customer execution.

## 3. Subscription Quantity

The subscribed seat quantity set by this draft renewal order form is **50 seats**.

The quantity is expressed as a total subscription quantity, not as a count of dock doors, distribution sites, carrier companies, appointment slots, or daily vehicle movements. For example, a receiving schedule containing appointments at Doors 3, 4, and 5 does not establish three additional seats. Likewise, the presence of a carrier name in an appointment record does not itself identify that carrier as a subscribed user.

## 4. Product and Pricing Basis

The product identified in this draft is Pemberline Dock. Pemberline Systems prices Pemberline Dock per seat per year.

The annual, per-seat pricing basis describes the product’s commercial model; it does not convert the preparation date of this draft into a subscription anniversary. Operational references below describe anticipated scheduling activity and do not establish a separate product purchase. In particular, discussion of receiving lanes, furniture unloading, and appointment reports concerns the use of Pemberline Dock rather than an additional software offering.

## 5. Receiving Operations Context

The renewal review concerns the use of Pemberline Dock by receiving personnel coordinating inbound home goods deliveries. The working operations example is a morning receiving board showing upholstered furniture at Door 4, flat-packed shelving at Door 5, and a mixed housewares trailer awaiting a door assignment.

The receiving desk uses the board to distinguish a booked arrival from a vehicle physically present in the yard. Those statuses are operationally different: a carrier’s appointment may remain on the schedule even when the driver has not yet checked in.

## 6. Appointment Review

The customer’s receiving supervisor has requested that the morning appointment view remain readable during the shift handover. The example under review involves two furniture trailers booked close together, with one driver reporting an earlier arrival than the dispatch estimate.

The practical review will examine the appointment time, check-in entry, assigned door, and receiving notes as separate items. The purpose is to avoid treating a changed arrival estimate as evidence that unloading has begun. The proposed subscription quantity remains 50 seats irrespective of the number of appointments displayed during that review.

## 7. Named-User Working List

The operational working list includes receiving desk staff, shift supervisors, and personnel who reconcile the prior day’s arrivals against the appointment board. The customer’s scheduling coordinator is assembling that list from the current shift roster.

The coordinator will distinguish personnel who actively use Pemberline Dock from employees merely mentioned in unloading instructions. A note stating “ask the floor lead before opening Door 5” is an instruction within a receiving record; it is not, by itself, a request to add the floor lead to the working list of users.

## 8. Reporting Review

The report selected for the operational walkthrough is the **Morning Arrival Exceptions** export. The walkthrough will use a receiving day containing an early arrival, a late arrival, and a trailer whose door assignment changed after check-in.

The receiving analyst will compare the exported rows with the corresponding appointment entries. Particular attention will be given to the customer name appearing in the report heading and to notes retained from the period before the name change. Historical text containing Covey & Lark Retail may explain an older entry without changing the customer identified in Section 1.

## 9. Operational Follow-Up

The receiving desk will provide a sample appointment showing the difference between a planned arrival and an actual check-in. The shift supervisor will provide the handover view used before the first furniture delivery is unloaded.

The scheduling coordinator will review the user working list alongside those examples. These activities concern the practical preparation for the renewal discussion. They do not constitute customer signature, supplier signature, or execution of this draft. No completed operational walkthrough shall be represented as acceptance of the order form.

## 10. Version and Draft Status

This document bears the designation “v2” to distinguish it from the earlier working version of the renewal order form. Its subscribed seat quantity is 50.

The draft designation applies to the entire document, including the subscription quantity and signature block. Neither a printed copy placed at the receiving desk nor a copy circulated with the Morning Arrival Exceptions export constitutes a signed order. This draft remains unsigned; both execution lines are intentionally blank.
