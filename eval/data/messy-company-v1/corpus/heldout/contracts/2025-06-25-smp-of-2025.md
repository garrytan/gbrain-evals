# Order Form: Saltmarsh Provisions

**Document Reference:** OF-SMP-2025-06  
**Drafted Date:** 2025-06-09  
**Execution Date:** 2025-06-25  
**Effective Date:** 2025-07-01  

This Order Form ("Order Form") is entered into by and between **Pemberline Systems** ("Provider") and **Saltmarsh Provisions** ("Customer," internal account code SMP). Although executed by the parties on the Execution Date of 2025-06-25, the subscription rights and obligations set forth herein shall commence on the Effective Date of 2025-07-01.

### 1. Subscription Services and Scope
1.1 **Software:** Provider grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to access and use the Pemberline Dock yard and dock scheduling software platform during the Order Form Term.  
1.2 **Operational Deployment:** The provisioned environment shall be configured to support Customer’s Lowcountry regional grocery distribution centers, including the dry goods receiving bays (Dock Doors 1 through 14) and the refrigerated cross-docking apron at the Beaufort depot, as well as the carrier check-in kiosk at the North Charleston cold storage annex.

### 2. Commercial Terms and Pricing
2.1 **Subscribed Seats:** 70 seats.  
2.2 **Standard List Rate:** $1,780 USD per seat per year.  
2.3 **Applicable Discount:** 7% off the Standard List Rate.  
2.4 **Fee Calculation:** Fees are calculated on an annual per-seat basis in accordance with the subscribed seat count, list rate, and discount stated in Sections 2.1 through 2.3 above.

### 3. Term and Renewal
3.1 **Order Form Term:** The subscription term governed by this Order Form commences on the Effective Date of 2025-07-01 and continues through the end of the current term (renewal date) on 2026-06-30, at 11:59 PM Eastern Time.

### 4. Administrative Contacts
4.1 **Customer Operational Notices:** Corinne Ashdown, VP Operations, Saltmarsh Provisions (routing: Beaufort main gate house administration office).  
4.2 **Billing and Provisioning:** All seat provisioning tickets (referencing onboarding batch #SMP-8841 for third-shift yard spotters and daytime dispatchers) shall be directed to Provider’s Deal Desk upon execution.
