## 1. Parties and Background

**1.1 Identification of Parties.** This Renewal Order Form (the "Renewal Order Form") is entered into by and between Pemberline Systems ("Pemberline") and Orlavine Medical Supply ("Customer," and together with Pemberline, the "Parties"). This document was initially drafted on 9/8/26, is executed by the authorized representatives of both Parties on 9/24/26 (the "Execution Date"), and shall take commercial and legal effect on 10/1/26 (the "Effective Date"). For clarity, the Execution Date of 9/24/26 governs the binding commitment of the Parties hereto, whereas the Effective Date of 10/1/26 marks the commencement of the renewal subscription period described herein.

**1.2 Reference to Governing Agreement.** This Renewal Order Form is governed by and incorporated into the existing master subscription agreement and prior ordering documentation previously entered into between Pemberline and Customer (collectively, the "Prior Agreement"). This Renewal Order Form amends, supersedes, and replaces the applicable commercial terms of the Prior Agreement solely with respect to the renewal period and per-seat pricing set forth below. Except as expressly modified by the terms of this Renewal Order Form, all terms, conditions, exhibits, and operational schedules of the Prior Agreement shall remain in full force and effect. In the event of any conflict or inconsistency between the provisions of the Prior Agreement and the provisions of this Renewal Order Form, the provisions of this Renewal Order Form shall control with respect to the subject matter hereof.

## 2. Subscription Scope and Operational Deployment

**2.1 Licensed Software.** Subject to the terms of the Prior Agreement and this Renewal Order Form, Pemberline grants to Customer a continuing right to access and use Pemberline Dock, Pemberline's proprietary yard and dock scheduling software platform (the "Service"). Access to the Service is licensed on a named-seat basis per year. Each individual user credential assigned to Customer personnel—including gate security officers, cold-chain receiving clerks, yard spotter dispatchers, and regional warehouse supervisors—constitutes a single seat assignment under Customer's production instance (`oms-mtn-prod`).

**2.2 Covered Facilities and Operational Environment.** Customer's deployment of the Service under this Renewal Order Form encompasses Customer's medical supply distribution operations across the Mountain West region. Specifically, the production configuration maintained under this Renewal Order Form supports:
*   **Salt Lake North Distribution Center (West Valley City, UT):** Inbound and outbound scheduling for Doors 1 through 28, including the dedicated temperature-controlled biologics receiving bays (Doors 14 through 19) and the sterile surgical kit staging yard (Yard Zone B).
*   **Boise Sterile Annex (Meridian, ID):** Dock scheduling for Doors 1 through 10, cross-dock transfer lanes, and drop-trailer lot tracking for regional courier fleets.
*   **Denver Regional Hub (Aurora, CO):** High-turn parcel and LTL freight doors (Doors 1 through 22), carrier self-scheduling portal access for third-party medical freight carriers, and automated gate kiosk check-in workflows.

**2.3 Technical Configuration Continuity.** The custom configuration parameters established during prior operational periods—specifically the automated detention alert rules associated with the `Cold-Chain-Dwell-Summary-v3` report and the webhook integration linking Pemberline Dock to Customer's warehouse management system (reference Engineering Ticket `PD-44918`, resolved following the August gate-house scanner firmware update at the Salt Lake North facility)—shall carry forward without interruption upon the Effective Date of 10/1/26.

## 3. Commercial Terms and Pricing

**3.1 Per-Seat Subscription Rate.** Commencing on the Effective Date of 10/1/26 and continuing through the expiration of the Renewal Term defined in Section 4.1, the subscription fee for Pemberline Dock under this Renewal Order Form shall be **USD $1,960 per seat per year**. This rate supersedes the per-seat rate set forth in the Prior Agreement.

**3.2 Seat Reallocation and Administrative Controls.** Customer's designated system administrators at the Salt Lake North, Boise, and Denver facilities may reassign active seat credentials among bona fide employees and onsite contractors when shift rotations or personnel changes occur (such as seasonal adjustments between the first-shift receiving crew and the third-shift hospital courier loading team), provided that the total number of concurrent active named seats does not exceed Customer's contracted seat allocation, and provided further that single seat credentials are not shared simultaneously across multiple individuals or terminal workstations at the dock doors.

**3.3 Invoicing and Remittance.** Invoices issued pursuant to this Renewal Order Form shall reference Customer Vendor Code `OMS-VEND-8841` and shall be transmitted electronically to Customer's accounts payable department in accordance with the billing procedures established under the Prior Agreement. All monetary amounts stated in this Renewal Order Form are denominated in United States Dollars (USD) and are exclusive of applicable state, local, or municipal sales and use taxes imposed by taxing authorities in Utah, Idaho, or Colorado.

## 4. Term and Renewal Schedule

**4.1 Renewal Term.** Notwithstanding the Execution Date of 9/24/26, the renewal term governed by this Renewal Order Form shall commence at 12:00 a.m. Mountain Time on **10/1/26** (the Effective Date) and shall continue in full force and effect until 11:59 p.m. Mountain Time on **9/30/27** (the "Renewal Date" and end of the current term), unless earlier terminated in accordance with the provisions of the Prior Agreement.

**4.2 Transition from Prior Term.** The Parties acknowledge that the subscription period under the Prior Agreement remains active through the close of business on 9/30/26. There shall be no lapse in production service availability, carrier portal access, or historical dock audit log retention during the transition between the expiration of the prior period and the Effective Date of 10/1/26.
