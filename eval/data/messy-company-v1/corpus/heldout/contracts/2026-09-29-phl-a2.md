# Amendment 2: Pellucid Harbor Logistics pricing

**Document Status:** Executed  
**Drafted Date:** September 14, 2026  
**Execution Date:** September 29, 2026  
**Amendment Effective Date:** March 1, 2027  

This Amendment 2 ("Amendment") is drafted as of September 14, 2026, and is entered into and executed on September 29, 2026 (the "Execution Date"), by and between Pemberline Systems ("Pemberline"), having its principal place of business at 4100 East Marginal Way, and Pellucid Harbor Logistics ("Customer"), having its principal operational offices at Terminal 4, Berth 12, Port of Galveston, Texas. Pemberline and Customer may each be referred to herein individually as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."

## 1. Recitals and Background

**1.1.** Whereas, the Parties previously entered into that certain master subscription agreement and associated order forms (collectively, the "Prior Agreement") pursuant to which Pemberline grants Customer access to and use of Pemberline Dock, a proprietary yard and dock scheduling software platform.

**1.2.** Whereas, Customer utilizes Pemberline Dock across its Gulf Coast port drayage and terminal logistics operations, including without limitation the scheduling of container chassis turnarounds at West Yard Gate 3, reefer plug-in queue management along Doors 14 through 28, and third-shift dispatcher handoffs for inbound coastal vessels.

**1.3.** Whereas, the Parties now desire to modify and amend certain commercial pricing and discount provisions set forth in the Prior Agreement, specifically to establish revised per-seat annual pricing and an updated commercial discount percentage to govern Customer's subscription commencing on the Amendment Effective Date defined below.

**1.4.** Now, therefore, in consideration of the mutual covenants, terms, and conditions set forth herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 2. Definitions and Interpretation

**2.1.** Capitalized terms used but not otherwise defined in this Amendment shall have the meanings ascribed to such terms in the Prior Agreement.

**2.2.** "Seat" means an individual named user credential provisioned within the Pemberline Dock production environment for use by Customer's gate clerks, yard jockey coordinators, drayage dispatchers, or terminal supervisors, provided that credentials may not be shared concurrently across multiple individuals on the same shift.

**2.3.** In the event of any conflict, inconsistency, or ambiguity between the terms and conditions of this Amendment and the terms and conditions of the Prior Agreement, the terms and conditions of this Amendment shall control and prevail solely with respect to the subject matter hereof.

## 3. Effective Date vs. Execution Date

**3.1.** The Parties expressly acknowledge and agree that although this Amendment is signed and executed by both Parties on September 29, 2026 (the "Execution Date"), the commercial modifications set forth in Section 4 of this Amendment shall not take effect until March 1, 2027 (the "Amendment Effective Date").

**3.2.** During the interim period between the Execution Date of September 29, 2026, and the Amendment Effective Date of March 1, 2027, the pricing and fee schedules established under the Prior Agreement shall remain in full force and effect without modification.

## 4. Amended Commercial Terms

**4.1.** **Price Per Seat Per Year.** Commencing on the Amendment Effective Date of March 1, 2027, the Prior Agreement is hereby amended such that the net subscription fee for Pemberline Dock shall be Two Thousand Ten United States Dollars ($2,010 USD) per Seat per year.

**4.2.** **Commercial Discount.** The Parties acknowledge and record that the pricing established in Section 4.1 reflects an applied commercial discount of nine percent (9%). This 9% discount supersedes and replaces any prior promotional rate, volume tier reduction, or legacy discount schedule previously set forth in the Prior Agreement.

**4.3.** **Currency and Invoicing.** All monetary amounts stated in this Amendment are denominated in United States Dollars (USD). Invoices referencing the amended pricing set forth in this Section 4 shall be directed to Customer's accounts payable registry at the Port of Galveston terminal office, referencing vendor portal routing code `PHL-AP-DOCK-02`, and shall be issued in accordance with the billing schedule governed by the Prior Agreement.

## 5. Operational Scope and Environment Reference

**5.1.** The pricing set forth in Section 4 applies to Customer's existing production tenant (`phl-gulf-prod-01`) hosted via Pemberline's designated cloud infrastructure provider, including standard access to the Gatehouse Check-In Module, the Automated Bolster & Chassis Manifest export (`Report_ID: PHL_DRAY_404`), and the TWIC badge verification log fields configured under implementation ticket `DOCK-8819`.

**5.2.** Nothing in this Amendment shall be construed to alter Customer's obligations regarding gate hardware maintenance, handheld scanner connectivity at Berth 12, or local network availability at Customer's Gulf Coast facilities.

## 6. Ratification and Miscellaneous

**6.1.** **No Other Modifications.** Except as expressly modified and amended by this Amendment, all terms, covenants, representations, and conditions of the Prior Agreement remain unchanged and are hereby ratified, confirmed, and continued in full force and effect.

**6.2.** **Entire Agreement.** This Amendment, together with the Prior Agreement which it amends, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous oral or written communications, proposals, or negotiations relating to the pricing adjustment drafted on September 14, 2026.

**6.3.** **Counterparts and Electronic Signatures.** This Amendment may be executed in two or more counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, but all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Signatures transmitted electronically or by digital signature platform shall be deemed original signatures for all legal purposes.
