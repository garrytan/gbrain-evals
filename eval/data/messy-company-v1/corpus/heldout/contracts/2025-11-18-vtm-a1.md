# Affiliate Amendment: Vantorre Mobility

This Affiliate Amendment (drafted 2025-11-03 and signed on 2025-11-18) is entered into by and between Pemberline Systems and Vantorre Mobility, and modifies the earlier agreement governing Vantorre Mobility's use of Pemberline Dock.

1. **Effective Date.** Although executed on 2025-11-18, this Amendment shall take effect on the Effective Date of 2025-12-01.

2. **Amended Commercial Terms.** As of the Effective Date, the following terms are updated for Vantorre Mobility:
   - **Subscribed Seats:** 48
   - **Support SLA Tier:** Tier 1 Plus

3. **Ratification.** Only the terms expressly listed in Section 2 above change for Vantorre Mobility; every other term of the parent agreement continues to apply to Vantorre Mobility in full force and effect.
