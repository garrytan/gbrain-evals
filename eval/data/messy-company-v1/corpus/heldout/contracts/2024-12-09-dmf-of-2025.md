# Order Form: Driftmoor Marine Freight 2025

## 1. Parties and Identification

This Order Form is entered into by Pemberline Systems (“Pemberline”) and Driftmoor Marine Freight (“Customer”) for a subscription to Pemberline Dock, Pemberline’s yard and dock scheduling software.

Customer is a marine freight and barge operator serving Puget Sound. The customer abbreviation “DMF” may be used in internal order-routing records; the customer name for this Order Form, the subscription record, and related billing records is Driftmoor Marine Freight.

This Order Form was drafted on 11/25/24. The execution record set forth below was completed on 12/9/24. June Osei-Lark, Deal Desk and Contracts Manager for Pemberline, prepared this executed record on 12/9/24.

## 2. Execution Date and Effective Date

The parties signed this Order Form on **12/9/24**. The subscription effective date is **1/1/25**.

The execution date and effective date are separate dates. Execution on 12/9/24 records the parties’ acceptance of this Order Form; it does not change the subscription effective date to 12/9/24.

For order-entry purposes, the signature date shall be recorded in the execution field and 1/1/25 shall be recorded in the subscription effective-date field. The subscription shall not be entered as commencing in December merely because the parties completed signatures during that month.

## 3. Subscription Ordered

Customer subscribes to **55 seats** of Pemberline Dock. The subscription is priced per seat per year in United States dollars.

| Order field | Agreed value |
|---|---:|
| Product | Pemberline Dock |
| Subscribed seats | 55 |
| Price per seat per year, USD | $1,820.00 |
| Discount | 6% |
| Effective date | 1/1/25 |
| End of current term / renewal date | 12/31/25 |

The seat quantity entered in the subscription record shall be 55. Operational descriptions, shift rosters, and user-assignment worksheets accompanying the order are administrative materials and do not replace the quantity stated in this section.

## 4. Price Calculation

The annual subscription calculation for the stated seat quantity is as follows:

| Calculation | Amount, USD |
|---|---:|
| 55 seats × $1,820.00 per seat per year | $100,100.00 |
| Less 6% discount | ($6,006.00) |
| Net annual subscription amount | **$94,094.00** |

The discount is applied to the aggregate annual seat price shown above. The resulting discounted annual price per seat is $1,710.80.

The pricing worksheet and order-entry summary shall use the same seat quantity, annual unit price, and discount percentage. In particular, the undiscounted annual amount shall remain visible as the calculation base rather than being replaced with the net amount in the unit-price field.

## 5. Current Term and Renewal Record

The current subscription term commences on **1/1/25** and ends on **12/31/25**. The end-of-current-term date, identified in the order record as the renewal date, is **12/31/25**.

The date 12/31/25 shall be entered consistently in the order summary and renewal calendar. The renewal calendar entry should identify the product as Pemberline Dock and the customer as Driftmoor Marine Freight so that it can be distinguished from Customer’s ordinary berth, vessel, and dispatch calendar entries.

This Order Form records the dates and commercial amounts stated herein. It does not set out a separate schedule of renewal pricing.

## 6. Operational Reference Materials

The operational handoff accompanying this order concerns Customer’s Puget Sound freight movements and the coordination of yard arrivals with dock availability. The working materials include a morning dispatch roster, a dock-door reference sheet, and a sample appointment report titled “Daily Barge Transfer Queue.”

For preparation of the handoff packet, Customer’s dispatch staff will mark the difference between a vessel movement reference and a truck appointment reference. A barge arrival noted in the dispatch sheet should not be copied into a truck appointment field solely because both entries carry the same shipment reference.

The dock-door reference sheet will retain the local labels “Door 2” and “Door 4.” The morning shift worksheet will identify the dispatcher reviewing the queue and the person checking arriving drivers against the day’s appointment list. These references describe the working materials for the handoff; they do not alter the subscribed seat quantity or price.

## 7. Order Administration

The executed copy shall be filed under “Order Form: Driftmoor Marine Freight 2025.” The accompanying order-entry worksheet shall show the execution date, effective date, seat quantity, pricing calculation, and current-term end date in separate fields.

Before the handoff packet is circulated, the preparer shall remove duplicate copies of the dock-door reference sheet and retain the version bearing the corrected Door 4 label. The sample appointment report shall remain labeled as a sample so that dispatch personnel do not mistake its entries for scheduled freight movements.

Questions arising from a mismatch between the order-entry worksheet and this signed Order Form shall be returned to June Osei-Lark for correction of the worksheet. The signed Order Form shall remain with the execution record.
