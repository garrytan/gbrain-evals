# Renewal Order Form: Pellow Home Living (formerly Covey & Lark Retail)

**Document Reference:** ROF-PHL-2026-04
**Drafted Date:** 2026-03-16
**Execution Date:** 2026-03-25
**Renewal Effective Date:** 2026-04-01

This Renewal Order Form ("Order Form") is entered into by and between **Pemberline Systems** ("Provider") and **Pellow Home Living** ("Customer"), formerly known as **Covey & Lark Retail** prior to its corporate name change on 2026-02-16. This Order Form amends and supersedes the commercial terms of the prior subscription agreement and earlier ordering documentation between the parties.

### 1. Precedence and Effect
1.1. Although executed by both parties on the Execution Date of 2026-03-25, the subscription renewal term and associated pricing governed by this Order Form shall not commence until the Renewal Effective Date of 2026-04-01.
1.2. Except as expressly modified herein, all underlying terms, conditions, and operational schedules set forth in the earlier agreement between Provider and Customer remain in full force and effect. Any reference to Covey & Lark Retail in earlier paperwork shall hereinafter be deemed a reference to Pellow Home Living.

### 2. Subscription Scope and Commercial Terms
2.1. **Product:** Pemberline Dock (yard and dock scheduling software).
2.2. **Subscribed Seats:** 46 seats.
2.3. **Price per Seat per Year:** $1,760.00 USD.
2.4. **Total Annual Subscription Fee:** $80,960.00 USD.
2.5. **Term and Renewal Date:** The renewal term governed by this Order Form commences on the Renewal Effective Date of 2026-04-01 and continues through the end of the current term (renewal date) on 2027-03-31.

### 3. Operational Deployment and Administration
3.1. The 46 subscribed seats allocated under Section 2.2 are authorized for deployment across Customer's Mid-Atlantic regional distribution centers, including the primary receiving clerks at the Hagerstown cross-dock (Doors 1 through 24) and the second-shift yard spotters at the Fredericksburg upholstery annex.
3.2. Provider's platform engineering team shall complete the tenant alias migration from the legacy `covey-lark-prod` environment identifier to `pellow-home-prod` during the scheduled maintenance window on Sunday, 2026-03-29, pursuant to internal support ticket DOCK-7741. Automated carrier appointment confirmation emails and the daily *Inbound Container Detention Report* shall display the Pellow Home Living trade name upon completion of the migration.
3.3. Customer's designated billing contact shall submit all remittance inquiries referencing the updated legal entity name.
