# Renewal Order Form: Pellucid Harbor Logistics 2026

**Document Status:** Executed  
**Drafted Date:** 2026-02-02  
**Execution Date:** 2026-02-19  
**Renewal Effective Date:** 2026-03-01  

This Renewal Order Form ("Order Form") is entered into by and between Pemberline Systems ("Provider") and Pellucid Harbor Logistics ("Customer"), collectively referred to herein as the "Parties." This Order Form was initially drafted on 2026-02-02, is executed by the Parties on 2026-02-19 ("Execution Date"), and shall become legally effective on 2026-03-01 ("Renewal Effective Date"). 

## 1. Reference to Prior Agreement

1.1. This Order Form amends, modifies, and supersedes the commercial terms of the prior subscription agreement and any earlier order forms previously executed between the Parties governing Customer's access to the Pemberline Dock platform (collectively, the "Prior Agreement"). 

1.2. Except as expressly modified by the terms set forth in this Order Form, all terms, conditions, representations, and operational covenants contained in the Prior Agreement shall remain in full force and effect. In the event of a conflict between the terms of the Prior Agreement and this Order Form, the terms of this Order Form shall govern with respect to the Renewal Term defined herein.

## 2. Subscription Scope and Operational Deployment

2.1. Provider shall continue to provision access to the Pemberline Dock yard and dock scheduling software for Customer's Gulf Coast port drayage and terminal logistics operations, including the East Basin container yard, Gate 4 chassis staging lanes, and the Bayport Cross-Dock facility (Doors 1 through 38).

2.2. Authorised users under this Order Form include Customer's terminal dispatchers, gate clerks, and night-shift yard hostler coordinators generating automated gate-pass manifests and daily dwell-time exception reports.

## 3. Renewal Term

3.1. Notwithstanding the Execution Date of 2026-02-19, the subscription term governed by this Order Form shall commence on the Renewal Effective Date of 2026-03-01 and shall continue through 2027-02-28 ("End of Current Term" or "Renewal Date"), unless terminated earlier in accordance with the provisions of the Prior Agreement.

## 4. Commercial Terms and Fees

4.1. During the Renewal Term, Customer's subscription to Pemberline Dock shall be governed by the following seat allocation and pricing schedule:

* **Product:** Pemberline Dock (Enterprise Yard & Dock Scheduling)
* **Subscribed Seats:** 145
* **Price per Seat per Year (USD):** $1,950.00
* **Total Annual Subscription Fee (USD):** $282,750.00

4.2. All fees are stated and payable in United States Dollars (USD). Invoicing and remittance shall be processed via wire transfer or ACH to the remittance account designated on Provider's invoice, referencing Customer Account Code PHL and Procurement Routing Ticket #PHL-8841-R.

## 5. Entirety of Execution

5.1. The signatories below warrant that they are duly authorised to bind their respective entities to the obligations set forth in this Order Form as of the Execution Date.
