# Amendment 2 (draft): Vantorre Group discount

**DRAFT — NOT SIGNED**

**Drafted: 8/14/26**  
**Prepared by: June Osei-Lark, Deal desk and contracts manager**

## 1. Parties and Purpose

1.1. This draft Amendment 2 identifies Pemberline Systems and Vantorre Group as the proposed parties and records the proposed discount for Pemberline Dock.

1.2. Pemberline Dock is Pemberline Systems’ yard and dock scheduling software, priced per seat per year. The commercial change set out in this draft is a discount of **18%**.

1.3. This document is a draft for review. It has never been signed. Its preparation, circulation, annotation, or placement in the contract review folder does not constitute execution by either party.

## 2. Proposed Discount Provision

2.1. The proposed operative provision is as follows:

> **Discount. The discount applicable to Vantorre Group’s purchase of Pemberline Dock shall be eighteen percent (18%).**

2.2. The percentage expressed in words and the percentage expressed numerically in Section 2.1 are identical. The discount field in the amendment review worksheet shall display “18%,” rather than a decimal or an unlabeled number.

2.3. The proposed provision does not state a seat quantity, a price per seat, an annual total, a renewal date, or a subscription term. No such figure or date is inserted by this draft.

## 3. Identification of the Customer

3.1. The customer named in this draft is Vantorre Group, a diversified industrial manufacturer in the Northeast. Correspondence concerning procurement review shall identify Vantorre Group by that name.

3.2. Mateo Quiroga, Head of Procurement, is the principal customer contact for the review packet. The packet cover shall identify the document as “Amendment 2 (draft): Vantorre Group discount” so that it can be distinguished from purchase requisitions and operational correspondence.

3.3. Internal references using VTG or VTRG shall be expanded to Vantorre Group in the signature copy. Abbreviations in a folder name, an email subject, or a worksheet tab shall not replace the party name in the amendment text.

## 4. Internal Discount Review

4.1. Anselm Grieve, CFO, approves discounts over 10%. The proposed 18% discount therefore requires his approval within Pemberline Systems’ internal review.

4.2. The internal review copy shall present the proposed percentage prominently. Reviewers shall not be required to derive the percentage from a net amount or reconstruct it from a worksheet formula.

4.3. Any approval response shall remain with the review materials. An internal approval response is separate from execution of the amendment by the parties and shall not be placed on a signature line or reproduced as a party signature.

4.4. Questions concerning the proposed percentage shall be returned to deal desk before a revised copy is circulated.

## 5. Procurement Review Packet

5.1. The procurement review packet shall contain this draft and a short cover note directing Mateo Quiroga to Section 2.1. The cover note shall request review of the proposed discount language without describing the draft as completed or executed.

5.2. Comments received in an email, a marked PDF, or a word-processing revision shall be collected in the same review folder. Where a comment refers to “the discount paragraph,” the review log shall identify Section 2.1.

5.3. The packet filename shall retain “draft.” A PDF generated for easier reading shall carry the same draft designation in its body. Conversion to PDF shall not remove the unsigned status notice.

## 6. Separation from Operational Requests

6.1. Operational requests shall remain in the support queue rather than being incorporated into the discount provision. For example, a request to move a carrier appointment from Door 4 to Door 6 is a scheduling matter, not a revision to the percentage in Section 2.1.

6.2. Likewise, a request to separate first-shift and second-shift arrivals in a yard report shall be routed for product review. Such a request shall not be added as a handwritten qualification beside the discount.

6.3. If procurement correspondence includes screenshots of the appointment board, the screenshots shall be filed as correspondence attachments. They shall not be treated as amendment schedules merely because they accompanied a reply concerning this draft.

## 7. Worksheet and Calculation Handling

7.1. Any worksheet used during review shall label the discount cell “Discount (%)” and show 18%. A reviewer preparing a comparison shall check that a copied formula has not converted the entry to 18 or 0.18 without the appropriate display format.

7.2. A calculation prepared for discussion shall remain distinguishable from the amendment text. Spreadsheet formatting, rounding, hidden rows, and print settings shall not alter the percentage stated in Section 2.1.

7.3. Before circulation, deal desk shall inspect the printed worksheet view for clipped column headings and missing percentage symbols. A clean printout is required for review; it is not a substitute for the proposed operative provision.

## 8. Treatment of Review Comments

8.1. Review comments shall identify the relevant section and the requested textual change. A comment stating only “approved” shall be retained with its surrounding message so that its subject remains clear.

8.2. Proposed edits to the party name, product name, or discount percentage shall be reviewed in the document itself. They shall not be implemented solely by changing an email subject line or renaming a file.

8.3. Resolved comments may be removed from a clean reading copy. The marked review copy shall remain available alongside it so that the clean copy can be compared against the text actually reviewed.

## 9. Preparation of a Signature Copy

9.1. A signature copy shall not be prepared from a version containing unresolved edits to Section 2.1.

9.2. Before preparing any signature copy, deal desk shall compare the title, party names, product name, and discount percentage against this draft and any subsequently accepted revisions.

9.3. The present document remains the unsigned draft dated 8/14/26. Its signature lines are intentionally blank.

## 10. Record Handling

10.1. This draft shall be retained under the Vantorre Group amendment materials. It shall not be filed in a folder labeled “Signed” or attached to a completion notice.

10.2. A later review copy shall use a distinct filename rather than overwrite this record. Supporting correspondence shall remain readable, including the sender, date, and attachment names.

## 11. Review Disposition

11.1. The next actions are to submit the proposed 18% discount for Anselm Grieve’s approval, assemble the procurement review packet for Mateo Quiroga, and resolve textual comments before preparing any signature copy.

11.2. Until those review steps are completed and the parties execute an amendment, this document shall continue to be handled as a draft.
