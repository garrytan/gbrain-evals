# Tansleigh Packaging

**Updated:** 2025-04-02  
**Account Code:** TSP  
**Seats:** 30

Quick update on the wiki page for Tansleigh Packaging since I took over as AM back in Jan 2025 (on top of the Central team lead stuff, pray for my calendar lol). 

They're a corrugated packaging manufacturer out in the Ohio Valley with their main plant in Zanesville. Note for anyone pulling tickets: Tansleigh Packaging and Tansley Parcel are completely unrelated companies. Ppl mix them up in Slack all the time so watch the account code—use TSP for these guys.

Operationally they're pretty chill, mostly running outbound box loads off doors 1 through 8 at Zanesville. Second shift supervisor (Mark) has been complaining a bit abt flatbed drivers showing up two hrs early for starch deliveries and clogging the west gate, so we're looking at tweaking their carrier check-in buffer next wk.
