# Marrow Agrisupply

**Last updated:** 2026-09-15  
**Account code:** MAS (though half the floor still calls it "Marrow Agri" in Slack)  
**Parent:** Marrow Fieldworks  
**Ownership:** Central team pooled account (no single account manager) since January 2025.
