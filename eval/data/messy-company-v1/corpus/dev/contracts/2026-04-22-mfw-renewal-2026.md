# Renewal Order Form: Marrow Fieldworks 2026

**Document Status:** Executed Record  
**Drafted Date:** 6 Apr 2026  
**Execution Date:** 22 Apr 2026  
**Renewal Effective Date:** 1 May 2026  

---

## 1. Parties and Governing Agreement

**1.1.** This Renewal Order Form ("Renewal Order Form") is entered into by and between **Pemberline Systems** ("Provider"), a Delaware corporation, and **Marrow Fieldworks** ("Customer"), an agricultural equipment dealer network operating in the Plains region, together with its subsidiary and participating affiliate **Marrow Agrisupply** ("Affiliate"). Provider, Customer, and Affiliate may each be referred to herein individually as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."

**1.2.** This Renewal Order Form was drafted on 6 Apr 2026 and is executed by the Parties on 22 Apr 2026 ("Execution Date"). Notwithstanding the Execution Date, the commercial terms, subscription quantities, and pricing set forth in this Renewal Order Form shall take effect on 1 May 2026 ("Renewal Effective Date"). For the avoidance of doubt, the Execution Date of 22 Apr 2026 and the Renewal Effective Date of 1 May 2026 are distinct dates, and the prior term's commercial terms remain in full force until the Renewal Effective Date.

**1.3.** This Renewal Order Form modifies, amends, and supersedes the relevant commercial schedules of the earlier agreement between the Parties governing access to the Pemberline Dock yard and dock scheduling software platform (the "Agreement"), as subsequently modified by the Affiliate Amendment previously executed by the Parties (the "Affiliate Amendment"). Except as expressly modified by this Renewal Order Form, all terms, conditions, definitions, and provisions of the earlier Agreement and the Affiliate Amendment shall remain in full force and effect. In the event of any conflict between the terms of the earlier Agreement, the Affiliate Amendment, and this Renewal Order Form, the terms of this Renewal Order Form shall control solely with respect to the subject matter specifically addressed herein.

---

## 2. Renewal Term

**2.1.** The renewal subscription term governed by this Renewal Order Form shall commence on the Renewal Effective Date of 1 May 2026 and shall continue through and expire on **30 Apr 2027** ("End of Current Term" or "Renewal Date"). 

**2.2.** The End of Current Term of 30 Apr 2027 applies co-terminously to both **Marrow Fieldworks** and **Marrow Agrisupply**. All active subscriptions under the earlier Agreement and the Affiliate Amendment for both Customer and Affiliate shall align to and terminate or renew on 30 Apr 2027.

---

## 3. Subscribed Seats and Pricing Schedule

**3.1. Product Description.** The subscriptions renewed hereunder grant access to the **Pemberline Dock** yard and dock scheduling software platform, licensed on a per-seat, per-year basis for use across designated dealer yards, heavy equipment staging bays, and retail farm supply receiving gates.

**3.2. Subscribed Seats for Marrow Fieldworks.** As of the Renewal Effective Date of 1 May 2026, the total number of subscribed seats allocated to **Marrow Fieldworks** shall be **95** subscribed seats. These seats are designated for named dispatchers, yard coordinators, and receiving clerks operating across Marrow Fieldworks equipment dealer locations (including the Kearney Machinery Annex, the Salina Parts Depot, and associated regional service yards).

**3.3. Subscribed Seats for Marrow Agrisupply.** This Renewal Order Form does not alter or restate the subscribed seat count for **Marrow Agrisupply**. The subscribed seats allocated to Marrow Agrisupply shall remain strictly as set by the Affiliate Amendment.

**3.4. Annual Unit Pricing.** Commencing on the Renewal Effective Date of 1 May 2026 and continuing through 30 Apr 2027, the price per seat per year for **Pemberline Dock** shall be **$1,880 USD** (One Thousand Eight Hundred Eighty United States Dollars). This annual unit price of $1,880 USD per seat per year applies equally to both **Marrow Fieldworks** and **Marrow Agrisupply**, superseding any prior per-seat pricing set forth in the earlier Agreement or the Affiliate Amendment.

| Entity | Product | Subscribed Seats | Price per Seat per Year (USD) | Term Start | Term End (Renewal Date) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Marrow Fieldworks** | Pemberline Dock | 95 | $1,880 | 1 May 2026 | 30 Apr 2027 |
| **Marrow Agrisupply** | Pemberline Dock | As set by the Affiliate Amendment | $1,880 | 1 May 2026 | 30 Apr 2027 |

## 4. Service Level Agreement and Operational Continuity

**4.1. Preservation of Affiliate Amendment Terms.** This Renewal Order Form leaves Marrow Agrisupply's subscribed seats and Service Level Agreement (SLA) strictly as set by the Affiliate Amendment. No modification to uptime commitments, scheduled maintenance windows, or service credit calculations under the Affiliate Amendment is effected by this instrument.

**4.2. Operational Scope and Provisioning.** Provider shall maintain continuous provisioning of existing production tenant configurations (`mfw-plains-prod` and `mas-retail-prod`), including custom carrier check-in kiosks at Gate 4 of the Salina Parts Depot, flatbed lowboy appointment templates, and automated EDI 214 carrier status exports, without interruption across the transition on 1 May 2026.

**4.3. Invoicing Administration.** Billing and invoicing for the fees resulting from this Renewal Order Form shall be processed through Provider's designated billing platform, Stoneharrow Billing, in accordance with the payment terms established in the earlier Agreement. Customer and Affiliate invoices shall reference administrative tracking code `ROF-MFW-MAS-2026-04`.
