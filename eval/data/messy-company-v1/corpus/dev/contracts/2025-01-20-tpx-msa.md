# Subscription Agreement: Tansley Parcel

## 1. Parties and Record Dates

This Subscription Agreement is entered into by Pemberline Systems and Tansley Parcel for the subscription to Pemberline Dock described below. Pemberline Dock is Pemberline Systems’ yard and dock scheduling software.

This Agreement was drafted on 2025-01-08. This executed record was written on 2025-01-20. Both parties signed this Agreement on 2025-01-20. The effective date is 2025-02-01; the execution date and effective date are distinct.

## 2. Subscription and Term

Tansley Parcel subscribes to 110 seats of Pemberline Dock. The subscription is priced per seat per year.

The current subscription term begins on 2025-02-01 and ends on 2026-01-31. The end of the current term, recorded as the renewal date, is 2026-01-31.

The subscribed quantity shall be recorded as 110 seats in the subscription record, billing worksheet, and access-allocation register. Operational lists identifying users by shift or location shall refer to that subscribed quantity rather than treat each shift roster as a separate subscription.

## 3. Price and Discount

The price per seat per year is USD 1,800. A discount of 10% applies to the subscription.

For 110 seats, the annual amount before discount is USD 198,000. The discount is USD 19,800, resulting in a discounted annual subscription amount of USD 178,200.

The billing worksheet shall display the seat quantity, annual unit price, discount percentage, and discounted annual amount as separate entries so that Tansley Parcel may reconcile the invoice against this Agreement.

## 4. Payment Terms

Payment terms are net 30 days. Invoice preparation shall use the subscription particulars set out in Sections 2 and 3.

Procurement correspondence concerning invoice routing shall identify Tansley Parcel by its full name. Lev Arkadin, Procurement lead, is the customer contact for the administrative exchange of billing instructions and subscription paperwork.

## 5. Operational Administration

The implementation file shall distinguish the seat-allocation register from dock-door and appointment records. For example, a dispatch supervisor listed on an evening-shift roster is a user entry; a door label such as “Door 4 — outbound parcels” is an operational scheduling entry.

Routine setup correspondence shall identify the relevant site, shift, and scheduling task. Requests concerning an appointment moved from an afternoon loading window to an evening window shall include the appointment reference, rather than rely solely on a driver’s name.

These administrative descriptions support preparation of the working records and do not replace the subscription quantity, price, discount, term dates, or payment terms stated above.

## 6. Execution

The parties execute this Agreement on 2025-01-20 for a subscription effective on 2025-02-01.
