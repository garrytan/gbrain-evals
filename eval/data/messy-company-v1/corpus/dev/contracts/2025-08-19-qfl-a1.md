# Amendment No. 1 to the Quarrendon Master Subscription Agreement

**Written:** 2025-08-19  
**Drafted:** 2025-08-11  
**Execution Date:** 2025-08-19  
**Effective Date:** 2025-09-01

## 1. Parties and Purpose

This Amendment No. 1 (“Amendment”) is entered into by Pemberline Systems and Quarrendon Freight Lines (each a “Party” and together the “Parties”). This Amendment modifies the earlier master subscription agreement and related subscription paperwork between the Parties concerning Quarrendon Freight Lines’ use of Pemberline Dock (collectively, the “Agreement”).

The Parties intend this Amendment to establish the subscribed seat quantity applicable from the Effective Date. References in this Amendment to the Agreement are generic references to the Parties’ earlier agreement and do not designate a separate or replacement subscription.

## 2. Amendment to Subscribed Seats

Effective 2025-09-01, the subscribed seat quantity for Quarrendon Freight Lines under the Agreement shall be **185 seats**.

The quantity stated in this Section replaces any different subscribed seat quantity appearing in the earlier Agreement or related subscription paperwork for Quarrendon Freight Lines. It shall be the controlling subscribed seat quantity for purposes of administering the Pemberline Dock subscription from the Effective Date.

This Amendment addresses subscribed seats only. It does not establish a separate subscription for any yard, dock, shift, or operating location.

## 3. Execution and Effective Date

The Parties executed this Amendment on **2025-08-19**. The seat amendment set forth in Section 2 takes effect on **2025-09-01**, not on the execution date.

Execution before the Effective Date permits the Parties to prepare their subscription records for the change. Such preparation does not accelerate the Effective Date or apply the amended seat quantity to an earlier period. The distinction between execution and effectiveness shall govern any administrative reference to this Amendment.

## 4. Administrative Implementation

Pemberline Systems shall record the amended quantity against the Quarrendon Freight Lines subscription. Quarrendon Freight Lines shall coordinate its user roster so that personnel administering dock appointments and yard scheduling can identify the subscription to which this Amendment applies.

For implementation purposes, the user roster should distinguish individual users from shared operational labels, including dispatch desk and receiving shift labels. Any roster cleanup undertaken in preparation for the Effective Date is an administrative activity and does not itself modify the Agreement.

## 5. Continuing Effect of Agreement

Except for the subscribed seat quantity expressly amended herein, the Agreement remains unchanged and continues in effect. This Amendment does not restate or replace the remaining provisions of the Agreement.

If a provision of the earlier subscription paperwork conflicts with Section 2 concerning subscribed seat quantity from the Effective Date, Section 2 controls. The Parties shall otherwise read this Amendment together with the Agreement.
