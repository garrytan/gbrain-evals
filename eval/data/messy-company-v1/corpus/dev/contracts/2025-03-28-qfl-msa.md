# Master Subscription Agreement: Pemberline Systems and Quarrendon Freight Lines

**Document Control:** Drafted 3/10/25 | Executed 3/28/25 | Effective Date 4/1/25

This Master Subscription Agreement (this "Agreement") is drafted as of 3/10/25, executed by the duly authorized representatives of the parties on 3/28/25 (the "Execution Date"), and made effective as of 4/1/25 (the "Effective Date"), by and between **Pemberline Systems**, a Delaware corporation having its principal place of business at 440 Commerce Way, Suite 900, Chicago, Illinois ("Pemberline"), and **Quarrendon Freight Lines**, a Minnesota corporation having its principal operational headquarters at 1880 North Terminal Drive, Saint Paul, Minnesota ("Customer" or "QFL"). Pemberline and Customer may each be referred to herein individually as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."

## RECITALS

WHEREAS, Pemberline develops, hosts, maintains, and markets a proprietary cloud-based yard and dock scheduling software platform known commercially as Pemberline Dock, designed for freight carriers, distribution centers, and logistics operators;

WHEREAS, Customer operates truckload freight terminals, cross-dock facilities, drop yards, and gatehouse operations across the Upper Midwest and desires to subscribe to Pemberline Dock for the management of its dock doors, trailer staging bays, gate check-ins, and yard jockey dispatches; and

WHEREAS, the Parties desire to establish the terms and conditions under which Pemberline will grant Customer access to the Platform and provide associated support services, as further specified in the Order Schedule attached hereto and incorporated herein by reference.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants, representations, warranties, and undertakings set forth herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 "Affiliate"** means, with respect to any entity, any other entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with such entity, where "control" means the direct or indirect ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting securities or equity interests of such entity, or the power to direct the management and policies of such entity.

**1.2 "Authorized User"** means an individual employee, yard clerk, gate guard, dispatcher, terminal manager, or dedicated third-party contractor of Customer who has been assigned a unique username and credential set by Customer to access and use the Platform under a Subscribed Seat.

**1.3 "Confidential Information"** has the meaning set forth in Section 8.1 of this Agreement.

**1.4 "Customer Data"** means all electronic data, text, records, carrier bills of lading numbers, tractor and trailer identifiers, seal numbers, driver check-in logs, dock door schedules, yard spot maps, Detention and Demurrage timestamps, and other operational information submitted, uploaded, imported, or transmitted by or on behalf of Customer or its Authorized Users into the Platform.

**1.5 "Documentation"** means the user manuals, release notes, administrator guides, API specifications, and online help files published by Pemberline from time to time and made generally available to subscribers of Pemberline Dock.

**1.6 "Effective Date"** means 4/1/25, which is the date on which the subscription rights, operational access, and service level obligations under this Agreement commence, distinct from the Execution Date of 3/28/25 on which the Parties signed this Agreement.

**1.7 "Order Schedule"** means the initial ordering document attached hereto as Schedule A, and any subsequent written ordering document executed by both Parties that references this Agreement.

**1.8 "Platform"** means the hosted, multi-tenant software-as-a-service application known as Pemberline Dock, including its web-based dispatcher console, gatehouse kiosk view, carrier self-scheduling portal, yard spotter task module, and standard application programming interfaces (APIs), as updated or modified by Pemberline from time to time in accordance with this Agreement.

**1.9 "Subscribed Seat"** means a single named-user license provisioned within the Platform for one (1) Authorized User. Each Subscribed Seat is specific to a designated individual and may not be shared concurrently among multiple personnel across shifts except as expressly permitted in Section 2.3.

**1.10 "Support Services"** means the technical support, maintenance, bug fixing, and operational assistance provided by Pemberline in accordance with the Tier 2 service level commitments described in Article 5.

## ARTICLE 2. SUBSCRIPTION GRANT AND SEATS

**2.1 Provision of Access.** Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement and Customer's timely payment of all applicable Fees, Pemberline hereby grants to Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable (except as permitted under Article 17), non-sublicensable, worldwide right during the active period of this Agreement to access and use the Platform and Documentation solely for Customer's internal business operations in managing its freight terminals, yards, and loading docks.

**2.2 Subscribed Seats.** As of the Effective Date (4/1/25), Customer subscribes to exactly one hundred forty (140) Subscribed Seats of Pemberline Dock. Customer may allocate these 140 Subscribed Seats across its operational terminals, including its Saint Paul hub, Duluth yard, Eau Claire cross-dock, and Des Moines relay station, at the discretion of Customer's system administrators.

**2.3 Seat Assignment and Reassignment.** Each of the 140 Subscribed Seats must be tied to a unique individual identity (using corporate email address or employee badge ID) within the Platform's identity management directory. Customer may not configure generic, shared, or role-based credentials (such as "gatehouse1@qfl.example" or "nightdispatch@qfl.example") to allow multiple individuals on rotating shifts to utilize a single Subscribed Seat. Notwithstanding the foregoing, Customer may permanently reassign a Subscribed Seat from one individual to another without penalty when an Authorized User terminates employment, transfers out of yard or dock operations, or goes on extended leave exceeding fourteen (14) consecutive calendar days.

**2.4 Seat Audits and True-Up.** Pemberline's Platform includes automated seat-count enforcement that prevents the provisioning of active credentials beyond the 140 Subscribed Seats unless an administrator with purchasing authority initiates an expansion request. If Customer requires additional seats during an active period, Customer may order additional Subscribed Seats in minimum blocks of five (5) seats by executing an addendum to the Order Schedule. Any mid-period seat additions shall be priced at the discounted net rate set forth in Section 3.1, prorated for the remainder of the then-current period.

**2.5 Reservation of Rights.** This Agreement is a subscription agreement for access to hosted software services and is not a sale of software or intellectual property. Customer acknowledges that it is obtaining only a limited right to access and use the Platform as hosted by Pemberline, and that irrespective of any use of the words "purchase," "sale," or "seat" hereunder, no ownership rights are being conveyed to Customer under this Agreement.

## ARTICLE 3. FEES AND PRICING

**3.1 Subscription Fees.** In consideration for the provision of the Platform and the Tier 2 Support Services, Customer shall pay Pemberline the annual subscription fees specified in this Section 3.1 and Schedule A (the "Fees"). The standard list price for Pemberline Dock is one thousand eight hundred fifty United States Dollars ($1,850.00 USD) per Subscribed Seat per year. Under this Agreement, Pemberline grants Customer a contractual discount of twelve percent (12%) off the standard list price, resulting in a net discounted price of one thousand six hundred twenty-eight United States Dollars ($1,628.00 USD) per Subscribed Seat per year.

**3.2 Total Annual Commitment.** Based on the subscription of 140 Subscribed Seats at the list price of $1,850.00 USD per seat per year ($259,000.00 USD gross annual value), less the twelve percent (12%) discount ($31,080.00 USD annual discount), Customer's total net annual subscription Fee for the initial twelve-month period beginning on 4/1/25 and ending on 3/31/26 is two hundred twenty-seven thousand nine hundred twenty United States Dollars ($227,920.00 USD).

**3.3 Non-Cancelable and Non-Refundable.** Except as expressly provided in Section 11.3 (Warranty Remedies), Section 12.1 (Intellectual Property Infringement Mitigation), or Section 16.2 (Termination for Cause by Customer), all Fees are non-cancelable and non-refundable once invoiced. Customer may not reduce its commitment below 140 Subscribed Seats during any active period of this Agreement. No refunds or credits will be issued for partial use, underutilization of the 140 Subscribed Seats, or seasonal reductions in freight volume at Customer's terminals.

**3.4 Taxes.** All Fees stated herein are exclusive of any federal, state, local, municipal, or foreign sales, use, value-added, excise, gross receipts, or other taxes, duties, or governmental assessments ("Taxes"). Customer is solely responsible for paying all Taxes associated with its purchases hereunder, other than taxes assessed on Pemberline's net income, property, or payroll. If Pemberline is legally required to collect and remit Taxes on Customer's behalf, such Taxes will be itemized separately on the applicable invoice unless Customer provides a valid, properly executed tax exemption certificate prior to invoicing.

## ARTICLE 5. SUPPORT AND SERVICE LEVELS

**5.1 Support Tier Assignment.** During the active period of this Agreement, Pemberline shall provide Customer with **Tier 2** Support Services for the Platform. Tier 2 Support Services are included in the annual subscription Fees set forth in Article 3 at no additional charge.

**5.2 Tier 2 Channels and Hours.** Under the Tier 2 support plan, Customer's designated system administrators (up to six (6) named individuals appointed by Customer's Director of Yard Operations) may submit support tickets via Pemberline's web-based support portal and by direct telephone hotline.
(a) **Severity 1 (Critical Yard Outage):** Telephone and portal support available twenty-four (24) hours per day, seven (7) days per week, three hundred sixty-five (365) days per year (24x7x365). A Severity 1 issue is defined as a complete inability of Authorized Users at one or more terminals to access the Platform, or a failure of core gate check-in or dock door assignment functions with no operational workaround, resulting in physical truck queues at terminal gates.
(b) **Severity 2 (Major Operational Degradation):** Support coverage available Monday through Saturday, 5:00 AM to 11:00 PM Central Time. A Severity 2 issue is defined as a substantial impairment of primary features (such as yard jockey task dispatch latency, failure of EDI 214 status message transmission, or carrier portal booking failure) where core yard operations can continue via manual workaround within the Platform.
(c) **Severity 3 (Minor Feature Issue):** Support coverage available Monday through Friday, 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM Central Time, excluding US federal holidays. A Severity 3 issue is defined as a non-critical malfunction, reporting discrepancy, or user interface defect that has minimal impact on daily dock scheduling.
(d) **Severity 4 (Cosmetic or Inquiry):** Support coverage available Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM Central Time. A Severity 4 issue includes general "how-to" configuration questions, documentation requests, or cosmetic enhancement requests.

**5.3 Tier 2 Target Response Times.** Under the Tier 2 SLA, Pemberline commits to the following initial human response times from the moment a ticket is logged in the support portal or received via the emergency telephone hotline:
* **Severity 1:** Within thirty (30) minutes, with continuous engineering engagement and status updates every sixty (60) minutes until service is restored.
* **Severity 2:** Within two (2) hours during covered hours, with status updates every four (4) hours.
* **Severity 3:** Within eight (8) business hours.
* **Severity 4:** Within two (2) business days.

**5.4 Platform Availability Commitment.** Under the Tier 2 SLA, Pemberline shall maintain a Monthly Uptime Percentage for the production instance of the Platform of at least ninety-nine and five-tenths percent (99.5%), measured across each calendar month. "Monthly Uptime Percentage" is calculated by subtracting from 100% the percentage of total minutes in the calendar month during which the Platform experienced Unscheduled Downtime.

**5.5 Exclusions from Unscheduled Downtime.** Unscheduled Downtime does not include unavailability resulting from: (a) Scheduled Maintenance windows occurring between Saturday 11:00 PM Central Time and Sunday 3:00 AM Central Time, provided Pemberline gives at least forty-eight (48) hours' prior electronic notice via the Platform status page; (b) Force Majeure Events as defined in Section 19.3; (c) failures of Customer's local yard Wi-Fi networks, cellular data terminals mounted in yard spotter cabs, barcode scanners, RFID gate readers, or local internet service providers; (d) interruptions caused by third-party transportation management systems (TMS) or warehouse management systems (WMS) integrated by Customer via API; or (e) suspension of Customer's access pursuant to Article 15.

**5.6 Service Credits.** If Pemberline fails to meet the 99.5% Monthly Uptime Percentage under the Tier 2 SLA in any given calendar month, Customer shall be eligible to receive a Service Credit applied against its next invoice, calculated as a percentage of one-twelfth (1/12th) of the net annual subscription Fee ($18,993.33 USD monthly equivalent), as follows:
* Monthly Uptime Percentage between 99.00% and 99.49%: Five percent (5%) credit.
* Monthly Uptime Percentage between 98.00% and 98.99%: Ten percent (10%) credit.
* Monthly Uptime Percentage below 98.00%: Twenty percent (20%) credit.
To receive a Service Credit, Customer must submit a written request within thirty (30) calendar days following the end of the calendar month in which the Unscheduled Downtime occurred. Service Credits are Customer's sole and exclusive financial remedy for any failure to meet the Tier 2 availability metrics, provided that if the Monthly Uptime Percentage falls below 97.00% for three (3) consecutive calendar months, Customer may treat such failure as a material breach under Section 16.2.

## ARTICLE 7. INFORMATION SECURITY AND PRIVACY

**7.1 Security Program.** Pemberline shall maintain a formal, written information security program that includes administrative, physical, and technical safeguards designed to protect the security, confidentiality, and integrity of Customer Data against unauthorized access, destruction, modification, or disclosure. Such safeguards shall be no less rigorous than accepted industry standards for enterprise B2B software-as-a-service providers, including alignment with AICPA SOC 2 Type II Trust Services Criteria.

**7.2 Technical Controls.** Without limiting the generality of Section 7.1, Pemberline's technical safeguards shall include at a minimum: (a) encryption of all Customer Data in transit over public networks using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2 or higher); (b) encryption of all Customer Data at rest in database storage and backup volumes using Advanced Encryption Standard with 256-bit keys (AES-256); (c) logical tenant isolation in the application and database layers to prevent any other customer from accessing Customer Data; (d) support for Security Assertion Markup Language (SAML 2.0) single sign-on (SSO) integration and multi-factor authentication (MFA) for all 140 Subscribed Seats; and (e) role-based access controls (RBAC) allowing Customer to restrict user permissions by terminal location and operational function.

**7.3 Audits and Certifications.** At least once every twelve (12) months, Pemberline shall engage an independent, certified third-party auditing firm to conduct a SOC 2 Type II examination of the controls governing the Platform. Upon Customer's written request (not more than once annually), Pemberline shall provide Customer with a confidential copy of its most recent SOC 2 Type II report and executive summary of its annual third-party penetration test, which shall be treated as Pemberline's Confidential Information under Article 8.

**7.4 Security Incident Notification.** In the event Pemberline confirms an unauthorized acquisition, access, alteration, or disclosure of unencrypted Customer Data residing on Pemberline's systems (a "Security Incident"), Pemberline shall notify Customer without undue delay, and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours of confirmation. Such notice shall be delivered to Customer's primary operational contact and legal department and shall describe the nature of the Security Incident, the categories of Customer Data affected, the root cause (to the extent then known), and the containment and remediation measures taken by Pemberline. Pemberline shall cooperate with Customer's reasonable requests for information necessary for Customer to comply with applicable data breach notification laws.

**7.5 Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery.** Pemberline shall maintain and annually test a disaster recovery and business continuity plan for the Platform. The production environment for Pemberline Dock shall utilize redundant availability zones within the continental United States, configured to achieve a Recovery Point Objective (RPO) of no more than fifteen (15) minutes and a Recovery Time Objective (RTO) of no more than four (4) hours in the event of a catastrophic data center failure.
