### 1. Parties and Governing Agreement

**1.1 Identification of Parties.** This Renewal Order Form and Operational Addendum ("Renewal Addendum") is entered into by and between **Pemberline Systems**, a Delaware corporation having its principal place of business at 410 Mercer Wharf, Suite 800 ("Provider"), and **Brackenwater Freight Co.** ("Customer" or "BWF"), a Michigan corporation operating less-than-truckload freight terminals across the Great Lakes region, with administrative offices at 1900 Lakefront Industrial Parkway, Toledo, Ohio. Provider and Customer may each be referred to herein individually as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."

**1.2 Reference to Master Agreement.** This Renewal Addendum supplements and modifies that certain Master Subscription Services Agreement between the Parties, together with all prior schedules and attachments thereto (collectively, the "Agreement"), governing Customer's access to and use of the **Pemberline Dock** yard and dock scheduling software platform (the "Hosted Service"). Except as expressly amended by the numbered provisions set forth herein, the terms and conditions of the Agreement shall remain in full force and effect. In the event of an irreconcilable conflict between the body of the Agreement and this Renewal Addendum, the provisions of this Renewal Addendum shall prevail solely with respect to the renewal period governed hereby.

---

### 2. Commercial Renewal Schedule

**2.1 Subscribed Seat Allocation.** Subject to Customer's timely remittance of applicable fees, Provider grants to Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to provision and maintain **75 subscribed seats** for the Hosted Service during the applicable renewal term. Subscribed seats are priced and allocated on a per-seat, per-year basis in accordance with Provider's standard licensing metrics.

**2.2 Named User Reassignment.** Each subscribed seat shall be assigned to an individual, named terminal clerk, yard spotter, dispatcher, or gate attendant employed or contracted by Brackenwater Freight Co. Customer may reassign a seat from a departing or transferred employee to a replacement employee without additional charge during shift rotations, provided that the aggregate number of active concurrent credentials provisioned across all BWF facilities does not exceed seventy-five (75) at any given time.

**2.3 Promotional and Commercial Discount.** In consideration of Customer's multi-terminal deployment and volume commitment hereunder, a commercial discount of **15%** shall be applied to Provider's prevailing per-seat annual list price for the 75 subscribed seats specified in Section 2.1. Pursuant to Provider's internal corporate governance policies, any discount exceeding ten percent (10%) requires the written authorization of Provider's Chief Financial Officer, Anselm Grieve, prior to final execution of this instrument by Provider.

### 3. Terminal Operations and Configuration Provisions (Customer Redline Insertions)

**3.1 Cross-Dock Gate Check-In Hardware.** *(Customer Counsel Insertion, 2025-10-28):* Provider acknowledges that Customer's third-shift freight handlers at the Toledo Central Hub (specifically Dock Doors 14 through 38) and the Grand Rapids West satellite yard utilize ruggedized handheld barcode scanners mounted to yard hostler cabs. Provider shall ensure that the Pemberline Dock mobile web view retains backward compatibility with Customer's existing Zebra TC77 handheld fleet without requiring firmware upgrades during peak mid-week LTL consolidation windows (Tuesday 22:00 Eastern through Friday 06:00 Eastern).

**3.2 Automated Detention Log Exports.** *(Customer Counsel Insertion, Oct 28):* Following the carrier dispute incident logged under Support Ticket #PD-88412 on October 14, 2025, regarding trailer dwell timestamps at the Milwaukee South Annex (Gate B), Provider agrees that the daily *Carrier Dwell & Yard Velocity CSV Report* shall automatically generate and deposit into Customer's designated SFTP directory no later than 04:30 Central Time each business day. Such report shall capture tractor arrival scan, gate pass issuance, dock door bump timestamp, and seal verification sign-off for each LTL linehaul trailer.

**3.3 Temporary Yard Overflow Rules.** In the event that winter weather advisories in the Great Lakes corridor require Customer to stage inbound pup trailers along the perimeter gravel lot at the Kalamazoo terminal, Customer's yard supervisors may generate unlimited virtual drop-spots within the Pemberline Dock yard map interface. Virtual drop-spots do not consume additional subscribed seats, provided that no additional user logins are created beyond the 75 seats authorized in Section 2.1.
