# Order Form: Brackenwater Freight Co. (2024-2025 term)

## 1. Parties and Record

This Order Form is between Pemberline Systems and Brackenwater Freight Co. for the subscription specified below. The subscribed product is Pemberline Dock, Pemberline Systems’ yard and dock scheduling software.

This instrument was drafted on 2024-11-01. The executed record was prepared on 2024-11-12 by June Osei-Lark, Deal desk and contracts manager, for inclusion in the order file. Brackenwater Freight Co. is identified in the associated setup materials by the abbreviations BWF and Bracken; those abbreviations refer to the same customer named in this instrument.

## 2. Execution and Effective Date

Both parties signed this Order Form on 2024-11-12. The effective date is 2024-12-01.

The execution date records the parties’ signatures. The effective date identifies the commencement of the subscription described herein. These dates are distinct; execution on 2024-11-12 does not replace or advance the stated effective date.

The signature block below forms part of this executed record.

## 3. Subscription and Commercial Schedule

The subscription is recorded as follows:

| Item | Order specification |
|---|---|
| Customer | Brackenwater Freight Co. |
| Supplier | Pemberline Systems |
| Product | Pemberline Dock |
| Subscribed seats | 60 |
| Price per seat per year | USD 1,700 |
| Discount | 8% |
| Effective date | 2024-12-01 |
| End of current term / renewal date | 2025-11-30 |

The annual per-seat price and the discount are stated separately. The discount applicable to this order is eight percent. The subscribed quantity is sixty seats.

The end of the current term, recorded as the renewal date, is 2025-11-30. That date is separate from both the drafting date and the execution date.

## 4. Operational Identification

Brackenwater Freight Co. operates in less-than-truckload freight in the Great Lakes region. The implementation worksheet accompanying the order file identifies the receiving desk, outbound dispatch desk, and evening yard desk as the initial points for collection of user details.

The worksheet includes a sample scheduling sequence for Door 14: an inbound trailer arrival, a receiving appointment, and an outbound door reassignment. It also includes a second-shift example in which dispatch moves a delayed pickup from the active door list to the next available appointment slot.

These entries describe the operational examples collected for setup review. They do not alter the subscribed seat quantity, annual per-seat price, discount, effective date, or term-end date stated in Section 3.

## 5. Administrative Handling

The order file includes this executed instrument and the setup worksheet titled “BWF Door Schedule — Initial Review.” The worksheet contains a column for shift designation and a separate column for the email address used at sign-in.

The receiving desk is collecting spelling corrections for the user list. Outbound dispatch is reviewing the Door 14 example against its paper appointment board. The evening yard desk is marking entries that represent shared desk labels rather than individual users. These administrative tasks concern setup preparation only.
