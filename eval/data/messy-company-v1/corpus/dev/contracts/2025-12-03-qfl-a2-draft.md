## AMENDMENT NO. 2 TO MASTER SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 2 (the **"Amendment"**), drafted as of 2025-12-03, is entered into by and between **Pemberline Systems**, a corporation having its principal place of business at 400 Mercer Waterway, Suite 900 (hereinafter **"Provider"** or **"Pemberline"**), and **Quarrendon Freight Lines**, a truckload freight carrier operating in the Upper Midwest with operational headquarters at 1880 North Terminal Road (hereinafter **"Customer"**, **"QFL"**, or **"Q-Freight"**). Provider and Customer may each be referred to individually as a **"Party"** and collectively as the **"Parties."**

### RECITALS

**WHEREAS**, Provider and Customer previously entered into that certain Master Subscription Agreement (the **"Agreement"**) governing Customer's access to and use of Provider's proprietary yard and dock scheduling software platform known as **Pemberline Dock**, which is licensed and priced on a per-seat, per-year basis;

**WHEREAS**, Customer desires to modify certain commercial terms applicable to its subscription for Pemberline Dock and to memorialize specific operational protocols relating to yard gate check-ins, dock door assignments, and carrier scheduling across its Upper Midwest terminal network; and

**WHEREAS**, the Parties desire to amend the Agreement on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth herein.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants, terms, and conditions set forth herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

---

### 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

**1.1.** Capitalized terms used but not otherwise defined in this Amendment shall have the meanings ascribed to such terms in the Agreement.

**1.2.** **"Operational Contact"** means Thea Ramaswell, Director of Yard Operations for Quarrendon Freight Lines, or such successor individual as Customer may designate in writing via notice to Provider's contract management desk.

**1.3.** **"Seat"** means an individual, named user credential authorized by Customer to access or administer the Pemberline Dock platform, whether assigned to a yard jockey, gatehouse clerk, dispatch coordinator, or shift supervisor, billed on a per-seat, per-year basis in accordance with the Agreement.

### 2. COMMERCIAL TERMS AND DISCOUNT STRUCTURE

**2.1. Promotional and Volume Discount Adjustment.** Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in the Agreement or any prior schedule or order form thereto, the Parties hereby agree that the applicable discount rate for Customer's subscription to Pemberline Dock shall be modified and fixed at **fifteen percent (15%)** off Provider's standard per-seat, per-year list price. 

**2.2. Application of Discount.** The 15% discount established in Section 2.1 of this Amendment shall apply to the per-seat, per-year subscription fees invoiced to Customer under the Agreement. Provider shall instruct its third-party billing and invoicing administrator, Stoneharrow Billing, to configure Customer's account profile under identifier code **QFL** to reflect the 15% discount rate upon full execution of this Amendment.

**2.3. Internal Finance Authorization Condition Precedent.** Customer acknowledges that under Provider's internal corporate governance policies, any discount exceeding ten percent (10%) requires the express written authorization of Provider's Chief Financial Officer, Anselm Grieve. Accordingly, this Amendment remains in **DRAFT** status and shall have no binding legal or commercial force unless and until such internal financial authorization is logged and this Amendment is executed by authorized signatories of both Parties.

### 3. OPERATIONAL COVENANTS AND SITE CONFIGURATION

**3.1. Yard and Dock Workflow Specifications.** In connection with the commercial terms set forth herein, Provider and Customer agree to complete the administrative configuration updates logged under Support Ticket #PD-88412, opened on November 19, 2025, by the Operational Contact. Specifically, the Pemberline Dock tenant environment for Q-Freight shall be configured to support the following operational parameters at no additional configuration fee:

**(a) Eau Claire Terminal (Site Code: QFL-EC04):** Re-mapping of Dock Doors 14 through 22 within the Pemberline Dock visual yard board to designate heated staging bays for winter drop-and-hook freight, together with automated detention alert rules triggering forty-five (45) minutes after gate arrival during the third shift (22:00 to 06:00 CST).

**(b) Duluth North Yard (Site Code: QFL-DL01):** Adjustments to the West Gate kiosk check-in workflow to capture tractor cold-start status and tandem axle weight declarations from incoming linehaul drivers prior to automatic dock door assignment.

**(c) Reporting Cadence:** Activation of the custom report titled *QFL_Daily_Dwell_And_Shunt_Summary_v3*, scheduled for automated delivery at 05:30 CST each business day to the Operational Contact and the designated yard shift leads.

**3.2. Seat Reassignment Rules.** Customer may reassign an active Seat credential from a departing or transferring terminal employee to a replacement employee during the applicable annual period without incurring an administrative transfer fee, provided that no two individuals may concurrently utilize a single Seat login across overlapping shifts at the Duluth or Eau Claire gatehouses.

### 4. ENTITY CLARIFICATION AND NON-AFFILIATION

**4.1. Exclusion of Look-Alike Entities.** For the avoidance of doubt, the rights, licenses, and the 15% discount granted under this Amendment are personal to **Quarrendon Freight Lines** (account codes QFL / Q-Freight) and its directly controlled terminals. Nothing in this Amendment shall be construed to extend any subscription rights, pricing disclosures, or access credentials to *Quarendon Freightways*, an unaffiliated trucking carrier operating competing yard management software. Provider's support desk and Stoneharrow Billing personnel shall verify the exact legal entity name and account code (QFL) prior to processing any billing inquiries or seat provisioning requests.

### 5. MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS

**5.1. No Other Modifications.** Except as expressly modified and amended by this Amendment, all terms, conditions, representations, warranties, and covenants contained in the Agreement shall remain unchanged and in full force and effect. In the event of any conflict or inconsistency between the provisions of this Amendment and the provisions of the Agreement, the provisions of this Amendment shall control with respect to the subject matter hereof.

**5.2. Counterparts and Electronic Delivery.** Once finalized and removed from draft status, this Amendment may be executed in two or more counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, but all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Counterparts may be delivered via electronic mail (including PDF or any electronic signature platform complying with the U.S. federal ESIGN Act of 2000), and any counterpart so delivered shall be deemed to have been duly and validly delivered.

**5.3. Governing Law.** This Amendment shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the governing law provisions set forth in the Agreement.
