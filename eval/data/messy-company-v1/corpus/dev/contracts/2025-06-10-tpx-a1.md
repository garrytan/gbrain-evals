# Amendment 1: Tansley Parcel volume discount

**Document Control:** Drafted 2025-06-02 | Executed 2025-06-10 | Effective Date 2025-07-01  
**Internal Reference:** TPCL-AMD-01

This Amendment 1 ("Amendment") to the underlying subscription agreement and prior ordering documentation (collectively, the "Original Agreement") is entered into by and between **Pemberline Systems** ("Pemberline") and **Tansley Parcel** ("Customer," and together with Pemberline, the "Parties"). 

WHEREAS, the Parties previously entered into the Original Agreement governing Customer’s access to and use of the Pemberline Dock yard and dock scheduling software platform, which is licensed on a per-seat, per-year basis; and

WHEREAS, the Parties now desire to amend the commercial terms of the Original Agreement to establish a revised volume discount applicable to Customer’s subscription;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants, terms, and conditions set forth herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

### 1. Definitions and Interpretation
**1.1.** Capitalized terms used but not otherwise defined in this Amendment shall have the meanings ascribed to them in the Original Agreement.  
**1.2.** In the event of any conflict, inconsistency, or ambiguity between the terms and conditions of this Amendment and the terms and conditions of the Original Agreement, the terms and conditions of this Amendment shall govern and control solely with respect to the subject matter hereof.

### 2. Dates of Drafting, Execution, and Effectiveness
**2.1.** The Parties acknowledge that this Amendment was initially drafted on 2025-06-02 for review by Customer’s procurement organization and Pemberline’s finance department.  
**2.2.** This Amendment is executed by the authorized representatives of both Parties on 2025-06-10 (the "Execution Date").  
**2.3.** Notwithstanding the Execution Date of 2025-06-10, the commercial modifications set forth in Section 3 of this Amendment shall become legally effective on 2025-07-01 (the "Effective Date"). The Execution Date and the Effective Date shall remain distinct for all accounting, invoicing, and audit purposes. Prior to 2025-07-01, the pricing terms of the Original Agreement shall remain in full force without adjustment.

### 3. Modification of Commercial Terms
**3.1. Volume Discount.** Commencing on the Effective Date of 2025-07-01, the applicable pricing schedule in the Original Agreement is hereby amended to apply a volume discount of **14%** (fourteen percent) against Pemberline’s standard per-seat, per-year subscription fees for Pemberline Dock.  
**3.2. Financial Approval and Billing Administration.** Customer acknowledges that the 14% discount established herein has been formally authorized by Pemberline’s Chief Financial Officer, Anselm Grieve, in accordance with Pemberline’s corporate governance policies for discounts exceeding ten percent. Following the Execution Date, Pemberline shall instruct its third-party billing and invoicing administrator, Stoneharrow Billing (via account representative Felix Duarte), to configure Customer’s billing profile (Account Code: TPCL) so that the 14% volume discount is automatically applied to invoices generated for billing periods on or after the Effective Date of 2025-07-01.

### 4. Operational Scope and Continuing Effect
**4.1. Operational Continuity.** This Amendment modifies only the discount percentage described in Section 3.1. It does not alter the technical provisioning of Pemberline Dock across Customer’s South Central parcel hubs, nor does it suspend ongoing operational workflows, including the cross-dock gate reassignment rules at the Little Rock sorting facility (Dock Doors 14 through 28) or the automated carrier check-in logs configured under support ticket PSD-4491.  
**4.2. Ratification.** Except as expressly modified by this Amendment, all terms, representations, warranties, and obligations contained in the Original Agreement remain unchanged and are hereby ratified and confirmed in their entirety.

### 5. Counterparts and Electronic Execution
**5.1.** This Amendment may be executed in two or more counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, but all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Counterparts may be delivered via electronic signature transmission, and any counterpart so delivered shall be deemed to have been duly and validly delivered and be valid and effective for all purposes.
