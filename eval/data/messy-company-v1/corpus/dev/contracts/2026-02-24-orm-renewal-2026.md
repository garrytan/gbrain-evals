# Renewal Order Form: Thornbury Cloth Co. (formerly Orrin Mills)

**Drafted Date:** 2/17/26  
**Execution Date:** 2/24/26  
**Effective Date:** 3/1/26  

---

## 1. Parties and Background

**1.1 Parties.** This Renewal Order Form (this "Renewal Order Form") is entered into by and between **Pemberline Systems** ("Provider"), a corporation having its principal place of business at 400 Mercer Street, Suite 900, Seattle, WA 98109, and **Thornbury Cloth Co.** ("Customer"), formerly known as **Orrin Mills**, a textile manufacturing corporation having its principal operational offices at 1820 Old Loom Highway, Spartanburg, SC 29301. Provider and Customer may each be referred to herein individually as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."

**1.2 Corporate Name Change.** The Parties acknowledge that Customer legally changed its corporate name from Orrin Mills to Thornbury Cloth Co. on 1/12/26. All references to Orrin Mills in the underlying agreement and any prior order forms, exhibits, or statements of work between the Parties shall hereafter be deemed to refer to Thornbury Cloth Co. without interruption of rights, obligations, or liabilities.

**1.3 Governing Agreement.** This Renewal Order Form is governed by and incorporated into the earlier agreement previously executed between Provider and Customer (then operating under the name Orrin Mills) governing the provision of Provider's hosted software-as-a-service offerings (the "Agreement"). This Renewal Order Form amends, supersedes, and replaces the commercial seat allocations and term dates set forth in the earlier paperwork for Customer as of the Effective Date specified herein. In the event of any express conflict between the provisions of this Renewal Order Form and the terms of the Agreement, the provisions of this Renewal Order Form shall prevail solely with respect to the subject matter of the subscription renewal detailed herein.

---

## 2. Dates and Renewal Term

**2.1 Distinction of Dates.** This Renewal Order Form was drafted by Provider on 2/17/26 and is executed and signed by the authorized representatives of both Parties on 2/24/26 (the "Execution Date"). Notwithstanding the Execution Date of 2/24/26, the commercial terms, seat allocations, and fee obligations established under this Renewal Order Form shall not commence until 3/1/26 (the "Effective Date"). Between the Execution Date of 2/24/26 and the Effective Date of 3/1/26, the Parties' rights and obligations shall continue to be governed by the prior order form then in effect under the Agreement.

**2.2 Renewal Term.** The subscription term governed by this Renewal Order Form shall commence at 12:00 a.m. Eastern Time on the Effective Date of 3/1/26 and shall continue in full force and effect through 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on 2/28/27 (the "Renewal Date" and the end of the current term), unless earlier terminated in accordance with the express termination provisions of the Agreement.

## 3. Subscribed Software and Commercial Terms

**3.1 Product Description.** Subject to the terms of the Agreement and timely payment of the applicable fees, Provider grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right during the Renewal Term to access and use **Pemberline Dock**, Provider's cloud-based yard and dock scheduling software platform, up to the quantity of subscribed seats specified below.

**3.2 Subscription Schedule.** As of the Effective Date of 3/1/26, Customer's subscription under the Agreement is hereby updated and set as follows:

| Subscription Item | Metric | Subscribed Quantity | Price per Seat per Year (USD) | Total Annual Fee (USD) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Pemberline Dock (Standard Yard & Dock Scheduling Platform) | Named User Seat | 40 | $1,700.00 | $68,000.00 |

**3.3 Summary of Commercial Terms.** For avoidance of doubt, the terms set by this Renewal Order Form are:
* **Subscribed Seats:** 40
* **Price per Seat per Year (USD):** $1,700
* **Total Annual Subscription Fee (USD):** $68,000
* **Effective Date:** 3/1/26
* **End of Current Term (Renewal Date):** 2/28/27

**3.4 Seat Administration and Reassignment.** Each of the 40 subscribed seats corresponds to a single unique Named User login credential within Customer's Pemberline Dock tenant (`tcc-carolinas-prod`). Named User credentials may not be shared concurrently across multiple individuals or shift desks. Customer's designated system administrators may reassign a Named User seat without additional charge only when an individual permanently leaves Customer's employ, transfers to a role outside yard and shipping operations, or goes on extended leave exceeding thirty (30) consecutive calendar days.

## 4. Operational Deployment and Tenant Configuration

**4.1 Covered Facilities.** The 40 subscribed seats under this Renewal Order Form are authorized for deployment across Customer's Carolina textile mill facilities, specifically:
1. **Spartanburg Weaving & Finishing Mill (Plant 1):** North Receiving Yard (Dock Doors N1 through N14, serving raw cotton bale and synthetic yarn flatbeds) and South Greige Goods Shipping Apron (Dock Doors S1 through S10).
2. **Gaffney Dye House & Distribution Annex (Plant 2):** West Chemical & Dye Unloading Bay (Dock Doors W1 through W4, hazardous/bulk tank check-in) and East Finished Bolt Loading Dock (Dock Doors E1 through E8).
3. **Cheraw Auxilliary Roll Storage Yard:** Gatehouse Guard Kiosk 1 and Drop-Lot Staging Lanes A through F.

**4.2 Administrative Tenant Rebranding.** Pursuant to Customer's name change from Orrin Mills to Thornbury Cloth Co. on 1/12/26, Provider's technical operations team shall complete the migration of Customer's automated carrier appointment confirmation emails, SMS driver gate passes, and bill-of-lading dock receipts from the legacy `orrinmills.com` sender header to `scheduling@thornburycloth.com` under Support Ticket #PD-88412 prior to the 3/1/26 Effective Date. The custom report template titled *Daily Greige Roll Dwell & Detention Log (Carolinas)* and the third-shift yard jockey tablet profiles at the Spartanburg gatehouse will remain active without reconfiguration.
