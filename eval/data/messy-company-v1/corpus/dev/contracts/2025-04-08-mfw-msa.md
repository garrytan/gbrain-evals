# Master Subscription Agreement: Marrow Fieldworks and Covered Affiliates

**Drafted:** 24 Mar 2025  
**Written and signed:** 8 Apr 2025  
**Effective date:** 1 May 2025

## 1. Parties and Purpose

1.1 This Master Subscription Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into by Pemberline Systems (“Pemberline”) and Marrow Fieldworks, for itself and its covered affiliate, Marrow Agrisupply (together, the “Customer”). Marrow Agrisupply is a subsidiary of Marrow Fieldworks and is expressly included within this Agreement.

1.2 This Agreement records the Customer’s subscription to Pemberline Dock, Pemberline’s yard and dock scheduling software. Marrow Fieldworks operates an agricultural equipment dealer network in the Plains. Marrow Agrisupply operates a farm supply retail chain in the Plains. Each entity’s subscription is separately identified below.

## 2. Execution and Effective Date

2.1 This Agreement was drafted on 24 Mar 2025 and executed by the parties on 8 Apr 2025. The date of execution is distinct from the effective date.

2.2 The Agreement becomes effective on 1 May 2025. Preparatory activities before that date, including roster collection, site naming, and review of sample appointment records, do not alter the effective date or the subscription quantities stated herein.

2.3 The end of the current term, designated as the renewal date, is 30 Apr 2026 for both Marrow Fieldworks and Marrow Agrisupply.

## 3. Subscription Schedule

3.1 The following subscription schedule applies:

| Subscribing entity | Subscribed seats | Price per seat per year (USD) | Discount | End of current term (renewal date) | Support SLA tier |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| Marrow Fieldworks | 90 | 1,800 | 10% | 30 Apr 2026 | Tier 2 |
| Marrow Agrisupply | 25 | 1,800 | 10% | 30 Apr 2026 | Tier 2 |

3.2 The stated annual per-seat price and discount apply to each entity listed in the schedule. The seat quantities are entity-specific: 90 seats apply to Marrow Fieldworks, and 25 seats apply to Marrow Agrisupply.

3.3 References to the Customer collectively shall not obscure the separate subscription entries. Administrative rosters, support submissions, and deployment worksheets shall identify the relevant subscribing entity by its full name.

## 4. Covered Affiliate

4.1 Marrow Agrisupply is the covered affiliate under this Agreement. Its inclusion permits its subscription to be administered within this master record while retaining the separate seat quantity shown in Section 3.

4.2 Marrow Fieldworks shall identify whether an operational request concerns Marrow Fieldworks, Marrow Agrisupply, or both. A request to configure a receiving calendar for Marrow Agrisupply shall not be treated as a request to change a Marrow Fieldworks calendar merely because the entities share an administrative contact.

4.3 Site labels shall distinguish the agricultural equipment receiving operation from the farm supply receiving operation. Similar location names shall be accompanied by the subscribing entity’s name in the deployment worksheet.

## 5. Deployment Records

5.1 The Customer shall prepare a deployment worksheet identifying site names, dock-door labels, receiving shifts, and personnel responsible for maintaining appointment calendars. The worksheet shall separate equipment deliveries from farm supply deliveries where those activities use different receiving areas.

5.2 Initial configuration review shall include representative appointment entries for an equipment unloading bay and a retail replenishment dock. Test entries shall be visibly marked as samples and shall not be issued to a carrier as live delivery instructions.

5.3 Where a location uses labels such as “Door 3” or “West Receiving,” the Customer shall use those labels consistently in its worksheet and appointment instructions. Informal directions such as “the usual gate” shall be replaced with the applicable site and receiving-area designation.

## 6. User Administration

6.1 The Customer shall maintain separate user rosters for Marrow Fieldworks and Marrow Agrisupply. Each roster shall identify the user’s business email address, subscribing entity, and operational function, including receiving clerk, dispatcher, or site administrator.

6.2 Shared shift responsibilities shall be described in the roster without substituting a generic shift label for an individual user’s identity. Departure and reassignment notices shall identify the affected user and entity so that administrative changes can be applied to the correct subscription record.

6.3 Before submitting a roster revision, the Customer shall check for duplicate entries caused by shortened names, alternate email addresses, or transfers between equipment and farm supply operations.

## 7. Appointment Information

7.1 The Customer shall supply operational information appropriate to yard and dock scheduling, including appointment reference, carrier name, scheduled arrival, destination site, and receiving location.

7.2 Appointment notes shall distinguish between scheduling instructions and descriptions of goods. For example, an instruction to report to the west gate shall be entered as an arrival instruction rather than embedded in a description of replacement tractor components.

7.3 The Customer shall review imported appointment data for obsolete door names, missing site references, and duplicate delivery references. A corrected import file shall identify the affected records rather than requiring receiving personnel to infer which appointment should remain active.

## 8. Support Administration

8.1 The support SLA tier applicable to both Marrow Fieldworks and Marrow Agrisupply is Tier 2.

8.2 A support submission shall identify the subscribing entity, affected site, relevant appointment reference, observed behavior, and steps preceding the issue. Where available, the submission shall include a screenshot showing the affected calendar or appointment view.

8.3 For a report that an appointment appears under the wrong receiving shift, the Customer shall include the intended shift and the displayed shift. For a report concerning a missing dock-door label, the Customer shall provide the label used in its deployment worksheet. These particulars support investigation and do not change the subscribed support tier.

## 9. Operational Review

9.1 The Customer’s receiving personnel shall review sample calendars before using them for live scheduling. Review shall cover site selection, door naming, shift boundaries, and the clarity of carrier-facing arrival instructions.

9.2 Review comments shall identify a specific screen, field, or appointment example. A comment such as “wrong dock” shall be supplemented with the intended receiving location.

9.3 The parties shall maintain deployment corrections separately from the subscription schedule. Correcting a door label or removing a duplicate sample appointment shall not amend the commercial entries in Section 3.

## 10. Executed Record

10.1 The signature block below records execution on 8 Apr 2025. The effective date remains 1 May 2025.

10.2 The customer signatory executes this Agreement for Marrow Fieldworks and its covered affiliate, Marrow Agrisupply.
