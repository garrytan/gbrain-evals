# Affiliate Amendment: Marrow Agrisupply

**Record date:** 2025-10-02  
**Drafted:** 2025-09-22  
**Executed:** 2025-10-02  
**Effective date:** 2025-10-15

## 1. Parties and Purpose

This Affiliate Amendment is entered into by Pemberline Systems and Marrow Agrisupply in respect of Marrow Agrisupply’s use of Pemberline Dock, the yard and dock scheduling software supplied by Pemberline Systems.

Marrow Agrisupply is a farm supply retail chain operating in the Plains and a subsidiary of Marrow Fieldworks. The purpose of this Amendment is to revise the subscribed seat count and support SLA tier applicable to Marrow Agrisupply under the earlier agreement governing its subscription, referred to in this Amendment as the “Parent Agreement.”

This Amendment records the changes for Marrow Agrisupply only. Its identification of Marrow Fieldworks describes the affiliate relationship and does not amend the terms applicable to Marrow Fieldworks.

## 2. Preparation, Execution, and Effectiveness

This Amendment was drafted on 2025-09-22 and signed by both parties on 2025-10-02. The execution date and effective date are distinct.

The changes stated in Section 3 take effect on 2025-10-15. The signatures dated 2025-10-02 record execution of this Amendment and do not make the amended seat count or support SLA tier effective before 2025-10-15.

The record date shown above identifies the date on which this executed document was entered into the internal amendment record. It does not replace or alter the effective date.

## 3. Amended Terms for Marrow Agrisupply

Effective 2025-10-15, the following terms apply to Marrow Agrisupply:

1. **Subscribed seats:** 40.
2. **Support SLA tier:** Tier 1.

These are the only terms changed by this Amendment. The subscribed seat count and support SLA tier stated above replace the corresponding terms in the earlier paperwork for Marrow Agrisupply.

The designation “Tier 1” identifies the support SLA tier applicable under the Parent Agreement. This Amendment does not separately restate the service provisions associated with that tier.

## 4. Continuing Application of the Parent Agreement

Only the listed terms change for Marrow Agrisupply, and every other term of the Parent Agreement continues to apply to Marrow Agrisupply.

Except for the subscribed seat count and support SLA tier expressly amended in Section 3, this Amendment makes no change to the Parent Agreement. The earlier paperwork remains applicable to Marrow Agrisupply subject to those two substitutions from the effective date.

The amendment is limited to the named company. References to an affiliate relationship, an internal customer code, or an operational location do not extend these changes to another company.

## 5. Administrative Identification and Implementation Record

For internal identification, Marrow Agrisupply may appear in implementation materials as “MAS” or “Marrow Agri.” Those labels refer to Marrow Agrisupply; they do not identify an additional party.

The implementation worksheet accompanying the record concerns ordinary account administration: matching the customer name on the subscription entry, checking the displayed seat count, and checking the support tier field against Section 3. The worksheet includes a receiving-office checklist for the early shift, with separate entries for the dock scheduling desk and the yard check-in terminal.

The receiving-office notes also identify a routine user-list cleanup: removing duplicate display entries for the shared scheduling workstation and checking that the door assignment report retains its existing column headings. Those operational notes do not create additional amended terms. A workstation label, shift designation, or report heading is not a separate subscription commitment.

The completed worksheet is to be filed with this signed record so that the effective-date entry can be checked without treating the signature date as the activation date.
