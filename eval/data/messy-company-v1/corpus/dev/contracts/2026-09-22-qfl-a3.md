# Amendment No. 3 to the Quarrendon Master Subscription Agreement

**Drafted:** 2026-09-08  
**Executed:** 2026-09-22  
**Effective Date:** 2027-01-01

## 1. Parties and Purpose

This Amendment No. 3 (“Amendment”) is entered into by Pemberline Systems (“Pemberline”) and Quarrendon Freight Lines (“Customer”). This Amendment modifies the earlier master subscription agreement between the parties, together with the applicable subscription paperwork previously entered into under that agreement (collectively, the “Agreement”), solely as set forth below.

The subscription addressed by this Amendment is for Pemberline Dock, Pemberline’s yard and dock scheduling software.

## 2. Subscribed Seats

Effective 2027-01-01, Customer’s subscribed seat count for Pemberline Dock shall be **230 seats**.

This seat count supersedes any different subscribed seat count stated in the earlier Agreement or applicable subscription paperwork for the subscription addressed herein. The revised count shall govern from the Effective Date and shall not be construed as taking effect on the date of drafting or execution.

## 3. Execution and Effective Date

The parties execute this Amendment on 2026-09-22. Notwithstanding execution on that date, the change specified in Section 2 becomes effective only on 2027-01-01.

The drafting date of 2026-09-08 identifies preparation of this Amendment and does not establish an earlier effective date. Execution authorizes the parties to prepare the corresponding subscription administration update before the Effective Date without accelerating the seat-count change.

## 4. Administrative Implementation

Pemberline shall record the revised subscribed seat count against Quarrendon Freight Lines in its subscription administration records for implementation on the Effective Date.

Customer’s operational preparation may include reviewing named-user access for yard coordinators, dock schedulers, and shift supervisors, including the morning dispatch desk and evening receiving desk. Such preparation does not itself change the subscribed seat count before 2027-01-01. User access reviews and scheduling assignments remain operational activities and do not amend this instrument.

## 5. Continuing Effect of Agreement

Except for the subscribed seat count expressly amended herein, the Agreement remains unchanged and continues in effect according to its terms.

If a provision of this Amendment conflicts with earlier subscription paperwork concerning the subscribed seat count addressed herein, this Amendment controls from the Effective Date. No other provision is modified by this Amendment.
