# Renewal Order Form 2026: Quarrendon Freight Lines

## 1. Parties and Preparation

This Renewal Order Form is entered into by Pemberline Systems and Quarrendon Freight Lines for renewal of Pemberline Dock, the yard and dock scheduling software. This instrument was drafted on 2026-03-02 and executed on 2026-03-20.

## 2. Effective Date

This Renewal Order Form takes effect on 2026-04-01. The execution date is distinct from the effective date and does not advance commencement of the renewal.

## 3. Commercial Terms

The price per seat per year is USD 1,920. A discount of 10% applies to that price. The resulting discounted price per seat per year is USD 1,728. The end of the current term (renewal date) is 2027-03-31.

## 4. Amendment of Earlier Agreement

This Renewal Order Form amends the earlier agreement between the parties solely with respect to the terms expressly set out herein. In the event of inconsistency concerning those terms, this Renewal Order Form controls. All other provisions of the earlier agreement remain unchanged.
