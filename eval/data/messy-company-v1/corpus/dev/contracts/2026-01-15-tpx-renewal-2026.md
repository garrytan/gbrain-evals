# Renewal Order Form: Tansley Parcel 2026

**Document Status:** Executed  
**Drafted:** Monday, Jan 5th, 2026  
**Execution Date:** Thursday, Jan 15th, 2026  
**Effective Date:** Sunday, Feb 1st, 2026  

This Renewal Order Form: Tansley Parcel 2026 (this "Renewal Order Form") was originally drafted on Monday, Jan 5th, 2026, and is executed on Thursday, Jan 15th, 2026 (the "Execution Date"), by and between Pemberline Systems ("Provider") and Tansley Parcel ("Customer"), each a "Party" and collectively the "Parties." 

Notwithstanding the Execution Date of Thursday, Jan 15th, 2026, the commercial and operational terms set forth in this Renewal Order Form shall not commence until Sunday, Feb 1st, 2026 (the "Effective Date"). The Execution Date and the Effective Date are distinct; between the Execution Date and the Effective Date, the Parties' obligations remain governed by the immediately preceding ordering terms under the underlying agreement.

---

### 1. Reference to Governing Agreement and Precedence

**1.1.** This Renewal Order Form is entered into pursuant to, and is governed by, the existing master subscription agreement and associated ordering paperwork previously entered into by and between Provider and Customer (collectively, the "Earlier Agreement"). 

**1.2.** This Renewal Order Form modifies and updates the commercial schedules of the Earlier Agreement solely with respect to the renewal period specified herein. Except as expressly modified by the provisions of this Renewal Order Form, all terms, conditions, exhibits, and prior amendments of the Earlier Agreement shall remain in full force and effect and shall continue to govern the relationship between the Parties. In the event of any direct conflict between the terms of the Earlier Agreement and the terms of this Renewal Order Form, the terms of this Renewal Order Form shall control exclusively for the Renewal Term defined in Section 2.

### 2. Renewal Term

**2.1.** The renewal subscription term governed by this Renewal Order Form shall commence at 12:00 a.m. Central Standard Time on the Effective Date of Sunday, Feb 1st, 2026, and shall continue through 11:59 p.m. Central Standard Time on Sunday, Jan 31st, 2027 (the "Renewal Term"). 

**2.2.** For the avoidance of doubt, Sunday, Jan 31st, 2027 constitutes the end of the current term and the scheduled renewal date for the subscription services hereunder.

### 3. Subscription Scope and Operational Allocation

**3.1.** **Product:** Pemberline Dock (yard and dock scheduling software platform).

**3.2.** **Subscribed Seats:** 120 seats.

**3.3.** **Operational Deployment:** During the Renewal Term, Customer is authorized to provision up to the total Subscribed Seats across its South Central regional parcel sortation and cross-dock facilities, including gatehouse dispatchers at the Tulsa West hub (East Apron, Doors 1 through 28), night-shift yard spotters at the Little Rock cross-dock (Bays 14 through 42), and linehaul scheduling clerks handling the Shreveport outbound feeder lanes. 

**3.4.** **Administrative Provisioning:** Provider shall maintain the existing single sign-on configuration and automated export schedule for the `TPCL_Daily_TurnTime_v3` and `Outbound_Trailer_Dwell_SouthCentral` reports as configured under ticket #PD-44918, without interruption upon transition to the Effective Date on Feb 1st, 2026.

### 4. Pricing, Carryover Discount, and Billing Mechanics

**4.1.** **Base Subscription Rate:** The gross price per seat per year for Pemberline Dock during the Renewal Term is $1,850 USD.

**4.2.** **Carryover of Prior Discount:** No new discount schedule is restated in this Renewal Order Form; the 14% promotional and volume discount established in Amendment 1 to the Earlier Agreement carries forward in full and shall be applied by Provider against the gross price per seat per year set forth in Section 4.1 for all 120 Subscribed Seats during the Renewal Term.

**4.3.** **Invoicing and Administration:** Invoicing for the Renewal Term shall be processed through Provider's designated billing platform, Stoneharrow Billing, referencing Customer identifier `TPCL`. All commercial notices, renewal statements, and billing inquiries directed to Customer under this Renewal Order Form shall be routed to Lev Arkadin, Procurement Lead, at Customer's South Central regional headquarters, with copies to Customer's accounts payable registry on file.

### 5. Miscellaneous and Counterparts

**5.1.** **Entirety of Order:** This Renewal Order Form, together with the Earlier Agreement and Amendment 1 thereto, constitutes the complete and exclusive statement of the agreement between the Parties regarding the subject matter hereof for the period beginning Feb 1st, 2026 and ending Jan 31st, 2027.

**5.2.** **Counterparts:** This Renewal Order Form may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall constitute one and the same legal instrument. Electronic signatures affixed on Thursday, Jan 15th, 2026 shall have the same legal validity and enforceability as original ink signatures.
