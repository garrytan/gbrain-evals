# Renewal Order Form: Brackenwater Freight Co. (2025-2026 term)

## 1. Parties and Record Dates

This Renewal Order Form is entered into by Pemberline Systems (“Pemberline”) and Brackenwater Freight Co. (“Customer”) for the renewal of Customer’s subscription to Pemberline Dock, Pemberline’s yard and dock scheduling software.

This Order Form was drafted on 2025-11-10 and executed by both parties on 2025-11-21. Its effective date is 2025-12-01. The execution date records the parties’ signatures; it does not replace or advance the effective date.

## 2. Purpose and Relationship to Earlier Agreement

This Order Form changes the earlier agreement between Pemberline and Customer solely with respect to the subscription quantities, pricing, discount, and term dates expressly stated below. It records the commercial terms applicable to the 2025-2026 renewal term.

References in this Order Form to the earlier agreement mean the existing agreement governing Customer’s subscription to Pemberline Dock. Except as expressly changed by this Order Form, that agreement remains unchanged. This Order Form is to be read together with the earlier agreement, rather than as a replacement for the complete contractual relationship between the parties.

## 3. Subscription and Seat Quantity

The subscribed product is Pemberline Dock. The subscribed seat quantity for the renewal term is **64 seats**.

The quantity stated in this section is the quantity established by this Order Form. Administrative records used to arrange access, maintain user lists, or coordinate renewal processing shall reflect that quantity. Operational correspondence concerning individual users does not itself amend the subscribed seat quantity stated here.

Customer’s use of the subscribed product remains governed by the earlier agreement, except to the extent expressly modified in this Order Form.

## 4. Annual Seat Price and Discount

The price per seat per year is **USD 1,760**. The applicable discount is **8%**.

For the 64 subscribed seats, the annual amount before discount is USD 112,640. The 8% discount equals USD 9,011.20, resulting in a discounted annual subscription amount of **USD 103,628.80**.

These calculations express the seat quantity, annual seat price, and discount established by this Order Form. The discount does not alter the subscribed seat quantity. Any corresponding billing record shall identify the renewal subscription consistently with these figures and with the term dates stated below.

## 5. Renewal Term

The renewal term begins on **2025-12-01**. The end of the current term, identified for this Order Form as the renewal date, is **2026-11-30**.

The parties distinguish these subscription dates from the drafting and execution dates. Preparation on 2025-11-10 and signature on 2025-11-21 do not create an earlier subscription commencement date under this Order Form.

## 6. Effect of Changes

Where earlier paperwork for Customer states a different subscribed seat quantity, annual seat price, discount, or term date for this renewal, the corresponding provision of this Order Form controls.

No other change is made by this Order Form. Provisions of the earlier agreement concerning matters not expressly addressed here continue to apply. Renewal processing and related internal record updates shall be carried out on that basis.

## 7. Administrative Identification

The customer name for this renewal is Brackenwater Freight Co. References to this renewal in subscription administration and billing correspondence should identify both Customer and the 2025-2026 term so that the executed order is associated with the appropriate renewal record.

The signed version of this Order Form records the parties’ acceptance of the terms set out above.

## 8. Execution

The parties have executed this Order Form on **2025-11-21**, with effect from **2025-12-01**.
