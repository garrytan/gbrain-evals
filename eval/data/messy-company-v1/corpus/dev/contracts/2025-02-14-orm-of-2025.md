# Order Form: Orrin Mills

**Document Reference:** ORM-OF-2025-01  
**Date Drafted:** 2025-02-03  
**Date Executed:** 2025-02-14  
**Effective Date:** 2025-03-01  

### 1. Parties

This Order Form ("Order Form") is entered into by and between **Pemberline Systems** ("Provider"), and **Orrin Mills** ("Customer"), located at 4100 Spindle Creek Road, Spartanburg, SC 29301. While this Order Form is executed by the Parties on 2025-02-14 (the "Execution Date"), the rights, obligations, and subscription access granted hereunder shall commence on 2025-03-01 (the "Effective Date").

### 2. Subscription Services and Commercial Terms

Subject to the terms of this Order Form, Provider shall provision access to the **Pemberline Dock** yard and dock scheduling software platform for Customer’s operations, including the raw bale receiving gates and finished bolt shipping doors at the Spartanburg Complex. The commercial metrics governing this subscription are as follows:

* **Product:** Pemberline Dock (Standard Edition)
* **Subscribed Seats:** 36
* **Price per Seat per Year (USD):** $1,650.00
* **Applicable Discount (%):** 0%
* **Total Annual Subscription Fee (USD):** $59,400.00

### 3. Subscription Term

The initial term of this Order Form shall commence on the Effective Date of 2025-03-01 and shall continue in full force and effect through 2026-02-28 (the "End of Current Term" and "Renewal Date"), distinct from the Execution Date of 2025-02-14.

### 4. Billing and Invoicing

All fees are denominated in United States Dollars (USD). Invoicing for the Total Annual Subscription Fee shall be issued upon the Effective Date via Provider's designated billing administrator, Stoneharrow Billing, and sent to Customer’s accounts payable department at `ap-textiles@orrinmills.com`.
