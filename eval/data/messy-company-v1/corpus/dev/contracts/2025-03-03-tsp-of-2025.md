# ORDER FORM: TANSLEIGH PACKAGING

**Document Reference:** OF-TSP-2025-0014  
**Draft Date:** 2/20/25  
**Execution Date:** 3/3/25  
**Effective Date:** 3/15/25  

This Order Form ("Order Form") is entered into by and between **Pemberline Systems** ("Pemberline"), a Delaware corporation, and **Tansleigh Packaging** ("Customer"), an Ohio corporation with principal operations in the Ohio Valley region (collectively, the "Parties"). This Order Form was originally drafted on 2/20/25, is executed by both Parties on the Execution Date of 3/3/25, and shall become legally binding as of the Execution Date, with the subscription period commencing on the Effective Date of 3/15/25 as set forth in Section 3 below.

---

### 1. INCORPORATION AND DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Governing Agreement.** This Order Form is subject to and incorporates by reference the Master Subscription Terms and Conditions of Pemberline Systems (the "Agreement"). In the event of any conflict or inconsistency between the provisions of the Agreement and the provisions of this Order Form, the terms of this Order Form shall control solely with respect to the subscription services procured hereunder.

**1.2 Product Description.** "Pemberline Dock" means Pemberline’s proprietary cloud-based yard and dock scheduling software application, including standard modules for gate check-in, dock door assignment, carrier appointment scheduling, and trailer yard tracking.

**1.3 Seat Definition.** A "Subscribed Seat" means a unique user credential assigned to one (1) named individual employee, contractor, or authorized guardhouse operator of Customer to access and administer Pemberline Dock. External motor carrier drivers and third-party freight dispatchers entering appointment requests via the self-service carrier portal do not consume a Subscribed Seat.

---

### 2. SUBSCRIPTION SCHEDULE AND COMMERCIAL TERMS

**2.1 Subscription Configuration.** Subject to the terms of the Agreement and timely payment of applicable fees, Pemberline grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to access and use Pemberline Dock during the Subscription Term in accordance with the following commercial schedule:

* **Subscribed Product:** Pemberline Dock (Enterprise Yard & Dock Scheduling Platform)
* **Customer Account Code:** TSP / TP
* **Subscribed Seats:** 30
* **Price per Seat per Year (USD):** $1,750.00
* **Discount (%):** 0%
* **Net Price per Seat per Year (USD):** $1,750.00
* **Total Annual Subscription Fee (USD):** $52,500.00

**2.2 Seat Allocation and Operational Scope.** Customer’s thirty (30) Subscribed Seats may be allocated across Customer’s corrugated packaging manufacturing plants and converting facilities located in the Ohio Valley, including without limitation the Portsmouth Corrugated Box Plant (East and West Receiving Bays, Dock Doors 1 through 18), the Marietta Sheet Feeder Facility (Shipping Bays A through H), and the Chillicothe Warehouse Annex. Customer may reassign Subscribed Seats among individual shift supervisors, shipping clerks, and scalehouse operators only upon the permanent termination or role transfer of a named user, and shall not permit concurrent login or credential sharing across first, second, and third shifts.

---

### 3. TERM Dates

**3.1 Execution Date vs. Effective Date.** For the avoidance of doubt, the date on which this Order Form is signed by both Parties is 3/3/25 (the "Execution Date"), whereas the date on which the subscription services and fee obligations commence is 3/15/25 (the "Effective Date"). Between 3/3/25 and 3/15/25, Pemberline shall provision Customer’s production tenant (`tansleigh-prod.pemberlinedock.io`) and import Customer’s baseline carrier master file and dock door profile data at no additional charge.

**3.2 Current Subscription Term.** The initial term of this Order Form shall commence at 12:00 a.m. Eastern Time on the Effective Date of 3/15/25 and shall continue in full force and effect through 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on 3/14/26 (the "End of the Current Term" or "Renewal Date"), unless earlier terminated in accordance with the Agreement.

### 5.OPERATIONAL PROVISIONING AND DATA INTERFACES

**5.1 Standard Configuration.** Customer’s instance of Pemberline Dock shall be configured with standard fifteen-minute (15-minute) dock appointment slot increments, automated detention tracking alerts for drop-trailer pools exceeding forty-eight (48) hours on the North Lot, and PDF bill-of-lading attachment support at the West Gate kiosk.

**5.2 Entire Order.** This Order Form constitutes the complete and exclusive statement of the commercial quantities, pricing, and term dates agreed between the Parties as of 3/3/25 and supersedes all prior quotes, drafts (including the initial draft dated 2/20/25), and oral negotiations relating to the subject matter hereof.
