# Order Form: Tamsin Cold Chain

**Document Reference:** OF-TCC-2025-0512  
**Date Drafted:** 2025-05-12  
**Date Signed:** 2025-05-30  
**Effective Date:** 2025-06-01  

This Order Form ("Order Form") is entered into by and between Pemberline Systems ("Provider") and Tamsin Cold Chain ("Customer"), referred to collectively as the "Parties." Although drafted on 2025-05-12 and executed by both Parties on 2025-05-30, the rights, obligations, and subscription access granted hereunder shall commence solely on the Effective Date of 2025-06-01.

### 1. Definitions and Scope of Use
1.1 **Service.** Provider grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to access and use the Pemberline Dock yard and dock scheduling software platform ("Service") during the Subscription Term, solely for Customer's internal refrigerated warehousing and cold-storage yard operations across its Pacific Northwest facilities, including the Auburn, Tacoma, and Pasco distribution centers.  
1.2 **Permitted Operational Use.** A "Subscribed Seat" authorizes one named user—including gate house clerks, cold-dock coordinators, blast-freezer staging supervisors, and yard hostler dispatchers—to log into the Service to schedule carrier appointments, monitor reefer pre-cool status at doors C-01 through C-36, generate the Daily Reefer Dwell & Shore Power Report, and manage trailer movements across designated yard slips.

### 2. Subscription Term
2.1 **Term Dates.** The subscription term governed by this Order Form shall commence on the Effective Date of 2025-06-01 and shall continue through 2026-05-31 ("End of Current Term" and "Renewal Date"), distinct from the execution date of 2025-05-30.  
2.2 **Provisioning Window.** Provider shall complete tenant configuration and Single Sign-On (SSO) directory synchronization prior to the 04:00 PDT morning receiving shift on 2025-06-01, ensuring no interruption to carrier check-in at the Auburn North Gate.

### 3. Commercial Schedule and Pricing
3.1 **Subscription Fees.** The commercial terms for the Subscription Term are established as follows:

* **Product:** Pemberline Dock (Enterprise Yard & Cold-Storage Scheduling)
* **Subscribed Seats:** 48
* **List Price per Seat per Year:** $1,980.00 USD
* **Gross Annual Subscription Fee:** $95,040.00 USD
* **Applicable Discount:** 5% ($4,752.00 USD)
* **Net Price per Seat per Year:** $1,881.00 USD
* **Total Net Annual Subscription Fee:** $90,288.00 USD

3.2 **Invoicing and Payment.** Provider shall issue the annual invoice for the Total Net Annual Subscription Fee upon the Effective Date (2025-06-01) via Provider's billing agent, Stoneharrow Billing. Customer shall remit full payment in United States Dollars via ACH or wire transfer to the account designated on the invoice. All fees are exclusive of applicable Washington and Oregon state or local sales and use taxes.

### 4. Support Services and Service Level Agreement
4.1 **Support SLA Tier.** Customer is enrolled in **Tier 1** Support for the duration of the Subscription Term.  
4.2 **Incident Handling.** Under Tier 1 Support, Customer's designated system administrators may submit support tickets via the Pemberline customer portal or by email for issues affecting dock appointment locks, carrier EDI 214 status updates, or gate kiosk barcode scanners (including pre-go-live configuration ticket #PD-88412 regarding temperature-threshold alerts on Tacoma Dock B). Provider shall respond to and remediate incidents in accordance with the response targets defined in the standard Tier 1 Support Schedule.

### 5. Entire Agreement and Execution
5.1 **Precedence.** This Order Form, drafted on 2025-05-12, sets forth the complete commercial agreement between Pemberline Systems and Tamsin Cold Chain for the period from 2025-06-01 through 2026-05-31. In the event of any conflict between this Order Form and any generalized terms of service, the terms of this Order Form shall govern.

**IN WITNESS WHEREOF**, the Parties have caused this Order Form to be executed by their duly authorized representatives on 2025-05-30, to take effect on the Effective Date of 2025-06-01.
