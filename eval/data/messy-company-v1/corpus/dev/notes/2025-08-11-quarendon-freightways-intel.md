# Competitive intel: Quarendon Freightways

Aug 11th — quick note from this morning’s dispatcher chat, before I forget which spelling goes where.

Quarendon Freightways is the Duluth-area trucking company, **not a Pemberline customer**. They’re running a competitor’s yard software. Quarendon’s dispatcher, Ellis, used to work at Quarrendon Freight Lines; that’s how the conversation wandered into appointment boards and the joys of drivers arriving before anyone has coffee.

Main gripe: their overnight dispatcher keeps a paper list beside the screen because trailer moves don’t show up quickly enough. On Friday, a driver waited at door 6 while the office still had his trailer marked in the staging lane. Ellis said the radio sorted it out, but “then you get to fix the computer after the truck’s gone.” Sounds annoyng.

Quarendon also exports the morning queue into a spreadsheet for the gate clerk. I’ll ask Ellis whether that’s a workaround or just habit next time we talk. Worth a closer look, especially the overnight handoff.
