# On-call handbook

The platform rotation is one engineer per week. Handoff happens every Monday at 10:00 Pacific in the platform channel, with a written summary of open alerts.

Pages escalate to the secondary after 15 minutes and to the head of platform after 30. Acknowledge every page, even a false alarm, and record false alarms in the weekly review so the alert can be tuned.
