# Threshold Robotics Series B Planning Call

[Amara Okafor](user/amara-okafor) and [Diego Alvarez](people/diego-alvarez) connected to discuss the upcoming [Threshold Robotics](companies/threshold-robotics) Series B raise and Halfway Capital's positioning.

## Discussion Summary

Diego opened by sharing that the Threshold team has been making solid progress on their warehouse automation pilot with the logistics partner in Phoenix. Unit economics are tracking ahead of projections from Q4.

Amara asked about timeline for the Series B. Diego confirmed they're planning to kick off the process in late Q3, likely September. The founding team wants to close before year-end to maintain momentum heading into 2027.

Regarding round size, Diego mentioned they're targeting $18M for the Series B. He noted this gives them 24 months of runway at projected burn, with room to expand the engineering team and launch two additional pilot sites.

Amara flagged that [Marcus Reid](people/marcus-reid) had previously expressed interest in co-investing if Halfway leads. Diego said he'd welcome that—Marcus has strong logistics connections that could help with enterprise sales.

They briefly discussed valuation expectations. Diego indicated the founders are hoping for something in the $70-80M pre-money range based on comparable deals, but acknowledged the market has cooled since last summer.

## Decisions

- Halfway will plan to participate in Series B diligence starting late August
- Diego to send updated metrics deck by end of April
