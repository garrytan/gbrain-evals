# Portfolio Review: Meridian Robotics Q1 Performance

[Amara Okafor](people/amara-okafor) and [Elena Rossi](people/elena-rossi) connected to discuss [Meridian Robotics](companies/meridian-robotics) Q1 2026 results and upcoming Series B considerations.

## Key Discussion Points

**Q1 Performance Overview**

[Elena](people/elena-rossi) reported that [Meridian](companies/meridian-robotics) closed Q1 with $2.1M ARR, up 34% from Q4 2025. Customer retention remains strong at 94%, though the sales cycle has lengthened due to enterprise procurement slowdowns. The warehouse automation vertical is performing above expectations, while manufacturing has been slower to convert.

[Amara](people/amara-okafor) asked about the CAC payback period, which [Elena](people/elena-rossi) confirmed has improved to 14 months from 18 months last quarter. She attributed this to the new channel partnership with [Thornfield Industrial](companies/thornfield-industrial).

**Series B Timing**

[Amara](people/amara-okafor) raised the question of Series B timing. Current runway extends to Q1 2027, but both agreed that starting conversations in Q3 2026 would be prudent given market conditions. [Elena](people/elena-rossi) mentioned she's had inbound interest from [Northvane Partners](companies/northvane-partners) and one other growth fund she declined to name.

[Amara](people/amara-okafor) offered to make introductions to [Marcus Reid](people/marcus-reid) at [Arcline Ventures](companies/arcline-ventures), who has been actively looking at industrial automation.

**Team & Hiring**

Engineering headcount is at 23, with plans to hire 4 more by end of Q2. [Elena](people/elena-rossi) flagged that the VP Engineering search is taking longer than expected and asked if [Amara](people/amara-okafor) had candidates in her network.
