# Portfolio Review: CarbonLoop Q1 Performance

[Priya Patel](people/priya-patel) walked through the quarterly numbers for [CarbonLoop](companies/carbonloop) and overall the trajectory looks solid.

## Key Metrics Discussion

Revenue came in at $2.1M for Q1, which is ahead of the $1.8M they projected in January. [Priya](people/priya-patel) noted the enterprise pipeline is stronger than expected, with three Fortune 500 pilots converting to paid contracts.

On the unit economics side, she confirmed the burn multiple is sitting at 1.8 right now, which is honestly better than I expected given they're still in growth mode. That's down from where they were six months ago, so the efficiency improvements [Marcus Reid](people/marcus-reid) pushed for are clearly showing up in the numbers.

## Concerns Flagged

[Priya](people/priya-patel) raised some concerns about their engineering hiring pace. They've only filled 2 of the 5 senior roles they opened in Q4. Competitive market for climate tech engineers apparently. She's going to connect them with a few recruiting firms that have worked well for other portfolio companies.

Also discussed the regulatory landscape briefly—the new EPA guidelines coming in July could actually be a tailwind for them if they position correctly.

## Decisions

- Approved the bridge extension terms we discussed last month
- Green-lit the Series B prep timeline for Q3
- Agreed to hold off on board observer seat until after next funding round
