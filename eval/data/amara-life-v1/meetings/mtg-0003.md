# Deep Dive: Solaris Grid Storage Series A

[Amara Okafor](user/amara-okafor) and [Marcus Reid](people/marcus-reid) discussed the Solaris Grid Storage opportunity. Marcus came prepared with updated materials following initial screening.

## Company Overview

Marcus walked through Solaris's positioning in the long-duration energy storage market. Team has deep experience from Tesla Energy and Fluence. Currently deploying iron-air battery systems with 100+ hour discharge capability.

## Amara's Diligence Questions

Amara asked Marcus to walk through the unit economics stack in detail — wanted to understand $/kWh installed cost breakdown across cells, BOS, and installation labor. Marcus noted they're targeting $25/kWh at scale vs. current $42/kWh.

Amara followed up on LCOE trajectory — specifically how they're modeling degradation curves over the 25-year asset life and what assumptions drive the $0.05/kWh levelized figure. Marcus to get more granular data from the team.

Amara pressed on offtake structure: "What's the contract mix between utility PPAs, merchant exposure, and behind-the-meter? I want to understand revenue certainty in year one versus year five." Marcus confirmed 60% contracted via 15-year PPAs with two California IOUs.

Further question on capacity factors — Amara wanted to know realized vs. nameplate across their three pilot installations and how seasonal variation affects revenue recognition. Marcus acknowledged the Arizona site underperformed at 78% vs. 85% modeled.

Finally, Amara asked about interconnection queue position and timeline risk for the 2027 pipeline.

## Decisions

- Solaris moves to full diligence; Amara will lead
- Marcus to schedule technical deep-dive with CTO for next week
