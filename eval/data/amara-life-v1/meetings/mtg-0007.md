# Portfolio Review: Codex Robotics Q1 Performance

[Amara Okafor](user/amara-okafor) and [Elena Rossi](people/elena-rossi) connected to review Q1 2026 performance for [Codex Robotics](companies/codex-robotics), one of Halfway Capital's Series A investments from late 2024.

**Key Metrics Discussion**

Elena walked through the numbers. ARR grew 34% quarter-over-quarter, now sitting at $2.8M. Net revenue retention came in at 118%, which Amara noted was strong for their stage. Customer count increased from 23 to 31 enterprise accounts, with the manufacturing vertical showing particular traction.

Burn rate has increased but remains within acceptable bounds—$420K/month versus the $380K projected. Elena explained the overage was due to accelerating the robotics engineering hire plan by one quarter.

**Concerns Raised**

Amara flagged that the sales cycle has lengthened from an average of 68 days to 89 days. Elena acknowledged this and attributed it to moving upmarket into larger enterprise deals. They agreed this needs monitoring but isn't necessarily negative if deal sizes scale proportionally.

Discussion around the competitive landscape—[Elena Rossi](people/elena-rossi) mentioned that a well-funded competitor out of Munich has started targeting the same manufacturing segment. Codex's technical differentiation on adaptive learning remains strong, but go-to-market execution will matter more in the next two quarters.

**Decisions**

- Halfway Capital will participate in the upcoming bridge round, maintaining pro-rata
- Board seat observation rights to continue with Amara attending Q2 board meeting in person (scheduled May 15th)
